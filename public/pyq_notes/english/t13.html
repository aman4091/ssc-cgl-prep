What is voice?

Active voice: the doer comes first. Riya wrote a letter. Passive voice: the thing that receives the action comes first. A letter was written by Riya. Meaning same, focus alag.

The helping-verb chart

Trick: "Continuous ka being, Perfect ka been, Modal ka be." Then add V3 (third form) of the main verb.

Rule 1 · Present simple → is / am / are + V3 PYQ ×8

Rule: writes / write becomes is / am / are + V3. Choose is or are by the NEW subject (the old object).

✗ The shopkeeper sells toys. → Toys is sold by the shopkeeper. → ✓ Toys are sold by the shopkeeper.

Rule 10 · Only transitive verbs have a passive CGL+

Rule: a verb needs an object to become passive. Die, sleep, go, arrive, happen, seem have no object, so they have no passive.
