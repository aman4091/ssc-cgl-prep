What is voice?

Active voice: the doer comes first. Ravi wrote the letter. Passive voice: the thing that receives the action comes first, and the doer moves to the end with "by". The letter was written by Ravi.

A passive verb always has two parts: a form of be (is, am, are, was, were, be, been, being) + V3 (the past participle: written, seen, made). The form of "be" shows the tense. The V3 never changes.

Trick: "Kaam karne wala pehle = active. Jis par kaam hua woh pehle = passive." Only a verb with an object can go into the passive: no object, no passive.

Rule 1 · Spot the passive: be + V3 Q

Rule: a sentence is passive when its verb is a form of "be" + V3 and its subject receives the action. "Be + -ing" is still active.

✗ Children are participating in the contest. (passive?) → ✓ No, this is active (be + -ing). The consignment was delivered is passive (be + V3).

Rule 11 · No passive: perfect continuous, future continuous, intransitive and linking verbs Q ×10

Rule: has / had / will have been + V-ing and will be + V-ing have no passive in standard grammar. Verbs without an object (stay, go, sleep) and linking verbs (taste, seem, look, become) have no passive either.

Rule: after make, bid, see, hear the active uses a bare verb (made her feel); the passive needs "to" (was made to feel). Going back, drop the "to". "Let" is usually changed to "allowed to".

✗ The staff had been made report discrepancies. → ✓ The staff had been made to report discrepancies.
