TrapWhat it looks likeExample from the dump
Extreme wordalways, only, never, completely, all, entirelyP8 future of energy: "It will rely only on fossil fuels" ✗ → balance renewable and traditional sources
Too narrowOne detail offered as the main ideaP1: "The benefits of fast internet" ✗ → The challenges and consequences of the digital divide
Too broadBigger than the passageP1: "The rise of new technologies" ✗ (the passage is about the gap, not all technology)

OppositeUses passage words but reverses themP19: confirmation bias "makes people more open-minded" ✗ → seek information that supports their beliefs

Trick: "Jo option zyada bolta hai, woh aksar galat hota hai." The right RC answer is usually mild (may, can, often, both…and). Strong words like always / only / never are usually the trap.

After a turn word (however, yet, but) and in the last line.

What a cloze is A short passage with 5 numbered blanks. Each blank has 4 options. Some blanks test grammar (which form of a word fits), and some test meaning (which word makes sense). In Steno 2026, many sets also asked the meaning of a word from the cloze passage.

Trick: "Blank ke dono taraf dekho." Look one word left and one word right of the blank. "an ____" needs a vowel sound (an imbalance); "____ away from" needs a moving verb (divert).

Check yourself

Options: Vary / Varies / Varying / Varied. Grammar clue or meaning clue?

Grammar clue: the same word in four forms. Match the subject; Steno keyed Varies (singular subject).

"…a simulation everywhere, but ____ where it is needed most." effective / disruptive / absent / omnipresent?

"but" is a U-turn after "everywhere", so the opposite: absent.

Which preposition follows "filled"?

Filled with.

Main Idea, Central Theme and Purpose

Asked in almost every set: "main idea", "central theme", "primary purpose", "main concern", "author's message"

Worked examples: find the clue

Example 1 · King Lear (Steno 12 Sep S2 · 190) Clue: the last line says the story warns how ego can shatter a family. Options about British territories or royal lifestyles are outside the passage. Answer: The destructive impact of ego and false trust on family.
Example 2 · Algorithmic living (B283) Clue: line 2 says algorithmic living "promises efficiency but risks eroding autonomy". The idea after "but" is the concern. Answer: Loss of autonomy due to filtered choices. "Faster online shopping" is a detail-sized trap.
Example 3 · Time management (Steno 10 Sep S2 · 161) One-word options: Technology, Stress, Productivity, Management. Technology, stress and productivity each appear in only part of the passage; every sentence is about managing time. Answer: Management.
Trick: "Pehli line + aakhri line = main idea." Join the first and last sentence in your head; the option closest to that join is the answer.

Detail Questions: "According to the Passage", NOT / EXCEPT, Complete the Line

The biggest group · answer is written in one line of the passage, usually in different words

Worked examples: find the clue

Example 1 · Madame Bovary (Steno 9 Sep S2 · 192) Keyword "turned to books". The passage says Emma took refuge in novels and was safer from an unkind world; it also says she was constrained by the patriarchal system. Answer: To seek refuge from an unkind world and patriarchal limitations. "Career as a scholar" is never mentioned.
Example 2 · Community conservation (B226, NOT question) The obstacle list in P45: low funding, human–wildlife conflict, unclear land-use rights. Tick these three. Answer: Climate change (not in the list).
Example 3 · Complete the line (Steno 12 Sep S1 · 170) "Addressing climate change requires international cooperation, renewable energy and ____." The list is all solutions, so the blank must be a solution: reforestation. Deforestation, mining and hunting are causes of harm.
Trick: "Same idea, naye shabd." The right option rarely copies the passage word for word. An option that copies words but changes who did what is the trap.
⚠ Answer-key note (B281, Dandi March)The passage says the marchers walked 240 kilometres, so the key 240 km follows the passage. The real distance from Sabarmati Ashram to Dandi was about 240 miles (about 385 km), 12 March to 6 April 1930. In an RC question, always answer from the passage; for a GK question, remember about 385 km / 24 days.

Check yourself

"insidious"?

Harmful in a slow, hidden way, like the "slow violence" of polluted air.

"credence"?

Belief, acceptance as true ("lend credence to a theory").

"effaced"?

Wiped out, erased.

Antonym of "pellucid"?

Nebulous (unclear). Pellucid = very clear.

"didactic"?

Meant to teach a lesson or moral.
