Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: cheeni ke cube

Maan lo tumhare paas 1 cm ke chhote cheeni ke cube hain. Ek dabba hai: 4 cm lamba, 3 cm chauda, 2 cm ooncha.

  • Neeche ki ek tah (layer) mein kitne cube aayenge? 4 × 3 = 12. Ye tah ka area hai.
  • Aisi kitni tah? Oonchai 2 hai, toh 2 tah. Kul 12 × 2 = 24 cube. Yahi volume hai: 24 cm³.
  • Ab dabbe ko bahar se kaagaz mein lapetna hai. 6 deewarein hain: upar-neeche 4 × 3 = 12 (do baar), aage-peeche 4 × 2 = 8 (do baar), daayen-baayen 3 × 2 = 6 (do baar). Kul 2 × (12 + 8 + 6) = 52 cm². Yahi surface area hai.

Bas itni si baat hai. Volume = andar kitna samaata hai (cm³, litre). Surface area = bahar kitna kaagaz ya rang lagta hai (cm²). Pura chapter isi do sawaal ka hai.

Teen badi baatein jinse saare formula nikalte hain 1. Seedhe khade solids (cuboid, cylinder, prism): volume = neeche ka area × oonchai. Tah ke upar tah. Cuboid = lb × h, cylinder = πr² × h, prism = B × h.
2. Nukeele solids (cone, pyramid): usi base aur oonchai wale seedhe solid ka ek-tihaai (⅓). Cone = ⅓πr²h, pyramid = ⅓ × B × h.
3. Gol solids: sphere = (4/3)πr³, hemisphere = aadha = (2/3)πr³. Surface: sphere 4πr², hemisphere ka gol hissa 2πr², flat dhakkan πr² aur.
Surface ke liye solid ko khol kar (unfold) socho: cylinder ki deewar khulti hai toh ek rectangle banti hai, lambai 2πr aur chaudai h. Isliye CSA = 2πrh.

Speed method 1 · 3 : 1 : 2 ka parivaar (cylinder, cone, sphere)

Kab use karo: jab do solids ka "same radius, same height" ya "equal volume" diya ho.

Soch aise: ek cylinder lo jiski oonchai 2r hai, aur usme bilkul fit hone wala ek cone aur ek sphere. Volume dekho:

  • Cylinder = πr² × 2r = 2πr³
  • Sphere = (4/3)πr³
  • Cone = ⅓ × πr² × 2r = (2/3)πr³

Teeno ko (2/3)πr³ se baanto: cylinder : sphere : cone = 3 : 2 : 1. Aur hemisphere (2/3)πr³ hai, yaani hemisphere = cone jiski oonchai 2r ho.

Speed method 2 · Lambai k guna → area k² guna → volume k³ guna

Kab use karo: "radius 20% badha, volume kitna badha", "surface 44% badha toh radius?", "radius aadha toh volume?".

Soch aise: ek 1 cm ka cube lo. Har side 2 guna karo. Ab ek face mein 2 × 2 = 4 chhote squares hain (area 4 guna). Aur poore cube mein 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 chhote cube (volume 8 guna).

Jab r aur h alag-alag badlein: a + b + ab/100 do-teen baar · ~15 sec Cylinder V ∝ r² × h. Radius +10% → r² mein 10 + 10 + 1 = +21%. Phir height −20%: 21 − 20 − (21 × 20)/100 = 1 − 4.2 = −3.2%.
Cone mein bhi wahi, kyunki ⅓ toh dono taraf kat jaata hai.
Trick: "Sirf ek cheez badli?" Volume us cheez ki power ke hisaab se badhta hai. Prism ki height +50% → volume +50% (power 1). Radius +50% → cylinder ka volume × 2.25 (power 2). Sphere ka radius +50% → × 3.375 (power 3).

Speed method 3 · Pighlana = volume wahi rehta hai (π aur 4/3 pehle kaato)

Kab use karo: "melted and recast", "kitni goliyan banengi", "naye solid ki oonchai".

Soch aise: lohe ka gola pighla, toh loha na badha na ghata. Bas shakal badli. Toh purana volume = naya volume (sab milakar). Ab chaalaaki: dono taraf π hai, kaat do. Dono taraf 4/3 hai, kaat do. Sirf r³ bachta hai.

  • Sphere → chhote spheres: n = (R/r)³. 12 → 2: (6)³ = 216.
  • Sphere → hemispheres: ek sphere = do hemisphere. n = 2 × (R/r)³.
  • Kai spheres → ek sphere: R³ = r₁³ + r₂³ + r₃³. 3, 4, 5 → 27 + 64 + 125 = 216 → 6. Yaad rakho: 3³ + 4³ + 5³ = 6³.
  • Cone → chhote cones: n = (R/r)² × (H/h). Radius ¼ aur height ¼ → 16 × 4 = 64.
  • Total surface area: ek sphere ko n barabar spheres mein todo → kul surface ∛n guna. 8 tukde → 2 guna.
Demo · Sphere r = 6 pighla kar cylinder banaya, oonchai 8. Radius? · ~10 sec (4/3) × 6³ = r² × 8 (π kaata). (4/3) × 216 = 288. → r² = 288 ÷ 8 = 36 → r = 6.
Trick: cube numbers 1 se 15 tak yaad karo (1, 8, 27, 64, 125, 216, 343, 512, 729, 1000, 1331, 1728, 2197, 2744, 3375). 728 dikhe toh turant "9³ = 729 ke paas" → answer 9.

Speed method 4 · Paani ka uthna = solid ka volume ÷ bartan ka base

Kab use karo: "ball daala, paani kitna utha", "paani 3 cm utha, bartan ka radius?", "kitna paani bahar gira".

Demo · Cylinder R = 6 cm, usme sphere r = 3 cm dooba. Paani kitna utha? · ~8 sec Sphere = (4/3)π × 27 = 36π. Base = π × 36. → Δh = 36π ÷ 36π = 1 cm.

Speed method 5 · Triplet se slant height aur diagonal

Kab use karo: cone mein l, pyramid mein l ya h, cuboid ka diagonal. Har jagah ek right triangle chhupa hai.

  • Cone: r, h, l → l² = r² + h².
  • Square pyramid: h, (a/2), l → l² = h² + (a/2)². Dhyan: aadhi side, poori nahi.
  • Cuboid: d² = l² + b² + h².

Speed method 6 · Cone ko kaata: 1 : 8 : 27 ki seedhi

Kab use karo: "cone ko base ke parallel kaata", "frustum", "upar ka hissa 1/8 volume".

Soch aise: upar ka chhota cone bilkul bade cone jaisa hai, bas chhota. Agar uski height poori height ki ⅓ hai, toh uska radius bhi ⅓, aur volume (⅓)³ = 1/27. Yahi method 2 hai, bas cone par.

Dhyan do: "base se 10 cm ki oonchai par kaata" aur "vertex (chotti) se 10 cm" alag baatein hain. Hamesha chotti se naapo, kyunki chhota cone wahin hai.

Speed method 7 · Prism aur pyramid: B × h, perimeter × h

Kab use karo: koi bhi prism (triangle, trapezium, hexagon, pentagon base) ya pyramid.

  • Prism volume = base ka area × height. Base kuch bhi ho, bas uska area nikaalo.
  • Prism ki side deewarein (LSA) = base ka perimeter × height. Kyunki har deewar ek rectangle hai: side × h. Sab jodo toh (sab sides) × h.
  • Pyramid volume = ⅓ × base × h. Pyramid LSA = ½ × perimeter × slant height (har deewar ek triangle).
  • "Tah" (layers) wale sawaal (heights AP mein): kul height = tah ki ginti × (pehli + aakhri) ÷ 2. Phir × base area.
Demo · Prism, equilateral base side 6, height 10. Volume? · ~8 sec Base = (√3/4) × 36 = 9√3. Volume = 9√3 × 10 = 90√3 ≈ 155.9 cm³.
Demo · Tah wali heights 3, 5, 7, 9 cm, base 20 cm². Volume? · ~6 sec Kul height = 4 × (3 + 9) ÷ 2 = 24. Volume = 20 × 24 = 480 cm³.

Speed method 8 · Andar fit (inscribed): kaun kisko chhoota hai?

  • Sphere cube ke andar (sab faces chhoo raha): diameter = side → r = a/2.
  • Cube / cuboid sphere ke andar (kone sphere par): cube ka diagonal = sphere ka diameter → a√3 = 2R.
  • Cylinder sphere ke andar: h² + (2r)² = D².
  • Sabse badi ball dabbe mein: diameter = dabbe ki sabse chhoti side.
  • Sphere cone ke andar: cone ka beech se kata triangle lo. r = triangle ka area ÷ (perimeter ka aadha).
  • Hemisphere cube ke andar (flat face neeche): r = a/2.
Trick: sphere cube mein → sphere = cube ka π/6 ≈ 52.4%. Khaali jagah ≈ 47.6%. Sphere cylinder mein (h = 2r) → sphere = cylinder ka ⅔.
