Pehle ek chhota sa kamra: 4 m × 3 m

Maan lo ek kamre ka farsh 4 m lamba aur 3 m chauda hai. Isse teen sawaal poochhe ja sakte hain. Bas teen.

  • Andar kitni jagah hai? 1 m × 1 m ki tiles bichhao. Ek line mein 4, aur aisi 3 lines. Kul 12 tiles. Yahi area hai: 12 m². Area hamesha "kitne chhote square" hota hai.
  • Kinare-kinare kitna chaloge? 4 + 3 + 4 + 3 = 14 m. Yahi perimeter hai. Fencing, rassi, wire, frame, border: sab perimeter hai.
  • Ek kone se saamne wale kone tak? 3 aur 4 ke beech right angle hai, toh seedhi doori 5 m (3-4-5). Yahi diagonal hai.

Paper ke 420 sawaal inhi teen cheezon ke alag-alag kapde hain. Shape badalti hai, sawaal wahi rehta hai.

Teen core ideas (sab yahin se aata hai)

1 · Area = rectangle ka koi roop Rectangle l × b. Triangle uska aadha: ½ × b × h. Parallelogram b × h. Rhombus ½ × d₁ × d₂. Trapezium average × h. Circle bhi isi tarah: chhote-chhote sector kaat kar ulte-seedhe jodo, toh lagbhag rectangle banta hai jiski lambai πr aur chaudai r. Isliye area = πr × r = πr².
2 · Right angle dikha, toh Pythagoras (triplet) Rectangle ka diagonal, rhombus ki side, chord ki doori, isosceles ki height: sab ek hi right triangle hai. Numbers aksar triplet hote hain (3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25). Pehchaan lo toh square-root lagana hi nahi padta.
3 · Lambai k guna, toh area k² guna Side dugni → area 4 guna. Radius 3 : 5 → area 9 : 25. Radius 20% badha → area 44% (20 + 20 + 4). Perimeter aur circumference seedhe badhte hain, square nahi hote.

Formula sheet, figure ke saath (ek baar dhyaan se dekho)

Triangles. Har triangle mein area = ½ × base × us base par height. Baaki formulas bas height dhoondhne ke tareeke hain.

Chaar-bhuja wali shapes. Har ek ke neeche: area, aur diagonal ya side ka rishta.

Circle ka parivaar. Sector circle ka ek hissa hai. Segment = sector minus triangle. Ring = bada circle minus chhota.

Hexagon aur circle-square ki jodi. Regular hexagon asal mein 6 equilateral triangle hai. Aur circle ko square mein (ya square ko circle mein) fit karne ke do hi rule hain.

Formula / value sheet to memorise √2 = 1.414 · √3 = 1.732 · π = 22/7 ≈ 3.14 (3.1416) · √3/4 = 0.433 · 3√3/2 = 2.598 (hexagon) · 2(1 + √2) = 4.83 (octagon)
Regular octagon circle R mein: A = 2√2 R² · Pentagon ka diagonal = 1.618 × side · n-gon ka perimeter = n × side
Chord, radius r, centre se doori d: r² = d² + (chord/2)² · chord = r hai toh angle 60° · arch (chord c, height h): r = (c²/4 + h²) ÷ 2h
Units: 1 m = 100 cm, isliye 1 m² = 10,000 cm². 1 km = 1000 m.

Kab use karo: jab π = 22/7 likha ho, ya numbers mein 7, 14, 21, 44, 88, 154, 616 dikhein.

Soch aise: 22/7 mein neeche 7 hai. Examiner chahta hai ki 7 kat jaaye. Isliye radius almost hamesha 7, 14, 21, 28, 35 ya 42 hota hai. r = 7 par C = 2 × 22/7 × 7 = 44 aur A = 22/7 × 49 = 154. Baaki sab isi ke guna hain: radius dugna, toh C dugna aur A chaar guna.

Speed method 3 · Jod, farq aur area ka jhatka: (l + b)² = d² + 2A

Kab use karo: rectangle mein area aur diagonal diya, perimeter poochha. Ya rhombus mein side aur area diya, diagonals ka jod poochha.

Soch aise: (l + b)² = l² + b² + 2lb. Ab l² + b² toh diagonal ka square hai, aur lb area hai. Toh (l + b)² = d² + 2 × area. Ghata do toh (l − b)² = d² − 2 × area. l aur b alag-alag nikalne ki zaroorat hi nahi.

Rhombus mein yahi kahani aadhe diagonals ke saath chalti hai. Wahan (d₁ + d₂)² = 4a² + 4 × area. (Kyunki d₁² + d₂² = 4a² aur d₁ × d₂ = 2 × area.)
