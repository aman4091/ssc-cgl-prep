2025–26 SSC PYQs (CGL, CHSL, CPO, Selection Post XIV, MTS, DP, GD 2026) · is hisse mein: kaun kis set mein, terminating/recurring, rational + irrational, √ ki tulna

Pehle ek kahani: numbers kaise bade hote gaye

Socho ek bachcha pehli baar ginti seekh raha hai. Wo bolta hai: 1, 2, 3, 4… Ye ginti wale numbers hain. Inhe Natural numbers (N) kehte hain.

  • Phir koi poochta hai: "tokri mein kitne aam hain?" Tokri khaali hai. Jawab 0. Ginti mein 0 jod diya, toh bane Whole numbers (W): 0, 1, 2, 3…
  • Phir sardi aayi. Thermometer −4°C dikha raha hai. Diver samundar mein −30 m par hai. Ab minus wale bhi chahiye. Ye sab mila ke Integers (Z): …, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2…
  • Phir roti aadhi kaati: 1/2. Teen-chauthai glass: 3/4. Jo bhi number p/q (q ≠ 0, p aur q integers) ki tarah likh sako, wo Rational (Q) hai. 5 bhi rational hai, kyunki 5 = 5/1.
  • Ab ek ajeeb baat. 1 meter ke square ka diagonal = √2. Ye kisi bhi p/q mein fit nahi hota. Iska decimal 1.41421356… na kabhi khatam hota hai, na repeat. Aise numbers Irrational hain: √2, √3, √51, π.
  • Rational + irrational, dono milake number line ka har point bhar dete hain. Ye hai Real numbers (R).
  • Aur √−7? Kisi bhi real number ka square minus nahi hota. Toh √−7 real nahi hai. Ye Complex parivaar mein jaata hai.
3 baatein jinse saare sawaal bante hain 1. Seedhi (ladder): N → W → Z → Q → R → C. Jo number neeche wale dabbe mein hai, wo upar wale sab dabbon mein bhi hai. 5 natural hai, toh whole, integer, rational, real sab hai.
2. Decimal se pehchaan: rational ka decimal ya toh khatam hota hai (terminating) ya repeat hota hai (recurring). Irrational ka na khatam, na repeat.
3. Rational aur irrational ka koi common number nahi. Dono ka jod (union) = Real. Dono ka common (intersection) = khaali.

Paper mein ek sawaal ke ~36 second milte hain. Is hisse ke zyada sawaal theory wale hain: 5 second mein ho jaane chahiye. Neeche ke tareeke wahi speed dete hain.

Perfect squares: pehchaan, sabse paas wala square, aur ginti

46 PYQs · sabse zyada: "kaunsa perfect square ho sakta hai", "kitna jodo / ghatao ki square bane", "beech mein kitne square"

Pehle ek kahani: square matlab chaukor

16 sikke lo. Unhe 4 line mein 4-4 rakho. Ek poora chaukor ban gaya. Isliye 16 ek perfect square hai: 16 = 4 × 4 = 4². Ab 17 sikke lo. Chaukor nahi banega, ek sikka bach jaata hai.

Ab 4 × 4 ke chaukor ko 5 × 5 banana hai. Kitne naye sikke chahiye? Ek taraf 4, doosri taraf 4, aur kone mein 1. Kul 9 = 2 × 4 + 1. Yahi chhoti si baat aadhe sawaal hal kar deti hai.

3 baatein jinse saare sawaal bante hain 1. Last digit: square ka last digit sirf 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, 9 ho sakta hai. Kabhi 2, 3, 7, 8 nahi.
2. Do squares ka gap: n² se (n+1)² tak 2n + 1. Beech mein 2n numbers, sab non-square.
3. Prime factors ki power even: 3600 = 2⁴ × 3² × 5². Har power even, toh square. Isi se "kisse guna karein ki square bane" hal hota hai.

Speed method 5 · Beech mein kitne squares / non-squares

  • n² aur (n+1)² ke beech: 2n natural numbers, sab non-square. 11² aur 12² ke beech 22.
  • a aur b ke beech kitne squares: √a ko upar round karo, √b ko neeche round karo. Ginti = bada − chhota + 1. 50 se 1000: √50 → 8, √1000 → 31 → 31 − 8 + 1 = 24.

Pehle ek kahani: baantna aur bachna

Tumhare paas 26 laddoo hain. 8 dosto mein barabar baantne hain. Har dost ko 3 mile (24 gaye), 2 bach gaye. Yahi poori kahani hai:

Ek line jo yaad rakhni hai Number = bhaajak × bhaagfal + sheshfal (Dividend = Divisor × Quotient + Remainder). 26 = 8 × 3 + 2.
Agar sheshfal 0 hai, toh number "divisible" hai. Divisibility ke saare rule bas ye batate hain ki poora bhaag kiye bina sheshfal 0 hai ya nahi.

Ye rule kaam kyun karte hain? 10, 100, 1000 ke andar chhupe hain. 100 = 4 × 25, toh 100 ka koi bhi multiple 4 se katega. Isliye 4 ke liye sirf last 2 digit dekhne hain. 1000 = 8 × 125, toh 8 ke liye last 3 digit. Aur 10 = 9 + 1, toh har 10 "9 ka multiple + 1" hai. Isliye 9 aur 3 ke liye digits ka jod.

LCM aur HCF: shuru se, aur 36-second wale tareeke

2025–26 SSC PYQs (CHSL, CGL, MTS, DP, GD 2026, Selection Post XIV) · sabse zyada: ratio + HCF/LCM, HCF × LCM = guna, tiles / chocolates (HCF), ghantiyan / traffic light (LCM), remainder wale

Pehle do chhoti kahaniyan

Kahani 1 · Rassi kaatna. Tumhare paas 12 m aur 18 m ki do rassiyan hain. Dono ko barabar tukdon mein kaatna hai, kuch bachna nahi chahiye. Tukda kitna bada ho sakta hai?

  • 1 m ke tukde? Chalega. 2 m? Chalega. 3 m? Chalega. 6 m? 12 = 6 × 2, 18 = 6 × 3. Chalega.
  • 9 m? 12 mein nahi jaata. Toh sabse bada tukda = 6 m.

Jo number dono ko poora kaat de, wo common factor. Inmein sabse bada = HCF (Highest Common Factor). HCF(12, 18) = 6.

Kahani 2 · Gym ka milan. Ravi har 4 din gym jaata hai, Amit har 6 din. Aaj dono saath the. Agli baar saath kab?

  • Ravi ke din: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24… Amit ke din: 6, 12, 18, 24…
  • Pehli baar common = 12. Ye dono ka sabse chhota common multiple = LCM (Least Common Multiple).
Ek line mein farq HCF = sabse bada number jo sabko kaate (tukda, naap, baantna). Hamesha sabse chhote number se chhota ya barabar.
LCM = sabse chhota number jise sab kaatein (milan, phir saath). Hamesha sabse bade number se bada ya barabar.

Speed method 1 · Prime factor ka gol-gol chitra

Kab use karo: numbers prime powers mein diye hon (2⁴ × 3² × 5…), ya 2 numbers ka HCF aur LCM ek saath chahiye.

Soch aise: har number primes ki ek thaili hai. 36 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 3. 48 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3. Jo primes dono thailiyon mein common hain, wo HCF. Sab primes ek baar (common sirf ek baar), wo LCM.

Speed method 2 · LCM: sabse bade number ke multiples chalao

Kab use karo: chhote numbers ka LCM (14, 21, 28 ya 90, 120, 150). Factor karne se tez hai.

Soch aise: LCM sabse bade number ka hi koi multiple hoga. Toh bade number ke multiples likhte jao: jo baaki sab se kat jaaye, wahi LCM.

Speed method 3 · HCF: farq nikaalo (Euclid ka jaadu)

Kab use karo: bade numbers ka HCF (378 aur 525), aur "same remainder" wale sawaal.

Soch aise: agar 21 dono rassiyon ko kaatta hai, toh unke farq ko bhi kaatega. Kyunki farq = (kuch 21) − (kuch 21). Toh HCF hamesha farq ka bhi factor hai. Bade numbers chhote farq mein badal jaate hain.

Kab use karo: "ratio 3 : 4, LCM 120", "ratio 5 : 8, HCF 6", "HCF 12, LCM 480, jod 156".
