Compound Interest: shuru se, aur 36-second wale tareeke

2025–26 SSC PYQs (CGL, CHSL, CPO, Selection Post XIV, MTS, Delhi Police) · sabse zyada: do amount se rate, CI − SI, "x guna kitne saal mein"

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: ₹1000 ka safar

Maan lo tumne bank mein ₹1000 rakhe. Bank kehta hai, har saal 10% dunga.

  • Saal 1: 1000 ka 10% = 100. Ab tumhare paas 1100 hain.
  • Saal 2: ab bank 1100 par 10% deta hai, sirf 1000 par nahi. 1100 ka 10% = 110. Paas mein 1210.
  • Saal 3: 1210 ka 10% = 121. Paas mein 1331.

Dekho, interest har saal badh raha hai: 100, 110, 121. Kyun? Kyunki pichhle saal ka interest bhi ab paise ka hissa ban gaya. Uspar bhi interest lag raha hai.

Agar bank har saal sirf 1000 par hi 10% deta, toh har saal 100 hi milta: 100, 100, 100. Isi ko Simple Interest (SI) kehte hain. Aur jo pehla wala tareeka tha, jismein interest par bhi interest lagta hai, wo hai Compound Interest (CI).

"Interest par interest" kahan se aaya?

  • Saal 2 ke 110 ko todo: 100 toh mool 1000 ka hai. Baaki 10 kahan se aaya? Saal 1 ke 100 rupaye par 10%. Yahi hai interest par interest.
  • Saal 3 ke 121 ko todo: 100 mool ka. 20 aaya pichhle do saal ke 100 + 100 par. Aur 1 aaya us 10 par jo khud interest ka interest tha.
  • Isliye pehle saal CI aur SI hamesha barabar hote hain. Farq saal 2 se shuru hota hai. PYQ ×2 CHSL · 29 Nov, 30 Nov 2025
  • Isliye CI hamesha SI se zyada hota hai (2 saal ya usse zyada mein). Option chunte waqt ye yaad rakhna.
Ab naam rakh dete hain Jo paisa shuru mein rakha = P (principal). Aakhir mein jo mila = A (amount). A − P = CI. Rate = r% har saal. Saal = n.
Population ka badhna bhi yahi kahani hai. Machine ki keemat ka girna (depreciation) bhi yahi hai, bas har saal % ghatta hai.

Ab asli baat. Paper mein ek sawaal ke liye lagbhag 36 second milte hain. Wahan koi saal-dar-saal decimal guna nahi karega. Neeche 7 tareeke hain jo haath se, rough mein, jaldi chalte hain.

Speed method 1 · Rate ko fraction banao (ratio method)

Kab use karo: jab rate ek saaf fraction ban jaye. 10% = 1/10, 20% = 1/5, 25% = 1/4, 12½% = 1/8, 16⅔% = 1/6, 5% = 1/20.

Soch aise: 10% = 1/10 ka matlab hai, 10 rupaye par 1 rupaya interest. Toh ek saal mein 10 rupaye 11 ban jaate hain. Yaani P : A = 10 : 11.

Do saal? Wahi kaam do baar: 10 × 10 = 100 aur 11 × 11 = 121. Toh P : A = 100 : 121. Teen saal: 1000 : 1331. Ab koi decimal nahi, sirf "parts".

Demo · 10% par 2 saal ka CI ₹2100 hai. P kitna? · ~10 sec 10% → 100 : 121. CI = 121 − 100 = 21 parts.
21 parts = 2100 → 1 part = 100 → P = 100 parts = ₹10,000.
Demo · ₹2500 par 20%, 3 saal ka amount? · ~10 sec 20% → 125 : 216. P = 125 parts = 2500 → 1 part = 20.
A = 216 × 20 = ₹4,320.

Ye rates ke fraction yaad kar lo. Har card mein: rate, uska fraction, aur 1 saal ka P : A.

Trick: "Rate 1/x hai toh P = x, A = x + 1." 2 saal ho toh dono ka square, 3 saal ho toh dono ka cube. Phir CI = A − P parts. Bas 1 part ki value nikaalo. Kabhi decimal mat banao.

Ghatne wala rate (depreciation): 10% girna → P : A = 10 : 9. 2 saal = 100 : 81, 3 saal = 1000 : 729.

Speed method 2 · Effective rate (2 aur 3 saal ka "ek jhatka")

Kab use karo: jab rate fraction mein saaf na baithe (jaise 12%, 15%, 9%), aur time 2 ya 3 saal ho.

Soch aise: 2 saal ka CI ek hi baar ka % bana do. 10% par 2 saal = 21%. Yaani P ka 21% hi CI hai. Phir bas "P ka itna %" nikaalna hai.

5 second wala formula: a + b + ab/100 Do baar a% aur b% badha, toh kul badhat = a + b + (a × b)/100.
12% do saal: 12 + 12 + (12 × 12)/100 = 24 + 1.44 = 25.44%.
9% do saal: 9 + 9 + 0.81 = 18.81%. Alag rate 8% phir 10%: 8 + 10 + 0.8 = 18.8%.

Speed method 3 · 1-3-3-1 ka ped (3 saal, kisi bhi rate par)

Kab use karo: 3 saal, aur rate ganda hai (12%, 15%, 6%, 4%). Yahi wo jagah hai jahan log decimal ka cube karne baith jaate hain. Uski zaroorat nahi.

Soch aise: ₹1000 par 12%. Pehla tukda = 1000 ka 12% = 120. Ye har saal aata hai, toh 3 baar. Doosra tukda = 120 ka 12% = 14.4 (interest par interest). Ye 3 baar banta hai. Teesra tukda = 14.4 ka 12% = 1.728. Ye sirf 1 baar. Ginti 1-3-3-1 Pascal ki seedhi se aati hai.

Demo · ₹25,000 par 10%, 3 saal ka CI · ~12 sec Tukde: 10% of 25,000 = 2500 → 10% of 2500 = 250 → 10% of 250 = 25.
CI = 3 × 2500 + 3 × 250 + 1 × 25 = 7500 + 750 + 25 = ₹8,275.
Trick: 4 saal ke liye seedhi 1-4-6-4-1. ₹30,000 par 10%: 4 × 3000 + 6 × 300 + 4 × 30 + 1 × 3 = 13,923. Pehla tukda (4 × 3000) hi SI hai.

Soch aise: 2 saal mein SI aur CI mein farq sirf ek hi cheez ka hai. Saal 1 ka interest jo bacha tha, us par saal 2 mein jo interest laga. Bas wahi ek chhota kona.

Speed method 5 · "x guna, t saal mein"

Kab use karo: "paisa 2 guna ho jata hai 3 saal mein, 8 guna kab hoga?" type sawaal.

Soch aise: 3 saal mein 2 guna. Agle 3 saal mein wo 2 guna phir 2 guna hoga = 4 guna. Agle 3 mein 8 guna. Toh guna power mein badhta hai, saal jod mein.

Speed method 6 · Half-yearly, quarterly, 8-monthly

Soch aise: half-yearly matlab bank saal mein 2 baar interest jodta hai. Toh har baar aadha rate milta hai, aur jodne ke mauke dugne ho jaate hain.

  • Half-yearly: rate ÷ 2, time × 2. 20% for 1½ saal → 10% for 3 baar → ratio 1000 : 1331.
  • Quarterly: rate ÷ 4, time × 4. 20% for 9 mahine → 5% for 3 baar → ratio 8000 : 9261.
  • Har 8 mahine: rate × 8/12, baar = kul mahine ÷ 8. 15% for 2 saal → 10% for 3 baar. PYQ ×2
  • Rate badal gaya, phir wahi purane tareeke: ratio, table ya ped.

Speed method 7 · Option se pakdo

  • CI > SI hamesha (2 saal ya zyada). SI pehle nikaalo (bas r × n %). Jo option SI se chhota ya barabar hai, kaat do.
  • CI bahut zyada bhi nahi hota. 2 saal mein CI − SI = P ka (r/100)². 10% par ye sirf P ka 1% hai. Toh CI SI se thoda hi upar hoga.
  • Last digit / paise dekho. ₹1000 par 10%, 3 saal → 331, poora number. ₹25,000 → 25 × 331, last digit 5. ₹2,320.5 jaisa answer aadhe rupaye par khatam hota hai, kyunki 5000 × 4641/10000.
  • Divisibility: 20% 3 saal → P 125 ka multiple aur A 216 ka multiple hona chahiye (jab answer poora ho).
  • "Approx" likha ho ya rate ganda ho: sabse paas ka saaf rate lo, answer ka andaaza lagao, sabse kareeb wala option chuno.
Demo · 3 saal mein 3 guna. Rate? Options 44.2%, 66.7%, 100%, 33.3% · ~20 sec 50% = 1/2 → 2 : 3 → 3 saal 8 : 27 → 27/8 = 3.4 guna (thoda zyada).
40% = 2/5 → 5 : 7 → 3 saal 125 : 343 → lagbhag 2.7 guna (thoda kam).
Toh rate 40 aur 50 ke beech → 44.2%.

Kaunsa method kab? (ek nazar mein)

Formula chahiye toh (bas pehchaan ke liye) Kitaab ka formula A = P(1 + r/100)n wahi baat hai jo ratio method karta hai. 10% par (1 + 1/10) = 11/10, yaani 10 : 11. Isliye formula ratta mat maaro, ratio ki seedhi samjho.
Half-yearly ka saal bhar ka asli rate = r + r²/400. 9% half-yearly → 4.5 + 4.5 + 0.2025 = 9.2025%. PYQ
CGL+ Rule of 72: paisa lagbhag 72 ÷ r saal mein dugna hota hai. 8% → ~9 saal. Sirf andaaze ke liye.

Check yourself

12½% ko fraction mein likho. 2 saal ka P : A kya hoga?

1/8. Ek saal 8 : 9, toh 2 saal 64 : 81. CI = 17 parts.

2 saal ke ped mein "r% ka r%" wale tukde kitne hote hain?

Sirf ek. Wahi ek tukda CI − SI hai, yaani P × (r/100)².

15% par 2 saal ka CI kitne % banta hai? 5 second mein.

15 + 15 + 225/100 = 32.25%.

Paisa 3 guna hota hai 4 saal mein. 27 guna kab?

27 = 3³, toh 3 × 4 = 12 saal.

Common traps

GaltiSahi tareeka
"2 saal 6 mahine, yearly" mein aadhe saal ko bhi compound karnaPoore saal compound. Aadha saal simple: 10% par bas +5% jodo (a + b + ab/100 se).
Half-yearly mein rate aadha kiya, par time wahi rakhaRate aadha aur baar dugne. 8-monthly: rate × 8/12, baar = mahine ÷ 8.
CI pucha, amount likh diya (ya ulta)Options mein dono hote hain. Sawaal ki aakhri line dobara padho.
"Saal 3 ka interest" ko 3 saal ka CI samajhnaSaal 3 ka interest = interest ki seedhi ka teesra kadam.
4 saal mein 2 guna → 8 guna 16 saal mein8 = 2³ → 3 × 4 = 12 saal. Power, seedha guna nahi.
3 saal mein 3 guna → rate 66.7% ya 100%Options ko 40% aur 50% se bracket karo → 44.2%.
Girawat mein peeche jaane ke liye 10% do baar jodnaRatio 10 : 9 → 100 : 81. Abhi ki keemat = 81 parts.
Paisa nikalne ya jama karne ke baad purana balance chalanaHar badlaav ke baad naya balance hi naya P.
3 saal ya half-yearly mein bhi P × (r/100)² lagana3 saal: × (3 + r/100). Half-yearly: CI aur SI dono parts mein nikaalo.
Option decimal mein (0.1)0.1 = 10%. CGL 15 Sep 2025 mein yahi tha.
Question bank ke baare mein Kul 103 sawaal. 3 chhode kyunki scan mein number ud gaye: DP 9 Jan 2026 Shift 1 ke do ("do saal baad aur … saal baad rakam", aur "do saal mein SI aur CI … rate") aur SP-XIV 29 Aug 2025 (14% SI aur 7% CI, farq ₹392). Chaar aur scan wale sawaal options se dobara banaye (1½ saal wala, 16⅔% wala, 1⅓ saal wala, aur half-yearly CI − SI wala). Har ek upar mark hai. CHSL 24 Nov 2025 ka ek sawaal "same annual simple interest rate" bolta hai par asal mein seedha CI hai. Use CI ki tarah hi hal kiya hai.

Check yourself

₹20,000 par 10%, 2 saal 6 mahine, yearly compounding. CI?

2 saal = 21%. Phir aadha saal 5%: 21 + 5 + 1.05 = 27.05%. 200 × 27.05 = ₹5,410.

₹5,000 par 8%, 2 saal: CI − SI?

Kona = 5,000 × 8/100 × 8/100 = 5,000 × 64/10,000 = ₹32.

Rakam 9 saal mein 27 guna hoti hai. 3 guna kitne saal mein?

27 = 3³, toh 3 guna 9 ÷ 3 = 3 saal mein.

2 saal baad ₹2,420, 3 saal baad ₹2,662. Rate aur P?

Badhat 242 = 2,420 ka 10% → 10%. 2,420 = 121 parts → 1 part = 20 → P = ₹2,000.

₹40,000 par 20%, 6 mahine, quarterly. CI?

5% × 2 baar → 5 + 5 + 0.25 = 10.25% → 400 × 10.25 = ₹4,100.
