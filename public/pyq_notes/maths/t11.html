Mixture & Alligation: shuru se, aur 36-second wale tareeke

2025–26 SSC PYQs (CGL, CHSL, CPO, SP-XIV, MTS, Delhi Police, GD) · sabse zyada: paani milao to ratio badlo, nikaalo-aur-bharo, profit wala alligation

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: do bori chawal

Ek dukaandaar ke paas do chawal hain. Ek ₹40 kilo, doosra ₹60 kilo.

  • 1 kg sasta + 1 kg mehnga milaya. 2 kg ka kharcha 40 + 60 = 100. Toh 1 kg mix = ₹50. Theek beech mein.
  • Ab 3 kg sasta + 1 kg mehnga. Kharcha 120 + 60 = 180, maal 4 kg. 1 kg = ₹45. Sasta zyada tha, toh price saste ki taraf khisak gaya.
  • 1 kg sasta + 3 kg mehnga: 40 + 180 = 220 ÷ 4 = ₹55. Ab mehnge ki taraf.

Teen baatein dikhi. Mix ka price hamesha dono ke beech rahta hai. Jiska maal zyada, price uski taraf jhukta hai. Aur 50 se 45 tak aane ke liye kitna "jhukna" hai, wo ratio se tay hota hai. Bas, poora chapter yahi hai.

Ab naam rakh dete hain Mix ka price = mean price (weighted average). Ratio diya, mean nikaalna = mixture wala sawaal.
Mean diya, ratio nikaalna = ulta kaam. Isi ulte kaam ke shortcut ko alligation kehte hain.
Price ki jagah % bhi ho sakta hai: 30% acid aur 60% acid. Ya ratio: doodh-paani 3 : 2 matlab doodh 3/5 = 60%. Kaam wahi.

Speed method 1 · Mean price: "sabse saste se kitna upar"

Kab use karo: ratio diya hai, mix ka price ya % pucha hai.

Soch aise: 40 aur 60 ko 3 : 2 mein milaya. Sab maal kam se kam ₹40 ka toh hai. Upar ka "extra" sirf mehnge maal mein hai: 20 rupaye, aur wo maal kul ka 2/5 hai. Toh mean = 40 + 20 × 2/5 = 40 + 8 = 48. Koi lamba jod-guna nahi.

Demo · 3 kg ₹16 wala + 5 kg ₹24 wala. Mean? · ~8 sec Base 16. Extra 8 rupaye, sirf 5/8 maal mein → 8 × 5/8 = 5.
Mean = 16 + 5 = ₹21.
Trick: "Base + farq × (mehnge ka hissa)". % mein bhi chalta hai: 25% fat ke 4 L + 40% fat ke 6 L → 25 + 15 × 6/10 = 34%.

Speed method 2 · Alligation cross (mean diya, ratio nikaalo)

Kab use karo: do cheezein, unka price (ya %), aur mix ka price diya hai. Ratio pucha hai. Ya ek quantity di hai, doosri puchi hai.

Soch aise: picture wali baat ulti chalao. Mean se mehnga jitna door, utna sasta lo. Mean se sasta jitna door, utna mehnga lo. Bas cross mein ghata do.

Demo · 30% acid aur 60% acid se 50% banana hai. Ratio? · ~6 sec 30 ke neeche 60 − 50 = 10. 60 ke neeche 50 − 30 = 20.
30% : 60% = 10 : 20 = 1 : 2.
Trick: Paani = ₹0 ya 0% maan lo. Pure cheez = 100%. Phir kisi bhi "paani milao" ya "pure daalo" sawaal par bhi cross chal jaata hai. ₹30 juice + paani → ₹24: 30 ke neeche 24, paani ke neeche 6 → juice : paani = 4 : 1.

Speed method 3 · Profit diya hai? Pehle SP ko CP banao

Kab use karo: "mix ko ₹X mein becha aur Y% munafa hua". Alligation hamesha cost price par lagta hai, selling price par nahi.

Soch aise: 20% munafa matlab 100 ka maal 120 mein. Toh CP = SP × 100/120 = SP × 5/6. Ye guna fraction se karo:

Munafa10%12½%16⅔%20%25%Nuksaan 10%

Demo · ₹60 aur ₹90 ki daal mili, mix ₹84 mein becha, 20% munafa. Ratio? · ~10 sec CP = 84 × 5/6 = 70. Cross: 60 ke neeche 90 − 70 = 20, 90 ke neeche 70 − 60 = 10.
60 wali : 90 wali = 2 : 1.

Speed method 4 · Teen ya zyada cheezein: "kul paisa" method

Kab use karo: 3 varieties ka ratio diya, ek ka price nahi pata. Cross 2 cheezon ke liye hai, isliye yahan seedha kul paisa barabar karo.

Soch aise: ratio 1 : 1 : 2 hai, toh 4 kg ka mix. Mix ₹150 kilo, toh 4 kg ka kul paisa = 600. Pehle do ka paisa ghatao, jo bacha wo teesre ka.

Demo · ₹120, ₹130, x ko 1 : 1 : 2 mein milaya, mix ₹150. x? · ~10 sec Kul = 4 × 150 = 600. Pehle do = 120 + 130 = 250. Bacha 350, ye 2 kg ka.
x = ₹175.

Speed method 5 · Ratio ko % banao (mixtures ke mixture)

Kab use karo: do bartan hain, har ek mein doodh-paani alag ratio mein. Unko milana hai.

Soch aise: 3 : 2 matlab doodh 3/5 = 60%. 4 : 1 matlab doodh 80%. Ab ye bas do % hain. Barabar maatra milao toh 70% → 7 : 3. Ratio puchha ho toh cross lagao.

Trick: Bartan alag size ke hain (jaise 3 : 4 : 5)? Har bartan ko "litre" maan lo jo denominators se kat jaaye. X (3 parts, 1 : 2) mein juice 1, Y (4 parts, 3 : 1) mein 3, Z (5 parts, 2 : 3) mein 2. Kul juice 6, kul 12 → 1 : 1.

Speed method 6 · Jo nahi chheda, use pakdo (paani ya doodh daala)

Kab use karo: "x litre paani mila diya, ratio badal gaya". Ek hi cheez daali gayi.

Soch aise: 7 : 4 mein 12 L paani daala, ratio 7 : 6 ho gaya. Alcohol ko kisi ne chhua hi nahi, wo dono ratio mein 7 hai. Paani 4 se 6 hua, yaani 2 hisse badhe = 12 L. Toh 1 hissa = 6 L.

Agar dono ratio mein unchhui cheez ka number alag ho (5 : 3 se 7 : 3), toh pehle use barabar kar lo (LCM). Neeche picture dekho.

Demo · 56 L mein doodh : paani 5 : 3. Kitna doodh daalein ki 7 : 3 ho? · ~8 sec Paani dono mein 3 hai. Toh doodh 5 → 7, 2 hisse badhe. 56 L = 8 hisse → 1 hissa 7 L.
Daalo = 2 × 7 = 14 L.

Speed method 7 · Nikaalo aur bharo (replacement)

Ek baar: mix ka 1/5 nikaala, toh har cheez 4/5 bachi. Phir jo bhara wo jod do. 60 L (7 : 3 = 42 + 18) mein se 12 L (1/5) nikaala → 33.6 + 14.4. 12 L doodh daala → 45.6 : 14.4 = 19 : 6.

Speed method 8 · Option se pakdo

  • Mean beech mein hi hoga. ₹350 aur ₹450 ka mix, 6 hissa mehnga → 400 se upar, 450 se neeche → 410 hi bachta hai.
  • Ratio ka jod kul se kate. 90 L ka 7 : 2 ho toh 9 hisse, 1 hissa 10 L. Option jo 9 parts mein na baithe, shak karo.
  • Zyada maal wali taraf. Mean jis price ke paas hai, ratio mein wahi bada hoga. 4% aur 8% se 7% → 8% wala zyada → 3 : 1, 1 : 3 nahi.
  • Ulta ratio jaal. Options mein 1 : 4 aur 4 : 1 dono hote hain. Sawaal "paani : doodh" puchhe ya "doodh : paani", aakhri line dobara padho.

Problems on Trains: shuru se, aur 36-second wale tareeke

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: train ki naak aur poonch

Ek train 100 m lambi hai. Platform par ek aadmi khada hai. Train ne aadmi ko "cross" kab kiya?

  • Jab train ki naak (engine) aadmi tak aayi, crossing shuru hui.
  • Jab train ki poonch (aakhri dabba) aadmi se aage nikli, crossing poori hui.
  • Is beech naak ne kitna chala? Poori train ki lambai: 100 m. Aadmi ki apni chaudai zero maan lo.

Ab wahi train 200 m ke platform ko cross kare. Naak platform ke shuru mein, aur poonch platform ke aakhir se bahar. Naak ne chala: 200 (platform) + 100 (khud ki lambai) = 300 m.

Bas yahi poora chapter hai. Train ke sawaal mein doori = train ki lambai + jo cross kiya uski lambai. Baaki sab wahi purana: doori = speed × time.

Boat & Stream: do speed, jod aur ghata

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: nadi mein naav

Ek naav shaant paani (jheel) mein 10 km/h chalti hai. Ab use nadi mein utaaro jahan paani 2 km/h se beh raha hai.

  • Dhaara ke saath (downstream): naav apne 10 bhi chal rahi hai, aur paani use 2 aage dhakel raha hai. Kul 12 km/h.
  • Dhaara ke ulte (upstream): naav 10 aage, paani 2 peeche kheench raha hai. Kul 8 km/h.

Ab ulta socho. Tumhe sirf 12 aur 8 pata hai. Naav ki asli speed? Theek beech mein: (12 + 8)/2 = 10. Dhaara? Aadha farq: (12 − 8)/2 = 2. Bas, yahi poora chapter hai.

Downstream D = b + s. Upstream U = b − s.

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: 12 litre ki balti

Ek balti 12 litre ki hai. Nal A use 4 minute mein bharta hai. Nal B 6 minute mein.

  • A har minute 12 ÷ 4 = 3 litre daalta hai.
  • B har minute 12 ÷ 6 = 2 litre.
  • Dono saath: 3 + 2 = 5 litre har minute. 12 litre bharne mein 12 ÷ 5 = 2.4 minute.

Dhyaan do: humne balti 12 litre ki kyun li? Kyunki 12, 4 aur 6 dono se kat jaata hai (LCM). Sawaal mein balti ka size diya hi nahi hota. Toh tank = LCM litre maan lo, aur saara fraction gayab.

Aur agar ek chhed (leak) ho jo 12 minute mein khaali karta hai? Wo har minute 1 litre nikaalta hai. Bas use minus likho: 3 + 2 − 1 = 4 litre/minute → 3 minute.
