Simplification practice (1): BODMAS ki lambi line, decimal, root aur identity

GD Constable 2026, MTS 2025, Selection Post XIV · sabse zyada: "93 − 27 + 38 of 1 …" wali lambi line (87 baar!), phir root aur (a ± b)² wale sawaal
Exam ke short naam GD = GD Constable 2026 · MTS = MTS 2025 · SP-XIV Grad / 12th / 10th = Selection Post Phase XIV 2025 (Graduate, 12th, 10th level). Date shift ki date hai. Har sawaal ke aage bold mein method likha hai.
Methods recap (inhi se saare sawaal bante hain) 1. Tokri method (BODMAS line): pehle bracket, "of", ×, ÷. Phir saare plus ek tokri mein, saare minus doosri mein. Ek hi ghatana. ~20 sec.
2. Decimal shift: ÷ ho toh dono ko 10, 100 se guna karke decimal hata do. × ho toh decimal places gin kar jodo. ~10 sec.
3. Root mein decimal aadha: √ mein places aadhe, ∛ mein ek-tihaai. √0.0225 = 0.15 (4 → 2 places). ~5 sec.
4. Identity pehchano: (a+b)² − 4ab = (a − b)², a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b), a³ + b³ + 3ab(a + b) = (a + b)³. Number ko kabhi square mat karo. ~10 sec.
5. Surd ko √2, √3, √5 mein todo: √48 = 4√3. Phir like terms jodo. ~10 sec.
6. Conjugate: (a + √b)(a − √b) = a² − b. ~10 sec.
7. 1 se kitna kam (fractions compare): 11/13 = 1 − 2/13. Jiska gap chhota, wo bada. ~10 sec.
