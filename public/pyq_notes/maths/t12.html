Time, Speed aur Distance: shuru se, aur 36-second wale tareeke

2025–26 SSC PYQs (CGL, CHSL, CPO, MTS, Selection Post XIV, Delhi Police, GD 2026) · sabse zyada: unit badlo, average speed, aamne-saamne milna, speed badli to time ka farq

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: cycle ki sawaari

Maan lo tum cycle chala rahe ho. Har ghante tum 12 km aage badhte ho.

  • 1 ghanta: 12 km.
  • 2 ghante: 12 + 12 = 24 km.
  • 3 ghante: 36 km.

Ye "har ghante 12 km" hi tumhari speed hai. Kitni der chale = time. Kul kitna chale = distance. Upar ki ginti se seedha dikhta hai: distance = speed × time. 12 × 3 = 36.

Poora chapter bas teen baaton se banta hai. Inhe pakad lo, baaki sab tareeke inhi se nikalte hain.

3 asli baatein 1. D = S × T. Teen mein se do pata hon, teesra nikal jaata hai.
2. Ratio ka khel. Time same ho toh distance speed ke saath badhta hai (seedha). Distance same ho toh time speed ka ulta chalta hai: speed dugni, time aadha.
3. Do log saath chalein toh "relative speed". Aamne-saamne aayein toh speed jodo. Ek doosre ke peeche bhaagein toh speed ghatao.

Ab asli baat. Paper mein ek sawaal ke liye lagbhag 36 second milte hain. Wahan koi equation bana kar x nahi dhoondhta. Neeche 8 tareeke hain jo rough mein, haath se, jaldi chalte hain.

Speed method 1 · Unit badlo: 5/18 aur 18/5

Kab use karo: jab speed km/h mein ho aur jawab m/s mein chahiye, ya ulta. Ya jab sawaal mein metre aur second hon.

Soch aise: 1 km = 1000 m aur 1 ghanta = 3600 second. Toh 1 km/h = 1000/3600 m/s = 5/18 m/s. Ulta, 1 m/s = 18/5 = 3.6 km/h.

Ek jodi yaad karo: 18 km/h = 5 m/s. Baaki sab isi ke guna hain. 36 = 2 × 18 → 10 m/s. 54 = 3 × 18 → 15 m/s. 72 → 20. 90 → 25.

Demo · 300 m, 24 second mein. Speed km/h mein? · ~8 sec 300 ÷ 24 = 12.5 m/s. 12.5 × 18/5 = 2.5 × 18 = 45 km/h.
Trick: "m/s ko km/h banana ho toh 18 se guna, 5 se bhaag." Pehle 5 se kaato, phir 18 se guna. 25 m/s → 5 × 18 = 90. m/min se km/h: × 6/100. 140 m/min → 8.4 km/h.

Speed method 3 · Ratio parts: speed ulta, time ulta

Kab use karo: distance same hai, speed badli, aur time ka farq (ya naya time) pucha hai.

Soch aise: speed 20 se 18 hui. Speed ka ratio 10 : 9. Distance same, toh time ka ratio ulta: 9 : 10. Time 1 part badha. Wahi 1 part = jo extra minute diye.

Demo · Speed 20 se 18 km/h, 8 minute zyada lage. Distance? · ~12 sec Speed 10 : 9 → time 9 : 10. Farq 1 part = 8 min → purana time 9 × 8 = 72 min = 6/5 ghanta.
Distance = 20 × 6/5 = 24 km.
Demo · Usual speed ka 60% par chala, 18 min late. Usual time? · ~8 sec 60% = 3/5 → speed 5 : 3 → time 3 : 5. Farq 2 parts = 18 → 1 part = 9.
Usual = 3 parts = 27 min.

Doosra roop: u(u + k) wala jodi tareeka. "180 km, speed 15 badhao toh 1 ghanta kam." Yahan seedha jodi dhoondho: u × (u + 15) = 180 × 15 ÷ 1 = 2700. 2700 = 45 × 60. Toh u = 45. Ya options daal kar dekho: 45 par 4 ghante, 60 par 3 ghante. Farq 1. Ho gaya.

Trick: "Speed ka ratio a : b, toh time ka b : a." Farq ke parts = diya hua farq. Bas 1 part ki value nikaalo. Kabhi x wali equation mat banao.

Speed method 4 · Relative speed: jodo ya ghatao

Kab use karo: do gaadiyan, do log, chor-police. Jab bhi do cheezein ek saath chal rahi hon.

Soch aise: do bus aamne-saamne aa rahi hain, 50 aur 70 km/h. Ek ghante mein pehli 50 km aage aayi, doosri 70 km. Beech ka gap 120 km kam hua. Toh gap a + b ki speed se ghatta hai. Agar dono ek hi disha mein hon, police 20 aur chor 15, toh gap sirf 5 km har ghante ghatta hai: a − b.

  • Milne ki jagah: dono ne same time chala, toh distance ka ratio = speed ka ratio. 450 km, 50 aur 40 → 5 : 4 → pehla 250 km chalega.
  • Ek der se nikla: pehle akele wale ka "head start" nikaalo (jo usne akele chala). Bacha gap ÷ (a + b). Phir clock time jodo.
  • "Chor kitna bhaaga": gap × chor ki speed ÷ farq. "Police kitna bhaagi": gap × police ki speed ÷ farq.
Demo · Chor 10:00 baje 20 km/h se bhaaga. Police 10:30 par 30 km/h se. Kab pakdega? · ~10 sec 10:30 tak chor 20 × 1/2 = 10 km aage. Gap ghatta hai 30 − 20 = 10 km/h.
10 ÷ 10 = 1 ghanta → 11:30.

Speed method 6 · Late aur early: ek line ka formula

Kab use karo: "S1 se chalo toh late, S2 se chalo toh early. Distance?"

Soch aise: dono speed se pahunchne ke time mein kul farq = late + early. Distance same hai. Toh D ÷ S1 − D ÷ S2 = farq. Isko seedha likho: D = S1 × S2 ÷ (S2 − S1) × farq.

Demo · 4 km/h par 15 min late, 6 km/h par 5 min early. Distance? · ~10 sec Farq = 15 + 5 = 20 min = 1/3 ghanta. 4 × 6 ÷ 2 = 12.
D = 12 × 1/3 = 4 km.

Usi formula ka bhai (jaana-aana, kul time diya): D = kul time × ab ÷ (a + b). 30 se gaya, 20 se aaya, kul 5 ghante → 5 × 600 ÷ 50 = 60 km.

Speed method 7 · Gol track

Soch aise: gol track par milna = tez wale ne dheeme wale par ek poora chakkar "extra" bana liya (ek disha), ya dono ne mil kar ek poora chakkar pura kiya (ulti disha). Toh "gap" = track ki lambai.

  • Kitni baar mile, T time mein? T × relative speed ÷ track. Jo poora number aaye wahi (0.75 wala tukda gino mat).
  • Teeno start point par kab? Har ek ka ek chakkar ka time nikaalo, phir LCM. 12, 10, 8 min → LCM 120 min.

Speed method 8 · Option se pakdo

  • Average speed dono speed ke beech hi hoga, aur seedhe average se kam. 40 aur 60 ka jawab 40 aur 50 ke beech.
  • "Speed badhao toh 1 ghanta kam" type: options daalo. Jis option par dono time ka farq sahi baithe, wahi. Equation se tez hai.
  • √x jaise sawaal: options daalo. 2 × √4 = 4 = x. Bas.
  • Seedhe sawaal mein bhi galti mat karo: 150 ÷ 50 = 3. Is bank mein chaar seedhe sawaalon ki key galat hai (neeche note). Apni ginti par bharosa rakho.
