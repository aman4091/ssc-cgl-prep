Geometry ki neev: line, angle, triangle, polygon

Pehle ek chhoti si baat: angle hota kya hai?

Angle = kitna ghoome. Ek poora chakkar = 360°. Aadha chakkar, yaani ek seedhi line = 180°. Chautha hissa = 90° (L shape, right angle).

Bas yahi teen number poori geometry chalate hain. Har sawaal mein koi na koi cheez 90, 180 ya 360 ke barabar hoti hai. Tumhe bas pakadna hai, kaunsi.

  • Do angle mil kar 90 = complementary. Mil kar 180 = supplementary (ek line par bane do angle, "linear pair").
  • Do line kaatein toh aamne-saamne wale angle barabar = vertically opposite. Paas-paas wale mil kar 180.
  • Triangle ke teen angle = 180. Quadrilateral ke chaar = 360. Kyunki quadrilateral = 2 triangle.

Parallel lines: sirf do hi angle hote hain

Do parallel lines ko ek tirchhi line (transversal) kaate. 8 angle bante hain. Log 8 naam ratte hain: corresponding, alternate, co-interior… Uski zaroorat nahi.

Soch aise: 8 mein se har angle ya toh chhota (a) hai ya bada (180 − a). Bas. Chhote sab barabar, bade sab barabar, aur ek chhota + ek bada = 180.

Triangle: 180 kahan se aaya?

Triangle ke upar wale kone se neeche ki line ke parallel ek line kheencho. Ab A ke dono taraf jo angle bane, wo alternate angle hain. Ek B ke barabar, ek C ke barabar. Teeno mil kar ek seedhi line banate hain. Toh A + B + C = 180.

Isi se ek zabardast shortcut nikalta hai. BC ko aage D tak badhao. C par bahar wala angle (exterior) = 180 − C = A + B. Yaani bahar ka angle = andar ke do door wale angles ka jod.

Triangle ke chaar "centre": kaun kya hai?

Ek triangle mein teen-teen line kheench sakte ho. Har baar teeno line ek hi point par milti hain. Wo point ek centre hai. Line badlo, centre badal jaata hai.

Trick: "In = andar, bhuja chhoo" (incircle bhujaon ko touch karta) · "Circum = kono ke chakkar" (circumcircle kono se guzarta) · Equilateral mein chaaron ek hi point. CGL+ G, O, H ek seedhi line par hote hain (Euler line), aur HG : GO = 2 : 1.

Do triangle "ek jaise" kab? Congruence aur similarity

  • Congruent (≅): bilkul same. Kaat kar ek ke upar doosra rakho, poora fit. Bhuja bhi same, angle bhi.
  • Similar (~): shape same, size alag. Photo ka zoom. Angle same rehte hain, bhujayein ek hi ratio mein badhti hain.
