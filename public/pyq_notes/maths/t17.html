Geometry (part 2): rekha, kon aur triangle, figure dekh kar 20 second mein

2025–26 SSC PYQs (CHSL, CPO, MTS, Selection Post XIV, Delhi Police, GD) · sabse zyada: complement wale kon, parallel lines ke figure, similar triangle ka ratio, triangle ke centre

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: ek seedhi line aur ek chakkar

Ek seedhi line lo. Uspar kahin bhi khade ho kar ek taraf se doosri taraf ghoomo. Tum aadha chakkar ghoome. Aadha chakkar = 180°. Poora chakkar = 360°.

  • Seedhi line par ek point se jitne bhi kon bane, sab ka jod 180°. Isse "linear pair" kehte hain.
  • Ek point ke chaaron taraf ke saare kon: 360°.
  • Do lines ek doosre ko kaatein toh "X" banta hai. Aamne-saamne ke kon barabar (vertically opposite). Bagal wale ka jod 180°.

Bas yahi teen baatein poore chapter ki jad hain. Triangle ka 180° bhi isi se aata hai: triangle ke ek kone par, saamne wali side ke parallel ek line kheencho. Teeno kon us seedhi line par aa kar baith jaate hain.

Ab naam rakh dete hain Jod 90° ho toh dono kon complementary. Jod 180° ho toh supplementary. 90° se chhota = acute, 90° = right, 90° aur 180° ke beech = obtuse.
Paper mein ek sawaal ke liye lagbhag 36 second milte hain. Geometry mein zyada time figure samajhne mein jaata hai. Neeche wale tareeke figure ko 5 second mein "padhna" sikhaate hain.

Speed method 1 · Complement ka ek-line formula

Kab use karo: "angle apne complement ka 3 guna hai", "complement angle se 12° zyada hai" jaise sawaal. 2025 ke CPO aur Selection Post mein ye har shift mein aaya.

Soch aise: angle = x, toh complement = 90 − x. Agar x = 3 × complement, toh 90 ko 3 : 1 mein baanto. x ko 3 hisse, complement ko 1 hissa. 90 ÷ 4 = 22.5, x = 3 × 22.5 = 67.5°. Koi equation nahi, sirf hisse.

Ek line ka formula x = k × (90 − x) + c  →  x = (90k + c) ÷ (k + 1)
"Complement = x ÷ 4" ka matlab x = 4 × complement, yaani k = 4 → 360 ÷ 5 = 72°.
Do complementary kon ka farq D ho: bada = (90 + D) ÷ 2, chhota = (90 − D) ÷ 2.
Demo · angle apne complement ke 2 guna se 10° zyada hai · ~8 sec k = 2, c = +10 → (180 + 10) ÷ 3 = 190 ÷ 3 = 63.33°.
Trick: "k guna" wale sawaal mein 90 ko (k + 1) hisson mein baanto. Supplement ho toh 90 ki jagah 180. Option check: angle + complement = 90 hona hi chahiye.

Speed method 2 · Parallel lines: sirf do size ke kon

Soch aise: do parallel lines ko ek tirchhi line (transversal) kaate, toh 8 kon bante hain. Par unke size sirf do hote hain: ek chhota (a) aur ek bada (b). Aur a + b = 180°. Bas ye dekho ki tumhara kon chhota dikhta hai ya bada.

Demo · AB ∥ CD, ∠PRB = 74°. ∠DSQ? · ~6 sec ∠PRB upar-right wala kon hai, chhota (a = 74). ∠DSQ neeche-right wala, bada dikhta hai → b = 180 − 74 = 106°.

Speed method 3 · Parallel lines ke beech ya bahar ek point

Kab use karo: MTS ka sabse pasandida figure. Do parallel lines, aur ek point E (ya P) jisse dono lines tak rekha jaati hai. Teen hi shape aate hain:

  • Point beech mein (arrow / M shape): E par bana kon = dono lines par bane kon ka jod. ~5 sec.
  • Point bahar: P par bana kon = bada − chhota. ~5 sec.
  • Bow-tie: AC aur BD, E par katti hain. Upar wale triangle ka kon neeche wale ke Z-partner ke barabar. Pehle upar wale triangle ka teesra kon nikaalo (180 − do kon), phir wahi neeche. ~10 sec.
Trick: Dekho ki point lines ke beech hai ya bahar. Beech = plus, bahar = minus. Option mein dono hote hain (96 aur 32), isliye ye ek nazar zaroori hai.

Speed method 5 · Triangle ka bahar wala kon

Soch aise: triangle ki ek side ko aage badhao. Bahar jo kon bana, wo andar wale kon ka 180 − partner hai. Aur andar ke teeno ka jod bhi 180 hai. Toh bahar wala kon = door wale do andar ke kon ka jod.

Trick: Square + diagonal + ek line ho, toh diagonal hamesha 45° deta hai. Upar ke figure mein ∠EFA = ∠DEC + 45° (75 → 120, 65 → 110).

Circle: chord, tangent aur kon, ek right triangle se sab

2025–26 SSC PYQs · sabse zyada: tangent ki lambai (5-12-13), chord ki centre se doori, kendra ka kon = 2 × paridhi ka kon, do circles ke common tangent

Pehle kahani: circle mein chhupa hua right triangle

Circle ke centre O se ek chord par seedha (perpendicular) neeche aao. Wo line chord ko theek beech se kaat-ti hai. Ab O, chord ka midpoint, aur chord ka ek sira. Ye teen points ek right triangle banate hain.

Tangent mein bhi yahi hai. Tangent jahan circle ko chhoota hai, wahan radius aur tangent mein 90°. Toh centre, chhoone wala point aur bahar ka point P, phir ek right triangle.

Poore chapter ki 3 jad 1. Centre se chord par perpendicular → chord aadhi, aur r² = d² + (aadhi chord)².
2. Tangent ⟂ radius → (tangent)² = OP² − r².
3. Ek hi chord ka kon: centre par = 2 × circle par.

Speed method 1 · Chord, doori aur radius: triplet pehchano

Kab use karo: radius, chord ki lambai, centre se doori: in mein se do diye hain, teesra pucha hai. 90% baar ye 3-4-5, 5-12-13 ya 8-15-17 nikalta hai.

Demo · radius 10, chord 16 aur 12, ek hi taraf. Beech ki doori? · ~10 sec 6-8-10 jodi. Chord 16 → aadhi 8 → doori 6. Chord 12 → aadhi 6 → doori 8. Ek taraf → 8 − 6 = 2 cm.
Trick: Chord 90° banaye centre par → chord = r√2, aur doori = chord ÷ 2. Chord = radius → centre par 60° (equilateral triangle). Sabse lambi chord = diameter.

Speed method 2 · Circle ke kon: teen niyam

  • Kendra = 2 × paridhi. Paridhi wala point badi arc par ho tab. Chhoti arc par ho toh 180 − (kendra ÷ 2).
  • Same segment ke kon barabar. Ek chord, ek taraf ke saare points → ek hi kon.
  • Cyclic quadrilateral: aamne-saamne ka jod 180°. ∠A + ∠C = 180, ∠B + ∠D = 180.
  • Chords andar katein (E par): ∠AEC = (∠AOC + ∠BOD) ÷ 2. Bahar milein (K par): ∠K = (badi arc − chhoti arc) ÷ 2.
  • Tangent aur chord ka kon = doosri taraf ke segment ka kon (alternate segment). Tangent-chord 30° → chord centre par 60°.
Trick: Equilateral triangle circle mein ho, toh har side centre par 120° banati hai. Badi arc par 60°, chhoti arc par 120°.

Speed method 3 · Bahar ke point se do tangent

Trick: P tak "sabse chhoti doori" di ho (circle se), toh OP = r + wo doori. Phir (r + d)² = r² + t² → r = (t² − d²) ÷ 2d.

Speed method 4 · Chords katein: guna barabar

Kab use karo: do chords ek point par katein (andar ya bahar), ya tangent aur secant.

  • Andar E par: AE × EB = CE × ED. 3 × 2 = 6 × ED → ED = 1. ~5 sec.
  • Bahar P par: PA × PB = PC × PD (P se dono sire tak, poori lambai). ~5 sec.
  • Tangent + secant: PT² = PR × PS. ~8 sec.

Concentric circles: bade circle ki chord chhote ko chhooye, toh chhoone ka point chord ka midpoint. Aadhi chord = √(R² − r²).

Pehle kahani: polygon ke kinare-kinare chalo

Ek regular pentagon ke kinare par chalna shuru karo. Har kone par tum thoda mudte ho. Poora chakkar laga kar wahin pahunchte ho jahan se chale the. Yaani kul milakar 360° mude.

Ye mudna hi exterior angle hai. 5 kone, toh har kone par 360 ÷ 5 = 72°. Aur andar ka kon = 180 − 72 = 108°. Kisi bhi polygon mein yahi.

Speed method 1 · Hamesha bahar ka kon nikaalo

Kab use karo: polygon ka koi bhi sawaal. Andar ka kon diya ho, ratio diya ho, farq diya ho, sab ko pehle bahar ke kon mein badlo. Phir n = 360 ÷ bahar.

  • Andar diya: bahar = 180 − andar. 160° → 20° → n = 18. ~5 sec.
  • Andar : bahar = k : 1: 180 ko (k + 1) hisson mein baanto. Bahar = 180 ÷ (k + 1). 5 : 1 → 30° → n = 12. ~5 sec.
  • Andar − bahar = D: bahar = (180 − D) ÷ 2. D = 150 → 15° → n = 24. ~5 sec.
  • Andar ke kon ka jod S: n = S ÷ 180 + 2. 2520 → 14 + 2 = 16. ~5 sec.
Formula / value sheet (polygon) Andar ke kon ka jod = (n − 2) × 180. Bahar ke kon ka jod = 360° = 2π, hamesha.
Diagonals = n(n − 3) ÷ 2. Yaad: 5 → 5, 6 → 9, 8 → 20, 9 → 27, 10 → 35, 11 → 44, 12 → 54, 16 → 104.
Regular hexagon ki side = uske circumcircle ka radius (6 equilateral triangle).
Centre par saare kon ka jod = 360°, polygon kitna bhi bada ho.
Trick: Diagonals se n nikaalna ho, toh upar ki list se seedha milao. 35 → 10, 44 → 11. Na mile toh 2 × diagonals ke paas ka n(n − 3) dhoondo: 88 = 11 × 8.
