Average: shuru se, aur 36-second wale tareeke

2025–26 SSC PYQs (CGL, CHSL, CPO, MTS, Selection Post XIV, Delhi Police, GD 2026) · sabse zyada: koi aaya / koi gaya, consecutive numbers, beech wala number, do group ka average

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: 4 dost, 40 toffee

Chaar dost hain. Kisi ke paas 6 toffee, kisi ke 9, kisi ke 11, kisi ke 14. Ladai na ho, isliye sab toffee ek jagah rakh do.

  • Kul toffee = 6 + 9 + 11 + 14 = 40.
  • Ab 4 logon mein barabar baanto: 40 ÷ 4 = 10 har ek ko.

Bas yahi average hai: sab kuch mila do, phir barabar baant do. Average ka matlab hai "agar sab barabar hote, toh har ek ke paas kitna hota".

3 baatein jinse poora chapter banta hai

Idea 1 · Total sab kuch hai Total = Average × ginti. 5 logon ka average 20 hai, toh kul = 100. Average ke sawaal mein jab bhi atko, total bana lo.
Idea 2 · Average se farq (deviation) ka jod hamesha 0 Upar picture mein: −4, −1, +1, +4. Jod = 0. Yahi sabse bada shortcut hai. Bade-bade number jodne ki zaroorat nahi, bas average se kitna upar ya neeche hai, wo jodo.
Idea 3 · Total mein jo badla, wo sab mein barabar baant jaata hai Total 30 badha aur log 30 hain, toh average 1 badha. Average ka badlaav = total ka badlaav ÷ ginti. Naya aaya, koi gaya, galat number likha, sab isi se hal hote hain.

Paper mein ek sawaal ke liye lagbhag 36 second milte hain. Neeche 11 tareeke hain. Har ek rough mein, bina lambi jod ke chalta hai.

Speed method 1 · Total nikaalo (Sum = Avg × n)

Kab use karo: jab ginti aur average diye hon, aur kuch hata ya jod raha ho. Ya jab "total" hi pucha ho.

Demo · 10 numbers ka avg 50, pehle 6 ka 45. Baaki 4 ka avg? · ~10 sec Total = 500. Pehle 6 = 270. Baaki 4 = 230 → 230 ÷ 4 = 57.5.

Speed method 2 · "Maan lo average" (deviation method)

Kab use karo: jab 4–6 bade number diye hon (5456, 5972 …) ya marks jaise 72, 68, 74. Jodna mat, average se farq likho.

Soch aise: ek aasaan number maan lo (jaise 70). Har number us se kitna upar ya neeche hai, wo likho. Farq ka jod ÷ ginti = asli average ka sudhaar.

Demo · 99, 75, 52, 97, 34 ka average · ~12 sec 70 maano. Farq: +29, +5, −18, +27, −36. Jod = +61 − 54 = +7.
Average = 70 + 7/5 = 71.4.
Demo · Missing number: 72, 68, 74, 70, x ka avg 71 · ~8 sec 71 se farq: +1, −3, +3, −1 = 0. Kul farq 0 hona chahiye, toh x ka farq bhi 0 → x = 71.
Trick: "Target average" wale sawaal (5th din kitna bechein ki avg 130 ho) mein target ko hi maan lo. Jitni kami ab tak hai, utna 5th number mein extra daalo.

Speed method 3 · Koi naya aaya (teacher, next innings)

Soch aise: 19 bachchon ka avg 17. Teacher aayi, avg 18 ho gaya. Matlab har bachche ko 1 saal "mila". Ye 19 saal teacher ne diye. Aur teacher ke paas khud ka naya average 18 bhi bacha. Toh teacher = 18 + 19 = 37.

Demo · 40 workers ka avg ₹24,000. Supervisor aaya, avg ₹24,500. Supervisor ki salary? · ~6 sec Naya avg 24,500 + 40 × 500 = 24,500 + 20,000 = ₹44,500.
Demo · Batsman: 25 innings ka avg 48. 26th mein kitne run ki avg 50 ho? · ~6 sec 50 + 25 × 2 = 100. (Same formula: naya avg + purani innings × badhat.)
Trick: Ulta bhi chalta hai. Naya score diya, naya avg pucha → badhat = (score − purana avg) ÷ (nayi ginti). 9 innings avg 55, 10th mein 145 → (145 − 55)/10 = 9 → 64.

Speed method 4 · Koi chala gaya (removed number)

Soch aise: 9 numbers ka avg 56. Ek gaya, baaki 8 ka avg 54. Matlab baaki 8 mein se har ek 2 kam hai. Ye 8 × 2 = 16 "extra" jaane wala le gaya. Toh gaya hua = 56 + 16 = 72.

Formula · ~5 sec Avg gira: gaya hua = purana avg + (bache hue × giravat).
Avg badha: gaya hua = purana avg − (bache hue × badhat).
6 numbers ka avg P, ek gaya, avg P + 5 → gaya hua = P − 5 × 5 = P − 25.

Speed method 5 · Replacement (ek gaya, ek aaya)

Soch aise: ginti wahi rahi. Toh total ka badlaav = ginti × avg ka badlaav. Naya − purana = n × badlaav.

Demo · 9 logon ka avg 3.5 kg badha jab 67 kg wale ki jagah naya aaya · ~6 sec Naya = 67 + 9 × 3.5 = 67 + 31.5 = 98.5 kg.
Trick: Do gaye, do aaye? Do ka jod badlega: naye do ka jod = purane do ka jod + n × badhat. Phir ÷ 2.

Speed method 6 · Galat entry (misread / wrongly entered)

Soch aise: galti sirf ek number mein hai. Total mein (sahi − galat) ka farq aaya, aur wo sab mein baant gaya.

Formula · ~6 sec Sahi avg = galat avg + (sahi − galat) ÷ n.
40 bachche, avg 72, 39 ki jagah 93 likha: (39 − 93)/40 = −54/40 = −1.35 → 70.65.
Ulta: 39 ki jagah 89 likha, avg ½ badha → 50 ÷ n = ½ → n = 100.

Speed method 7 · Do group ka average (alligation cross)

Kab use karo: "30 clerks ka avg 12,000, 10 officers ka 20,000" type. Ya ulta: kul avg diya, ginti puchi.

Soch aise: combined average hamesha dono ke beech mein hota hai, aur jis group mein log zyada, uske paas khisakta hai. Ginti ka ratio = ulte farq ka ratio.

Combined average ka 8 second wala formula Kul avg = chhota avg + farq × (bade wale group ka hissa).
30 clerks 12,000 + 10 officers 20,000: farq 8,000, officers ka hissa 10/40 = ¼ → 12,000 + 2,000 = 14,000.
35% women 72,000, baaki men 68,000: 68,000 + 4,000 × 0.35 = 69,400.

Speed method 8 · Beech wala number (pehle k ka avg, aakhri k ka avg)

Kab use karo: "11 numbers ka avg 68, pehle 5 ka 63, aakhri 5 ka 72, 6th number?" Ye sabse zyada aane wala sawaal hai.

Soch aise: sabka farq (deviation) nikaalo. Pehle 5: 5 × (63 − 68) = −25. Aakhri 5: 5 × (72 − 68) = +20. Kul farq 0 hona chahiye. Toh bacha hua 6th number +5 laaya → 68 + 5 = 73.

Demo · 11 ka avg 61, pehle 6 ka 57, aakhri 6 ka 65. 6th? · ~8 sec 6 + 6 = 12 > 11 → overlap. Farq: 6 × (−4) = −24, 6 × (+4) = +24. Jod 0.
6th = 61 + 0 = 61.

Soch aise: 31, 33, 35, 37, 39. Beech wala 35. Baayein wale jitne kam, daayein wale utne zyada. Toh average = beech wala = (pehla + aakhri) ÷ 2. Ye har barabar-gap wali series (AP) par chalta hai.

Speed method 10 · Jode wale sawaal (A + B, B + C …)

Soch aise: teeno ka total aur do-do ke jod diye hon, toh B = (A + B) + (B + C) − (A + B + C). B do baar gina, baaki ek baar.

Demo · A, B, C ka avg 30; A, B ka 34; B, C ka 28. B? · ~8 sec Total 90. A + B = 68, B + C = 56. B = 68 + 56 − 90 = 34.
Trick: "l, m ka avg aur m, n ka avg ka farq 17" → m kat jaata hai → l − n = 2 × 17 = 34. Teeno equation diye hon toh sab jod do.

Speed method 11 · Har number par same kaam

Soch aise: sab mein 5 joda → average mein bhi 5 juda. Sab ko 2 se guna → average bhi 2 guna. Jo kaam har number par, wahi average par.

Demo · 15 numbers ka avg 28. Har ek ko double karke 5 joda · ~4 sec 28 × 2 + 5 = 61. Aadhe +4, aadhe −6 → avg mein (4 − 6)/2 = −1.

Speed method 12 · Average speed (yahan average ka jaal hai)

Soch aise: 40 km 20 km/h se (2 ghante) aur 40 km 40 km/h se (1 ghanta). Kul 80 km, 3 ghante → 26.67. Seedha (20 + 40)/2 = 30 galat, kyunki dheemi speed par zyada waqt laga.

Speed method 13 · Option se pakdo

  • Range check: combined average hamesha dono averages ke beech. 12,000 aur 20,000 ka mix 14,000 ho sakta hai, 21,000 kabhi nahi.
  • Zyada log wale ki taraf jhukta hai: 30 clerks + 10 officers → answer 12,000 ke paas, 16,000 se neeche.
  • Average speed < seedha average (alag speed par barabar doori). 20 aur 40 → 30 se kam wala option.
  • Consecutive odd/even: 4 consecutive odd ka average hamesha even hota hai (1, 3, 5, 7 → 4). 5 consecutive even ka average even hota hai.
  • Parcel, marks, din ka average sabse bade aur sabse chhote ke beech hi hoga. 1.5 … 2.7 kg ka average 4 kg nahi ho sakta.

Type 4 · Ek naya aaya: uski value ya naya average (26 Qs)

Yaad karo: naya aadmi = naya avg + (purane log × badhat). Avg gira toh minus. Naya avg pucha ho toh: badhat = (naye ki value − purana avg) ÷ nayi ginti.
