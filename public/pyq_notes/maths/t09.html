Simple Interest: shuru se, aur 36-second wale tareeke

2025–26 SSC PYQs (CGL, CHSL, CPO, MTS, Selection Post XIV, Delhi Police) · sabse zyada: seedha SI, "dugna kab", do amount se P, do hisson wala sawaal

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: ₹1000 ka udhaar

Tumne dost ko ₹1000 udhaar diye. Dost kehta hai, har saal 10% kiraya dunga. Paise ka kiraya hi interest hai.

  • Saal 1: 1000 ka 10% = 100.
  • Saal 2: phir se 1000 ka 10% = 100. Kyunki udhaar abhi bhi 1000 hi hai.
  • Saal 3, 4, 5: har baar wahi 100.

Bas yahi Simple Interest hai. Har saal ek jaisa tukda. Interest par interest kabhi nahi lagta (wo Compound Interest mein hota hai). Isliye SI ka graph ek seedhi line hai, sidhi seedhi ki tarah.

Do baatein jinse poora chapter banta hai

  • Baat 1 · % matlab "har 100 mein". 10% = har 100 rupaye par 10 rupaye. Toh 1000 par 100, 5000 par 500.
  • Baat 2 · Rate × Time = kul %. 10% har saal, 5 saal = P ka 50%. 8% har saal, 3 saal = P ka 24%. Bas ek guna, aur SI "P ka kitna %" ban gaya.
Ab naam rakh dete hain Udhaar ki rakam = P (principal). Har saal ka % = R. Saal = T. Kul interest = SI. Aakhir mein milne wala = A (amount) = P + SI.
Kitaab ka formula SI = P × R × T / 100 yahi "R × T = kul %" wali baat hai. Formula ratta mat maaro, "kul %" socho.

Paper mein ek sawaal ke liye lagbhag 36 second milte hain. Neeche 8 tareeke hain jo rough mein, haath se, jaldi chalte hain.

Speed method 1 · "Kul %" banao, phir 1% nikaalo

Kab use karo: P, R, T mein se teeno diye hain, SI ya amount pucha hai. 2025 mein sabse zyada yahi aaya.

Soch aise: P ko 100 dabbon mein baanto. Har dabba = P ka 1%. Ab "kul %" jitne dabbe utha lo.

Demo · ₹1,450 par 9%, 3 saal ka SI · ~8 sec Kul % = 9 × 3 = 27%. 1% = 14.5.
14.5 × 27 = 14.5 × 30 − 14.5 × 3 = 435 − 43.5 = ₹391.50.
Trick: 1% nikaalna = do jagah decimal khiskao. 1,450 → 14.5. 20,000 → 200. Phir "kul %" se guna. Bade numbers mein 10% pehle nikaalo, phir aadha ya double karo.

Speed method 2 · Ulta chalo: amount ya SI se P

Kab use karo: SI ya amount diya hai, P pucha hai.

  • SI diya: SI = kul % dabbe. Toh 1 dabba = SI ÷ kul %. P = 100 dabbe.
  • Amount diya: Amount = (100 + kul %) dabbe. Toh 1 dabba = Amount ÷ (100 + kul %).
Demo · 8% par 15 saal ka SI ₹58,410. P? · ~10 sec Kul % = 8 × 15 = 120%. 1% = 58,410 ÷ 120 = 486.75.
P = 100 × 486.75 = ₹48,675. (Shortcut: 120% = 6/5, toh P = 58,410 × 5/6 = 9,735 × 5.)
Demo · 10% par 2 saal mein ₹8,800 bana. P? · ~8 sec Amount = 120% = 6/5 of P. P = 8,800 × 5/6 = ₹7,333.33.

Speed method 3 · Mahine aur tareekh ko saal ka hissa banao

Kab use karo: "18 mahine", "3 saal 4 mahine", ya "15 March se 15 September".

  • Time hamesha saal mein chahiye. 6 mahine = ½, 4 mahine = ⅓, 8 mahine = ⅔, 3 mahine = ¼, 9 mahine = ¾, 18 mahine = 3/2.
  • Tareekh wale sawaal mein, agar din same hai (15 se 15), toh bas mahine gin lo. 1 April se 1 September = Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug = 5 mahine.
  • Phir kul % = R × (mahine/12). 9% for 5 mahine = 9 × 5/12 = 3.75%.
Demo · ₹4,800 par 6%, 3 saal 4 mahine · ~8 sec 3 saal 4 mahine = 3⅓ = 10/3 saal. Kul % = 6 × 10/3 = 20%.
20% of 4,800 = ₹960.

Speed method 4 · Do amount diye: farq hi SI hai

Kab use karo: "2 saal mein ₹6,600 aur 4 saal mein ₹7,200" type sawaal.

Soch aise: 2 saal se 4 saal ke beech P toh wahi hai. Jo badha wo sirf 2 saal ka SI hai. SI har saal same hota hai, toh utna hi peeche jaakar P mil jaayega.

Ek line ka formula (jab gap ajeeb ho) t1 saal mein A1, t2 saal mein A2. Toh 1 saal ka SI = (A2 − A1) ÷ (t2 − t1). P = A1 − t1 × (1 saal ka SI).
Rate seedha: R = (A2 − A1) × 100 ÷ (A1t2 − A2t1). CGL+

Speed method 5 · "x guna" = (x − 1) P ka interest

Kab use karo: "dugna", "teen guna", "5 times", "240% of itself".

Soch aise: paisa 2 guna hua matlab interest = 1P. 5 guna matlab interest = 4P (ek P toh apna tha). SI har saal same, toh interest aur saal seedhe anupaat mein chalte hain.

Demo · 2.5 guna in 5 saal. 5 guna kab? · ~8 sec 2.5 guna → interest 1.5P in 5 saal. 5 guna → interest 4P.
Saal = 5 × 4/1.5 = 5 × 8/3 = 40/3 = 13⅓ saal.
Trick: Rate = (x − 1) × 100 ÷ saal. Dugna in T saal → rate = 100/T. Yaad rakho: dugna in 8 → 12.5%, 10 → 10%, 5 → 20%, 15 → 6⅔%, 20 → 5%.

Speed method 6 · Do hisse, do rate: alligation ka cross

Kab use karo: "₹10,000 do hisson mein, 7% aur 12% par, kul interest ₹900".

Soch aise: poore 10,000 par average rate kya bana? 900/10,000 = 9%. Ab 7 aur 12 ke beech 9 kahan hai? 9, 7 se 2 door hai aur 12 se 3 door. Jo rate paas hai, uska hissa bada.

Doosra tareeka (bina cross ke, ~10 sec): maan lo saara paisa 7% par hai → 700. Asal mein 900 hai, toh 200 zyada. Ye 200 us hisse se aaya jo 12% par tha, aur har rupaye par 5% zyada deta hai. 200 = 5% of x → x = 4,000. Isko "sab ek rate par maan lo" bolo.

Speed method 7 · Barabar interest → rakam ulti rate ke anupaat mein

Kab use karo: "teeno schemes se same interest mila" ya "A ka 4 saal ka SI = B ka 6 saal ka SI".

  • Interest same hai toh rakam × rate × time same hai. Toh rakam ∝ 1 ÷ (rate × time).
  • 4%, 5%, 10% par same interest → rakam 1/4 : 1/5 : 1/10. LCM 20 se guna → 5 : 4 : 2.
  • A 4 saal, B 6 saal, same rate, same SI → A : B = 6 : 4 = 3 : 2.
Demo · ₹2,200 teen jagah 4%, 5%, 10% par, interest barabar. 5% wala? · ~10 sec 1/4 : 1/5 : 1/10 = 5 : 4 : 2 → 11 parts = 2,200 → 1 part = 200.
5% wala = 4 parts = ₹800.

Speed method 8 · Option se pakdo

  • Amount > P hamesha. SI pucha ho aur option mein amount bhi ho, toh dono milte-julte dikhte hain. Aakhri line padho.
  • Kul % se andaaza. 6840 = 127% of P. 5,400 × 1.27 = 6,858, thoda zyada. Toh P thoda kam → 5,385.83.
  • Last digit / paise. 1,450 × 27% → .50 par khatam. Jis option ka ant .50 nahi, kaat do.
  • SI linear hai. Rate 2% badha toh SI P ka (2 × T)% badha. Bas itna hi socho, poora naya SI mat nikaalo.

Kaunsa method kab? (ek nazar mein)

Formula / value sheet (yaad karo) SI = P × R × T / 100 · A = P + SI = P × (100 + RT)/100.
Dugna in T saal → R = 100/T. x guna in T saal → R = (x − 1) × 100/T.
R = T ho aur SI = P ka k% → R² = k (jaise 36% → R = 6).
Mahine → saal: 3m = ¼ · 4m = ⅓ · 6m = ½ · 8m = ⅔ · 9m = ¾ · 18m = 3/2 · 30m = 5/2.
Same sum, same rate: SI ∝ time (6 aur 9 saal → 2 : 3).
CGL+ Kisht (SI, n barabar saalana kisht x): jo rakam n saal baad deni hai = n·x + x·R·(1 + 2 + … + (n − 1))/100. Pehli kisht sabse zyada saal interest kamaati hai.

Progression: AP, GP aur series ka jod, bina lambe formula ke

2025–26 SSC PYQs (CGL, CHSL, MTS, SP-XIV, DP AWO/TPO, GD 2026) · sabse zyada: 1 + 2 + … ka jod, squares ka jod, AP ka nth term

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: seedhi ki paidiyan

Ek seedhi socho. Pehli paidi 7 cm oonchi, har agli paidi 4 cm zyada: 7, 11, 15, 19 … Har baar same cheez judti hai. Isko AP (Arithmetic Progression) kehte hain. Jo har baar judta hai wo d (common difference).

Ab ek video socho. 5 log share karte hain, har koi 3 naye logon ko bhejta hai: 5, 15, 45, 135 … Yahan har baar same cheez se guna ho raha hai. Isko GP (Geometric Progression) kehte hain. Guna wala number r (common ratio).

Teen baatein jinse poora chapter banta hai 1. Kadam gino. 15th term tak pahunchne ke liye pehle term se 14 kadam chalne hain. Toh T15 = a + 14d.
2. AP ka jod = terms ki ginti × average. Aur AP ka average = (pehla + aakhri) ÷ 2. Beech wala term bhi yahi average hai.
3. GP mein kadam = guna. T4 = a × r × r × r = a × r³.

Speed method 1 · Jod = ginti × average (Gauss ki jodi)

Kab use karo: koi bhi AP ka jod: 1 + 2 + … + 45, 3 + 6 + … + 300, salary har saal 20,000 badhti hai.

Soch aise: 1 + 2 + … + 8 likho. Neeche ulta likho: 8 + 7 + … + 1. Upar-neeche ki har jodi = 9. Aisi 8 jodiyan → 72. Ye do baar ka jod hai, toh jod = 36.

Ginti kaise nikaalo (sabse zaroori kadam) Ginti = (aakhri − pehla) ÷ d + 1. Jaise 1, 4, 7, …, 100 → (100 − 1)/3 + 1 = 34.
Multiples ho toh aur aasaan: 6 + 12 + … + 612 = 6 × (1 + 2 + … + 102). Ginti = 612 ÷ 6 = 102.
Demo · 41 + 42 + … + 70 · ~8 sec Ginti = 70 − 41 + 1 = 30. Average = (41 + 70)/2 = 55.5.
Jod = 30 × 55.5 = 1,665.

Speed method 2 · Odd numbers ka jod = square

Kab use karo: 1 + 3 + 5 + …, ya 1 + 2 + … + n + … + 2 + 1 (pahaad jaisi series).

Speed method 3 · Squares aur cubes ka jod: formula + yaad wali values

Kab use karo: 1² + 2² + … + n², ya 11² + … + 45² (beech se shuru).

  • 1² + … + n² = n(n + 1)(2n + 1) ÷ 6. Teeno numbers likho, pehle 6 ko kaato, phir guna.
  • 1³ + … + n³ = (1 + 2 + … + n)². Pehle seedha jod nikaalo, phir uska square.
  • Beech se shuru ho: (1 se bada tak) − (1 se chhote tak). 11² … 45² = Σ45 − Σ10.

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: ek thaila, 10 goli
