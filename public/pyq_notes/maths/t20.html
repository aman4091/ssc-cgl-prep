Pehle 20 second ki yaad: flat wale solids ek hi kahani hain

Ek A4 kaagaz lo. Uske upar wahi kaagaz rakhte jao, 12 cm unchi gaddi ban gayi. Gaddi ka volume = ek kaagaz ka area × unchai. Bas. Cuboid ho, triangle wala prism ho, ya hexagon wala, sab gaddi hain.

  • Volume = base area × h. Cuboid mein base area = l × b, toh V = lbh.
  • Deewaron ka area (LSA) = base ka gher (perimeter) × h. Deewar ko khol ke bichhao, ek lamba rectangle banta hai.
  • TSA = LSA + 2 × base. Upar aur neeche ke do dhakkan.
  • Pyramid = usi gaddi ka 1/3. Nukeela kar diya toh volume teesra hissa. Cone bhi cylinder ka 1/3 hai.
