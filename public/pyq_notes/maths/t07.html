Profit & Loss: shuru se, aur 36-second wale tareeke

2025–26 SSC PYQs (CGL, CHSL, CPO, SP-XIV, MTS, Delhi Police, GD 2026) · sabse zyada: seedha CP / SP / %, ratio wale, "₹x zyada mein bechta toh", articles wale, same SP par ±%

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: ₹100 ka pen

Ek dukaandaar ne pen ₹100 mein khareeda. Usne wo pen ₹120 mein bech diya.

  • Jo diya, wo hai CP (cost price, khareed ki keemat) = ₹100.
  • Jo mila, wo hai SP (selling price, bikri ki keemat) = ₹120.
  • Bacha ₹20. Ye profit hai. SP > CP toh profit, SP < CP toh loss.

Ab socho, ₹20 ka profit bada hai ya chhota? Ye depend karta hai ki kitne paise lagaye the. ₹100 lagakar ₹20 bache, matlab har ₹100 par ₹20. Isliye profit 20%.

Agar wahi pen ₹90 mein bechta, toh ₹10 ka nuksaan. ₹100 par ₹10 = 10% loss.

Teen baatein, jinse saara chapter nikalta hai 1. Profit % aur loss % hamesha CP par. Profit % = profit ÷ CP × 100. Jab tak sawaal khud "on selling price" na bole.
2. CP = 100 maan lo. 20% profit → SP = 120. 15% loss → SP = 85. Ab koi formula nahi chahiye.
3. Percentage = fraction. 20% = 1/5. Matlab profit, CP ka 1/5 hai. CP = 5 hisse, profit = 1 hissa, SP = 6 hisse. Yaani CP : SP = 5 : 6.
Ab naam rakh dete hain Profit = SP − CP. Loss = CP − SP. Profit % = (profit ÷ CP) × 100.
SP = CP × (100 + p)/100. CP = SP × 100/(100 + p). Loss mein + ki jagah −.
Ye formulas wahi "CP = 100 maan lo" wali baat hain. Ratta mat maaro, kahani yaad rakho.

Paper mein ek sawaal ke liye lagbhag 36 second milte hain. Wahan koi lamba bhaag nahi karega. Neeche 9 tareeke hain jo rough mein, jaldi chalte hain.

Speed method 1 · % ko fraction banao, CP : SP ratio likho

Kab use karo: jab % saaf fraction ban jaye: 10, 12½, 16⅔, 20, 25, 33⅓, 40, 50%. SSC ke aadhe se zyada sawaal isi se 5–10 second mein nikalte hain.

Soch aise: 25% = 1/4. CP 4 hisse, profit 1 hissa, SP 5 hisse. Toh profit mein CP : SP = 4 : 5. Loss mein CP : SP = 4 : 3. Ab SP diya ho ya CP, bas ek hissa ki value nikaalo.

Demo · ₹720 mein becha, 20% profit. CP? · ~5 sec 20% = 1/5 → CP : SP = 5 : 6. SP = 6 hisse = 720 → 1 hissa = 120.
CP = 5 hisse = ₹600.
Demo · ₹750 mein becha, 25% loss. CP? · ~5 sec 25% = 1/4 → loss mein CP : SP = 4 : 3. 3 hisse = 750 → 1 hissa = 250.
CP = 4 hisse = ₹1,000.
Trick: "Neeche wala CP, upar-neeche ka jod SP." 1/5 profit → 5 : (5 + 1) = 5 : 6. 1/5 loss → 5 : (5 − 1) = 5 : 4.

Speed method 2 · Pehle 1% nikaalo (ganda % ho toh)

Kab use karo: jab % saaf fraction na bane: 8%, 12%, 15%, 18%, 28%.

Soch aise: 1% = number ÷ 100, bas do digit khiska do. 2500 ka 1% = 25. Phir 12% = 25 × 12 = 300. 10% aur 5% toh aur bhi aasaan hain: 10% = ek digit khiskao, 5% = uska aadha.

Demo · CP ₹2,500, 12% profit. SP? · ~6 sec 1% = 25 → 12% = 300. SP = 2,500 + 300 = ₹2,800.
Demo · CP − SP = ₹420, loss 16%. CP? · ~8 sec Farq hi loss hai. 16% = 420 → 4% = 105 → 100% = 105 × 25 = ₹2,625.
Trick: "% = ₹" wala sawaal aaye (profit ₹81 aur profit 18%), toh seedha bolo: 18% → 81, toh 2% → 9, toh 100% → 450. Har baar chhote % tak aao, phir 100% tak.

Speed method 3 · Gap method ("₹x zyada mein bechta toh")

Kab use karo: "10% loss par becha. ₹45 zyada mein bechta toh 5% profit hota. CP?" Ye type is bank mein 14 baar aaya.

Soch aise: CP wahi hai, sirf SP badla. −10% se +5% tak ka safar = 15% ka gap. Ye 15% hi wo ₹45 hai. Bas.

Demo · 25% profit; ₹60 zyada mein bechta toh 40%. CP? · ~5 sec Dono profit, toh gap = 40 − 25 = 15%. 15% = 60 → 5% = 20 → CP = ₹400.

Jab CP bhi badle ("10% kam mein khareedta aur ₹18 kam mein bechta"): CP = 100 maan lo. Purana SP aur naya SP dono "hisson" mein likho. Unka farq = diye hue ₹. Type 9 mein poora dikhaya hai.

Speed method 4 · Do SP, ek CP (barabar ya "k guna" profit-loss)

Kab use karo: "₹1,100 par jitna profit, ₹900 par utna hi loss" ya "₹2,000 par profit, ₹1,200 par loss ka 19 guna".

Soch aise: CP dono SP ke beech mein khada hai. Profit = upar wale SP tak ki doori. Loss = neeche wale SP tak ki doori. Barabar ho toh CP theek beech mein. 19 guna ho toh gap ko 1 + 19 = 20 hisson mein kaato; CP neeche se 1 hissa upar.

Trick: "k guna" mein CP = chhota SP + gap ÷ (k + 1). 9 guna, SP 1,200 aur 1,000 → gap 200 ÷ 10 = 20 → CP = 1,020.

Speed method 5 · Articles wale sawaal (n becha, x ka CP/SP kamaya)

Kab use karo: "5 kursi bech kar 2 kursi ka CP kamaya", "15 bech kar 3 ka SP kamaya", "15 ka SP = 20 ka CP".

Soch aise: profit hamesha CP par nikaalna hai. Toh jo bhi diya ho, use "itne articles ka CP" mein badlo.

Speed method 6 · Ratio guna karo (chain, jhootha baat, "10 ke daam mein 8")

Kab use karo: jab ek ke baad ek do-teen % lagen. A ne B ko 20% par becha, B ne C ko 10% loss par. Ya dukaandaar jhootha baat use kare aur upar se % bhi le.

Soch aise: har % ko CP : SP ratio banao, phir saare ratio guna kar do. Do % ho toh a + b + ab/100 bhi chalta hai.

Demo · A → B 20% profit, B → C 10% loss, C → D 5% profit. D ne ₹1,134 diye. Pehla CP? · ~12 sec 5 : 6, 10 : 9, 20 : 21. Guna: 5 × 10 × 20 = 1000 aur 6 × 9 × 21 = 1134.
1134 hisse = 1,134 → 1 hissa = 1 → CP = ₹1,000.
Trick: "10 ke daam mein 8 nahi, 8 ke daam mein 10" yaani 10 cheez ka CP = 8 ka MP. Toh CP 8, SP 10 (MP par). Discount ho toh SP ko us % se ghatao: 2% off → 9.8. Profit = 1.8/8 = 22.5%.

Speed method 7 · Same SP, ek par +x% ek par −x%

Kab use karo: "Do cheezein ₹500–500 mein becheen. Ek par 25% profit, doosri par 25% loss."

Soch aise: lagta hai hisaab barabar. Par nahi. Loss wali cheez ka CP bada tha (SP 500 = CP ka 75%). Profit wali ka CP chhota tha (SP 500 = CP ka 125%). Bade CP par 25% zyada ₹ hote hain. Isliye hamesha loss, aur wo loss = x²/100 %.

Demo · ₹960–960 mein do cheezein, ±20%. Kul? · ~3 sec Loss % = 20 × 20 ÷ 100 = 4% loss. ₹ mein chahiye toh: CP = 960 × 5/6 + 960 × 5/4 = 800 + 1,200 = 2,000 → loss 80.

Dhyaan: ye rule tabhi jab dono % barabar hon aur dono SP barabar. 15% aur 10% ho toh har cheez ka CP alag nikaalo.

Speed method 8 · Alligation cross (mixture aur "kuch hissa x% par")

Kab use karo: do daam ka maal mila diya, ya maal ka ek hissa ek % par aur baaki doosre % par becha, aur kul % pucha ya diya hai.

Soch aise: kul % hamesha dono % ke beech aata hai. Jis % ke paas kul % hai, uska hissa bada. Cross se hisse seedhe nikalte hain: doori ulti likho.

Demo · 60 pen 20% profit par, 40 pen 10% loss par. Kul? · ~6 sec Hisson ka average: (60 × 20 − 40 × 10) ÷ 100 = (1,200 − 400) ÷ 100 = 8% profit.
Ye tabhi seedha chalta hai jab har pen ka CP barabar ho (yahan ₹10).

Speed method 9 · Choti chaabiyan (profit on SP, dono badle, option check)

  • Profit SP par diya: SP = 100 maan lo. 25% on SP → profit 25, CP 75 → CP par 25/75 = 33.33%. Rule: p/(100 − p). Loss on SP: p/(100 + p). 30% → 30/130 = 23.08%.
  • CP aur SP dono same % se badle ya ghate: ratio wahi rehta hai, toh profit % bhi wahi. 30% profit, dono +40% → abhi bhi 30%.
  • "x% of CP = y% of SP": CP : SP = y : x (ulta). 30% CP = 25% SP → CP : SP = 25 : 30 = 5 : 6 → 20% profit.
  • Option check: CP nikaalna ho aur sawaal ulta-pulta ho, toh option ko seedha daal kar dekho. Ek option 10 second mein check hota hai.
  • Andaaza: profit % hamesha (profit ÷ CP) hai, toh agar SP par divide kiya to answer chhota aayega. 50/1000 = 5% aata hai par asli 50/950 = 5.26%. "Thoda zyada" wala option.
