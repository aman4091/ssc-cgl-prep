Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: 2 + 3 × 4

Do dost ek hi sawaal karte hain: 2 + 3 × 4.

  • Ramu left se chalta hai: 2 + 3 = 5, phir 5 × 4 = 20.
  • Shyam pehle guna karta hai: 3 × 4 = 12, phir 2 + 12 = 14.

Sahi kaun? Shyam. Socho: "2 rupaye ka pen, aur 3 notebook 4-4 rupaye ki." Bill = 2 + 12 = 14. Guna ek "packet" hai. Pehle packet bandho, phir jodo.

Bas yahi poora chapter hai. Simplification ka matlab: ek lambi line ko sahi kram mein todna, aur har number ko uske sabse aasaan roop mein badalna.

Saari baat 3 idea se nikalti hai 1. Kram (VBODMAS): bar → bracket → of → ÷ × → + −. Kram galat, answer galat.
2. Ek number, kai roop: 0.125 = 1/8 = 12.5%. 0.333… = 1/3. Jo roop aasaan ho, wahi lo.
3. Pattern pehchano, guna mat karo: 4.1² + 1.9² + 2 × 4.1 × 1.9 dekh kar 4.1 × 4.1 mat karo. Ye (4.1 + 1.9)² = 6² = 36 hai.
"of" ka matlab "of" = guna. ¾ of 64 = 48. GD 2026 ke papers mein "28 of 2" jaisa likha aata hai. Wo bas 28 × 2 = 56 hai. "18 of 1" = 18.

Ab asli baat. Ek sawaal ke liye lagbhag 30–36 second milte hain. Neeche 9 tareeke hain jo haath se, rough mein, jaldi chalte hain.

Speed method 4 · Square root: last digit + paas wala square

Kab use karo: √7396, √6561, √46656, √0.0009 jaise sawaal. Bina lambi division ke.

Soch aise: 6 par khatam hone wala square sirf 4 ya 6 se banta hai (4² = 16, 6² = 36). Toh root ka last digit 2 options tak simat jaata hai. Baaki hissa "kaun se do squares ke beech" se milta hai.

  • Decimal ka root: point ke baad digits ka aadha. √0.0009 = 0.03 (4 digit → 2 digit). √0.16 = 0.4. √2070.25 = 45.5.
  • 5 par khatam: 45² = 4 × 5 = 20, peeche 25 → 2025. Ulta: √2025 = 45.
  • 100 ke paas: 9999² = 99980001 (10000 − 1 ka square = 1000 0000 − 2 0000 + 1). 101² = 10201.

Speed method 7 · Rationalise: neeche ka "jodidaar" (conjugate)
