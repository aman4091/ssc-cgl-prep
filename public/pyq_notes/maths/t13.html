Time and Work: shuru se, aur 36-second wale tareeke

2025–26 SSC PYQs (CGL, CHSL, CPO, Selection Post XIV, MTS, Delhi Police, GD 2026) · sabse zyada: "saath kitne din", "ek ka time nikaalo", "beech mein koi chala gaya", man-days

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: 60 eentein

Maan lo ek deewar mein 60 eentein lagni hain. Ramesh akela 20 din mein lagata hai. Suresh akela 30 din mein.

  • Ramesh roz kitni eent lagata hai? 60 ÷ 20 = 3 eent roz.
  • Suresh roz? 60 ÷ 30 = 2 eent roz.
  • Dono saath lagayen toh roz 3 + 2 = 5 eent. Toh 60 eent lagengi 60 ÷ 5 = 12 din mein.

Bas. Poora chapter isi ek kahani mein hai. Sawaal mein deewar ki eentein nahi di hoti, sirf din diye hote hain. Toh eentein hum khud maan lete hain. Kitni? Itni ki sabke din usmein poore-poore baith jaayen. Yaani dino ka LCM. 20 aur 30 ka LCM = 60.

Teen baatein jinse saara chapter banta hai

Ye teen line ratt lo 1. Kaam = roz ka kaam × din. (60 = 3 × 20.) Isi ko efficiency × time bolte hain.
2. Kaam same ho toh efficiency aur time ulte chalte hain. 2 guna tez → aadha time. Time ka ratio 2 : 3 → efficiency 3 : 2.
3. Saath kaam karo toh roz ka kaam JUDTA hai, din nahi. 3 + 2 = 5 roz. 20 + 30 din jodna bilkul galat hai.

Kitaab ye sab 1/20 + 1/30 = 1/12 likh kar karti hai. Wahi baat hai, par fraction jodne mein 15 second jaate hain. Units mein 3 second. Isliye neeche har jagah units.

Speed method 1 · LCM units (sabse zaroori)

Kab use karo: jab bhi alag-alag logon ke "itne din" diye hon. 90% sawaal yahi hain.

  • Step 1: sab dino ka LCM = kul kaam (units).
  • Step 2: har aadmi ka roz ka kaam = LCM ÷ uske din.
  • Step 3: jo pucha hai, units mein jodo-ghatao. Aakhir mein units ÷ roz ka kaam = din.
Demo · A 12, B 15, C 20 din. Teeno saath? · ~10 sec LCM 60 → A 5, B 4, C 3 → roz 12 → 60 ÷ 12 = 5 din.
Demo · A+B 10 din, B akela 15. A akela? · ~8 sec LCM 30 → (A+B) 3, B 2 → A = 3 − 2 = 1 → 30 ÷ 1 = 30 din.
Trick: "Din ka LCM = kaam, kaam ÷ din = speed." Kisi ek ka time nikaalna ho toh speeds ghatao, din kabhi mat ghatao.

Speed method 2 · Do logon ka "guna ÷ jod"

Kab use karo: sirf do log, aur numbers chhote. LCM likhne ki bhi zaroorat nahi.

  • Saath ka time = a × b ÷ (a + b). 12 aur 8 → 96 ÷ 20 = 4.8 din.
  • Ek ka time (saath t, doosra a) = a × t ÷ (a − t). Saath 10, B 15 → 150 ÷ 5 = 30 din.
  • Teen log? Ye formula mat lagao. Method 1 (LCM) hi chalao.
Demo · A 12, B 36. Saath? · ~5 sec 12 × 36 ÷ 48 = 432 ÷ 48 = 9 din. (Ya: 36 = 3 × 12, toh A = 3 B. Kul 4 B ki speed → 36 ÷ 4 = 9.)
Trick: saath ka time hamesha sabse tez aadmi se kam hota hai. A 12, B 36 → answer 12 se neeche hi hoga. Iske upar wale options seedha kaato.

Speed method 4 · Man-days (aadmi × din × ghante = kaam)

Kab use karo: "15 aadmi 20 din", "9 ghante roz", "khana 60 din chalega", "6 aadmi chale gaye".

Soch aise: 1 aadmi 1 din = 1 unit. 12 aadmi 20 din = 240 units. Kaam ka size badla nahi, toh aadmi kam honge toh din badhenge.

Formula (ek hi): M₁ × D₁ × H₁ ÷ W₁ = M₂ × D₂ × H₂ ÷ W₂ M = aadmi, D = din, H = roz ke ghante, W = kitna kaam. Jo nahi diya, use hata do.
Demo · 40 aadmi, 9 ghante, 50 din. 6 ghante, 75 din mein kitne aadmi? · ~10 sec 40 × 9 × 50 = M × 6 × 75. Kaato: 9 × 50 = 450, 6 × 75 = 450 → barabar → M = 40.

Speed method 5 · Bacha kaam (koi gaya, koi aaya)

Kab use karo: "4 din baad A chala gaya", "B baad mein juda", "baaki C ne 5 din mein kiya".

  • Units mein kaam lo. Jitne din jo team chali, uska kaam ghata do.
  • Bacha kaam ÷ nayi team ki speed = aur din.
  • Ulta sawaal (kisi ka time pucha): bacha kaam ÷ din = nayi team ki speed → usmein se jaane-pehchaane logon ki speed ghatao.
Demo · A 12, B 18. Saath shuru, 4 din baad B gaya. A aur kitne din? · ~10 sec LCM 36 → A 3, B 2. 4 din × 5 = 20. Bacha 16 → 16 ÷ 3 = 5⅓ din.
Trick (ulta sawaal): "A+B 30 din. A 16 din, phir B ne 44 din." B ke 44 din ko todo: 16 din A ke saath + 28 din akela. 16 din saath = 16/30. Baaki 14/30 B ne 28 din mein → B poora 60 din. Jo aadmi poore time raha, uske din pehle gino.

Speed method 6 · Jodiyan (A+B, B+C, C+A) → sab jodo, aadha karo

Kab use karo: teen jodiyon ka time diya ho.

Demo · A+B 8, B+C 12, C+A 16. A akela? · ~15 sec LCM 48 → 6, 4, 3 → jod 13 → teeno 6.5. A = 6.5 − (B+C) 4 = 2.5 → 48 ÷ 2.5 = 19.2 din.

Speed method 7 · Baari-baari aur chakkar (cycle)

Kab use karo: "alternate days", "har teesre din B madad karta hai", "B har teesre din gayab".

  • Ek chakkar (2 din, 3 din …) ka kul kaam nikaalo.
  • Poore chakkar chalao jab tak kaam lagbhag khatam na ho. Phir bacha hua din-ba-din bharo.
  • Dhyaan: aakhri chakkar se pehle ruko. Jo pehle aata hai (A) wo akele kaam khatam kar sakta hai.

Speed method 8 · Paise (wages) = kiye gaye kaam ke hisaab se

  • Sab saath shuru-khatam karein → paise efficiency ke ratio mein. A 25, B 30 → speed 6 : 5.
  • Alag din kaam kiya → paise speed × din ke ratio mein. A 6 din × 3, B 4 din × 5 → 18 : 20.
  • C ka hissa (teeno t din mein) → C ka kaam = kul − (A + B) ka kaam. Hissa = C units ÷ kul units.
Demo · A 12, B 18, ₹7,200. C ke saath 5 din. C ko? · ~12 sec LCM 180 → A 15, B 10. Teeno 180 ÷ 5 = 36 → C = 11. C ka hissa 11/36 × 7,200 = 11 × 200 = ₹2,200.

Speed method 9 · Option se pakdo

  • Saath ka time < sabse tez akele ka time. Aur > (sabse tez ka time ÷ logon ki ginti).
  • Ek ka time nikala? Wo "saath" wale time se zyada hi hoga.
  • Fraction answer: denominator = (speeds ka jod). 12, 15, 20 → speed jod 12 → answer x/12 jaisa. 72/13 dikhe toh speed jod 13 tha.
  • Ghante-minute wale options: 1/7 din = 24/7 ghante ≈ 3 ghante 26 min. 0.2 din = 4.8 ghante = 4 ghante 48 min.

Kaunsa method kab? (ek nazar mein)

Formula / values yaad karne wale Saath (2 log) = ab/(a + b) · ek ka time = at/(a − t) · teen log: LCM units.
k guna tez, saath t → dheema (k + 1)t, tez (k + 1)t/k.
Jodiyan: (A+B) + (B+C) + (C+A) = 2(A+B+C).
M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂ · khana: log × din = fixed.
1 din = 24 ghante: ⅓ din = 8 ghante, ⅙ = 4 ghante, 1/7 ≈ 3 ghante 26 min, 0.2 = 4 ghante 48 min.
CGL+ Pipe aur cistern bhi yahi chapter hai: bharne wala nal = kaam karne wala (+), khaali karne wala = todne wala (−).
