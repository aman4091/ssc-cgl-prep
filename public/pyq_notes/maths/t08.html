Discount: shuru se, aur 36-second wale tareeke

2025–26 SSC PYQs (CGL, CHSL, CPO, MTS, Selection Post XIV, Delhi Police, GD 2026) · sabse zyada: seedha SP, successive discount, markup + discount se profit, "MP kitna tha"

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: dukaan ki teen keemat

Ek dukaandaar ne shirt ₹100 mein khareedi. Ye uski khareed hai. Isko CP (cost price) bolte hain.

Ab wo shirt par tag lagata hai: ₹140. Ye tag wala daam hai. Isko MP (marked price, list price, printed price, tag price) bolte hain. Sab ek hi cheez hai.

Customer aaya. Dukaandaar bola, "20% chhoot de deta hoon." 140 ka 20% = 28. Customer ne diye 140 − 28 = ₹112. Ye asli bikri hai. Isko SP (selling price) bolte hain.

  • Jo 28 kam kiye, wo discount hai. Discount hamesha MP par lagta hai.
  • Dukaandaar ko mila 112, laga tha 100. Toh 12 ka faayda. Profit hamesha CP par ginte hain: 12%.
  • MP ko CP se 40% upar rakha, ise markup kehte hain. Markup bhi CP par ginte hain.
Poora chapter in 3 baaton se banta hai 1. Discount MP par, profit aur markup CP par. Galti yahin hoti hai.
2. % = fraction. 20% chhoot = 1/5 kata = 4/5 bacha. Har sawaal "bacha hua hissa" se chalta hai.
3. SP beech ki kadi hai. Ek taraf SP = MP ka (100 − d)%, doosri taraf SP = CP ka (100 + p)%. Dono ko barabar rakho, sawaal khatam.

Paper mein ek sawaal ke liye lagbhag 36 second milte hain. Neeche 8 tareeke hain jo haath se, rough mein, jaldi chalte hain.

Speed method 3 · Successive discount: a + b − ab/100

Kab use karo: "20% aur phir 10%" ya "do lagataar chhoot". Single equivalent, final SP, ya chhoot ki rakam.

Soch aise: ₹100 ki cheez. Pehle 20% kata, 80 bache. Ab doosri chhoot 80 par lagti hai, 100 par nahi. 80 ka 10% = 8. Toh kul kata 20 + 8 = 28, 30 nahi.

Speed method 5 · MP : CP = (100 + p) : (100 − d)

Kab use karo: discount % aur profit % dono diye. MP, CP ya markup % pucha.

Soch aise: SP ek hi hai. MP ka 80% = SP, aur CP ka 110% = SP. Toh MP × 80 = CP × 110. Jo zyada guna ho raha hai, wo chhota hai. Isliye MP : CP = 110 : 80. Ulta number ulti taraf.

Demo · CP ₹800, profit 10%, chhoot 12%. MP? · ~8 sec MP : CP = 110 : 88 = 5 : 4. CP = 4 parts = 800 → MP = 5 parts = ₹1,000.

Speed method 6 · D, SP, MP ke parts

Kab use karo: "discount MP ka 2/17 hai", "SP discount se 120% zyada hai", "MP discount ka 140% hai" jaise sawaal.

Soch aise: hamesha MP = D + SP. Bas jo diya hai use parts mein likho, teesra jod ya ghata kar nikaalo.

Trick: "X% of" aur "X% more than" alag hain. MP, D ka 140% → MP = 140. MP, D se 140% zyada → MP = 240. Pehle yahi padho.

Speed method 7 · Offers: buy x get y, flat ₹ off, GST

Soch aise: dukaandaar kitni cheezein de raha hai, aur kitni ka paisa le raha hai. Free wali cheez hi discount hai.

  • Flat ₹ off: % = off ÷ MP. ₹150 off on ₹1,200 = 1/8 = 12.5%.
  • GST / VAT / tax: chhoot ke baad wale daam par lagta hai. ₹2,000, 20% off, 18% GST → 1,600 × 1.18 = 1,888.
  • Cashback / extra ₹ off: % wali chhoot ke baad seedha ghatao. Shart ("₹15,000 se upar ho toh") hamesha chhoot ke baad wale bill par check karo.

Speed method 8 · Option se pakdo

  • SP < MP hamesha. MP pucha hai toh SP se chhota option kaat do. SP pucha hai toh MP se bada kaat do.
  • Successive chhoot < seedha jod. 20 + 10 ka jawab 30% se thoda kam (28%). 30% wala option jaal hai.
  • Last digit: 1,500 × 0.9 × 0.95 = 1,282.5. Options mein 1,283 hi .5 ke paas hai.
  • Andaaza: 14,490 = 1.53 × CP. 1.53 × 9,500 = 14,535, thoda zyada. Toh CP 9,500 se thoda kam → 9,471.
