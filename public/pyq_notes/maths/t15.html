2025–26 SSC PYQs (CGL, CHSL, CPO, Selection Post XIV, Delhi Police) · sabse zyada: ek ratio se baaki ratio, "jod = 90°" wale sawaal, radian, aur sec + tan / sin + cos / tan + cot ki jodi

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: deewar aur seedhi

Ek seedhi deewar se tiki hai. Seedhi ki lambai 5 m hai. Uska paanv deewar se 4 m door hai. Toh seedhi deewar par 3 m ki oonchai tak pahunchti hai. (3² + 4² = 5².)

  • Ab seedhi 10 m ki le lo, wahi jhukaav rakho. Paanv 8 m door, oonchai 6 m. Har bhuja dugni ho gayi.
  • Par oonchai ÷ seedhi dekho: 3/5, aur 6/10. Dono baar 0.6. Bhujaen badlin, ratio nahi badla.
  • Ratio tab badalta hai jab jhukaav, yaani angle, badalta hai. Toh har angle ka apna ek fixed ratio hai.

Bas yahi trigonometry hai: right triangle mein angle aur bhujaon ke ratio ka rishta. Inhi ratios ke naam sin, cos, tan hain.

Teen basic ideas, jinse saare sawaal bante hain

  • Idea 1 · Ratio = bhuja ÷ bhuja. Koi bhi ek ratio mila (jaise sin = 3/5) toh triangle ki do bhujaen mil gayin. Teesri Pythagoras se. Phir koi bhi ratio padh lo.
  • Idea 2 · Pythagoras ko ratio mein likho. P² + B² = H². Poore ko H² se bhaag do: sin² + cos² = 1. B² se bhaag do: tan² + 1 = sec². P² se bhaag do: cot² + 1 = cosec². Teeno ek hi baat hain.
  • Idea 3 · Doosra kona. Right triangle ke baaki do angle mil kar 90° hote hain. Doosre kone (90° − θ) se dekho toh P aur B aapas mein badal jaate hain. Isliye sin θ = cos(90° − θ), tan θ = cot(90° − θ), sec θ = cosec(90° − θ).
Ab naam rakh dete hain sin, cos, tan = mool ratio. cosec, sec, cot = unke ulte (cosec = 1/sin, sec = 1/cos, cot = 1/tan).
sin–cos, tan–cot, sec–cosec = co-ratio jodiyan (90° wala rishta).
Angle degree mein bhi naapte hain aur radian mein bhi: π radian = 180°.

Ab asli baat. Paper mein ek sawaal ko lagbhag 36 second milte hain. Trig mein formula se lambi algebra karne ka time nahi hai. Neeche 9 tareeke hain. Har PYQ inhi mein se kisi se 5–20 second mein nikalta hai.

Kab use karo: jab 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90° ki value chahiye, ya ratio dekh kar angle pehchanna ho (sec = 2 matlab 60°).

Soch aise: ginti likho 0, 1, 2, 3, 4. Har ek ka √ lo aur 2 se bhaag do. Ye sin ki line hai. Cos ki line wahi hai, ulti. Tan = sin ÷ cos.

Speed method 8 · "Angle maan lo" (θ = 45°, 30°, 60°)

Kab use karo: "simplify karo" ya options mein poore expression (sin x, sec²x, 1 − tan²x…). Jo identity har angle par sach hai, wo 45° par bhi sach hogi.

Speed method 9 · a sin + b cos ki jodi → a² + b²
