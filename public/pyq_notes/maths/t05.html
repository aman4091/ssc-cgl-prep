Ratio & Proportion: "parts" mein socho, 10 second mein khatam

2025–26 SSC PYQs (CGL, CHSL, CPO, Selection Post XIV, MTS, Delhi Police, GD 2026) · sabse zyada: total/farq se hissa, A:B aur B:C ko jodna, "6 saal baad ratio"

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: class ke 30 bachche

Ek class mein 18 ladke aur 12 ladkiyan hain. Dono ko 6 se kaato: 3 aur 2. Toh ladke : ladkiyan = 3 : 2.

Iska matlab kya hai? Har 3 ladkon ke saath 2 ladkiyan. Bas itna. Ratio asli ginti nahi batata. Wo sirf batata hai ki ek cheez doosri ki kitne guna hai.

  • 3 : 2 wali class mein 30 bachche ho sakte hain (18 + 12).
  • 50 bhi ho sakte hain (30 + 20).
  • Bas kul hamesha 5 ke multiple mein hoga. Kyunki ek "set" mein 3 + 2 = 5 bachche hain.

Is "set" ke har bachche ko hum 1 part bolenge. 3 : 2 matlab ladke 3 parts, ladkiyan 2 parts, kul 5 parts. Ek part ki value mil gayi, toh sab mil gaya. Poora chapter isi ek line par chalta hai.

Teen baatein jinse poora chapter banta hai

  • Ratio = parts. A : B = 3 : 2 → A = 3 parts, B = 2 parts. Kul 5 parts, farq 1 part.
  • Ratio = fraction. A : B = 3 : 2 → A, kul ka 3/5 hai. A = (3/2) × B. Aur 7A = 5B ka matlab A : B = 5 : 7 (ulta, kyunki chhote A ko zyada baar lena padta hai).
  • Proportion = do ratio barabar. 2 : 3 = 4 : 6. Bahar wale (2, 6) ka guna = andar wale (3, 4) ka guna = 12. Isi se mean, third, fourth proportional nikalte hain.
Ab naam rakh dete hain a : b mein a = antecedent (pehla), b = consequent (doosra).
a : b = c : d mein a aur d = extremes (bahar wale), b aur c = means (andar wale).
Ratio ki koi unit nahi hoti. Par dono cheezein ek hi unit mein honi chahiye: 2 ghante : 90 minute = 120 : 90 = 4 : 3.

Paper mein ek sawaal ko lagbhag 36 second milte hain. Ratio ke zyada sawaal 10–15 second ke hain, agar tum "x maan lo, equation banao" wala lamba raasta chhod do. Neeche 10 tareeke hain.

Speed method 1 · Parts method (sabse zaroori)

Kab use karo: ratio diya hai, aur saath mein kul, ya farq, ya kisi ek ka hissa diya hai.

Soch aise: jo number diya hai, wo kitne parts ka hai? Use utne se baanto. 1 part mil gaya. Ab jo poocha hai, uske parts se guna karo.

Demo · ₹4,800 ko 3 : 4 : 5 mein baanto. B, A se kitna zyada? · ~8 sec Kul = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 parts = 4,800 → 1 part = 400.
B − A = 4 − 3 = 1 part = ₹400. B aur A ko alag alag nikaalne ki zaroorat hi nahi.
Demo · Do number 6 : 5 mein, farq 11. Number? · ~5 sec Farq = 6 − 5 = 1 part = 11. Number = 6 × 11 aur 5 × 11 = 66 aur 55.
Trick: "Diya hua ÷ uske parts × maange hue parts." Bas yahi. Average diya ho toh pehle kul banao: 3 numbers ka average 100 → kul 300.

Speed method 2 · Do ratio jodna (beech wala barabar karo)

Kab use karo: A : B aur B : C alag alag diye hain. Poocha hai A : B : C ya A : C.

Soch aise: B dono ratio mein hai, par dono mein B ki value alag hai (3 aur 4). Jab tak B ek jaisa nahi hoga, jod nahi sakte. Toh B ko dono ka LCM bana do.

Demo · P:Q = 6:11, Q:R = 5:4, R:S = 9:7. P:Q:R:S? · ~20 sec Pehle do: Q ko 55 banao → 30 : 55 : 44.
Ab R ko 44 aur 9 ka LCM banao: pehle wale ko × 9, R:S ko × 44 → 270 : 495 : 396 : 308.
Option check fast: 270 : 495 ko 45 se kaato = 6 : 11 ✔. Bas.
Trick: options mein ratio diya ho toh banana zaroori nahi. Har option ke pehle do number kaat kar 6 : 11 bante hain ya nahi, dekho. 5 second.

Speed method 3 · "3X = 4Y = 7Z" (ulta kar do)

Kab use karo: sawaal mein "guna barabar" diya hai: 7A = 5B, ya 3X = 4Y = 7Z, ya "A ka 4/11 = B ka 3/5".

Soch aise: 7A = 5B. Jiske saath bada number laga hai, wo khud chhota hoga. Toh A : B = 5 : 7 (number ulte jagah).

  • Teen ho toh: 3X = 4Y = 7Z = LCM (84) maan lo. X = 28, Y = 21, Z = 12. Toh 28 : 21 : 12.
  • Aasaan ho toh: 8P = 4Q = 2R = 8 maan lo → P = 1, Q = 2, R = 4.
  • Fraction ho toh: A ka 4/11 = B ka 3/5. Cross guna: A : B = 3 × 11 : 5 × 4 = 33 : 20.
Demo · ₹427 ko aise baanto ki 3X = 4Y = 7Z. Z? · ~12 sec LCM 84 → X : Y : Z = 28 : 21 : 12. Kul 61 parts = 427 → 1 part = 7.
Z = 12 × 7 = ₹84.

Speed method 4 · Fraction wala ratio → LCM se guna

Kab use karo: ratio fraction mein hai: 1/4 : 1/6 : 1/8, ya 2/5 : 3/10 : 1/2.

Soch aise: ratio ke har term ko ek hi number se guna karo, ratio nahi badalta. Toh neeche wale numbers (4, 6, 8) ka LCM (24) le kar sabko guna kar do. Fraction gaayab.

Demo · ₹650 ko 1/4 : 1/6 : 1/8 mein. Pehla hissa? · ~10 sec × 24 → 6 : 4 : 3. Kul 13 parts = 650 → 1 part = 50. Pehla = 6 × 50 = ₹300.

Speed method 5 · Jo nahi badla, use pakdo

Kab use karo: ek hi cheez badli hai (sirf ladkiyan aayi, sirf neeli goliyan nikli). Doosri wahi ki wahi hai.

Soch aise: ladke 7 : 5 mein the. 20 ladkiyan aayi. Ab 7 : 6. Ladke nahi badle, aur dono ratio mein ladke 7 parts hain. Toh part ka size bhi nahi badla. Ladkiyan 5 parts se 6 parts hui. Wo 1 extra part hi 20 ladkiyan hain.

Agar "na badalne wala" dono ratio mein alag ho? Laal : neeli = 5 : 3. 15 neeli nikaali, ab 5 : 2. Laal dono mein 5 hai, toh seedha: neeli 3 → 2 parts, 1 part = 15. Laal = 75.
Laal : neeli : hari = 3 : 4 : 6, baad mein 4 : 5 : 7 (teeno badle) → yahan ye method nahi chalta. Option check karo (Type 6 dekho).

Speed method 6 · Umar wale sawaal: farq kabhi nahi badalta

Kab use karo: dono mein same number juda: "6 saal baad", "dono ne ₹3000 aur bachaye", "dono mein 9 joda".

Soch aise: A aur B ki umar mein jo farq aaj hai, wahi 6 saal baad bhi hoga. Dono ek saath bade hote hain. Toh dono ratio mein farq wale parts barabar kar do. Phir jo parts badhe, wo hi "6 saal" hain.

Demo · Do number 5 : 8 mein. Dono mein 12 joda, ratio 11 : 17. Sum? · ~15 sec Farq: 8 − 5 = 3 aur 17 − 11 = 6. Barabar karo: 5 : 8 ko × 2 = 10 : 16 (farq 6).
Ab 10 → 11, yaani 1 part badha = 12. Toh 1 part = 12.
Numbers = 10 parts aur 16 parts = 120 aur 192 (120 : 192 = 5 : 8 ✔). Sum = 312.
Trick: "Farq barabar, phir badhat = joda hua number." 5 : 7 → 7 : 9 (6 saal baad): farq 2 aur 2, badhat 2 parts = 6 → 1 part = 3 → B = 7 × 3 = 21.

Speed method 7 · Ratio par % badhat

Kab use karo: "dono 10% aur 25% badhe, naya ratio?" ya "kharch 2 : 4 : 4, alag alag % badle, kul kitna badla?"

  • Naya ratio: har term ko (100 + badhat) se guna. 3 : 2, +10% aur +25% → 3 × 110 : 2 × 125 = 330 : 250 = 33 : 25. ~8 sec.
  • Kul % badlaav: har hisse ke parts × uska %, sab jodo, kul parts se baanto. 2 : 4 : 4 aur −10, +5, −15 → (−20 + 20 − 60) ÷ 10 = −6%. ~10 sec.
Trick: % se pehle hi parts ko 10 ka multiple bana lo (5 : 3 : 2 ka kul 10). Tab jawab seedha % mein aata hai.

Speed method 8 · "Maan lo" (value daal do)

Kab use karo: a : b diya hai, aur (6a + 2b) : (6a − 2b) jaisa kuch poocha hai.

Soch aise: a : b = 5 : 3 hai toh a = 5, b = 3 hi le lo. Seedha daalo: (30 + 6) : (30 − 6) = 36 : 24 = 3 : 2. Koi algebra nahi.

  • (x + y) : (x − y) = 5 : 2 diya ho: x = (5 + 2) = 7, y = (5 − 2) = 3. Yaani x : y = 7 : 3. Phir maan lo x = 7, y = 3.
  • (x + y) : (y + z) : (z + x) = 7 : 9 : 13: teeno jodo = 29 = 2(x + y + z) → x + y + z = 14.5. Ab x = 14.5 − 9 = 5.5, y = 14.5 − 13 = 1.5, z = 14.5 − 7 = 7.5 → 11 : 3 : 15.
  • p − q : q − r : r − s = 3 : 4 : 7: s = 0 maan lo. r = 7, q = 11, p = 14 → (p + s) : r = 14 : 7 = 2 : 1.

Speed method 9 · Proportion ke 4 aujaar

Soch aise: a : b = c : d ka matlab, bahar wale ka guna = andar wale ka guna. ad = bc. Baaki sab isi se nikalta hai.

"Kya ghatayein ki proportion ban jaye?" · 5 second wala formula a, b, c, d mein se x ghatao: x = (a × d − b × c) ÷ ((a + d) − (b + c)).
10, 12, 13, 16: ad = 160, bc = 156, (26 − 25) = 1 → x = 4/1 = 4.
Jawab minus aaye toh matlab utna jodna hai. 12, 20, 28, 44: (528 − 560) ÷ (56 − 48) = −4 → 4 jodo.
Ya seedha option daalo: 10 − 4 = 6, 12 − 4 = 8, 13 − 4 = 9, 16 − 4 = 12 → 6 × 12 = 8 × 9 ✔.

Speed method 10 · Unitary aur "seedha/ulta anupaat"

  • Seedha (direct): ek badhe toh doosra bhi utne hi guna badhe. 5 pencil ₹25 → 1 pencil ₹5 → 8 pencil ₹40. Ya seedha 25 × 8/5.
  • Ulta (inverse): ek badhe toh doosra ghate, guna wahi rahe. 30 cheezein × 45 row = 1350. Ab 25 ek row mein → 1350 ÷ 25 = 54 row.
  • Power wala: "r³ ke saath seedha": radius dugna → time 2³ = 8 guna. 5 min → 40 min.
  • Mix: I ∝ P ÷ d². Naya = purana × (P badhat) × (d ghatat)² = 15 × 100/60 × (2/3)² = 15 × 5/3 × 4/9 = 100/9 ≈ 11.11.
Trick: decimal ko fraction mein badlo. 0.375 = 3/8, 0.625 = 5/8, 0.125 = 1/8. 0.375 L har second → 8 second mein 3/8 × 8 = 3 L.

Speed method 11 · Option daal kar dekho

  • Order ulat gaya (swap) wale sawaal: option ka ratio le lo, bill pehle aur baad mein nikaalo, % milao. Equation se tez.
  • Umar wale: option ki umar mein 6 jodo, ratio milta hai? 15 second.
  • Divisibility: 2 : 5 : 3 ratio hai toh kul 10 ka multiple. Jo option 10 se nahi bata, kaat do.
  • Decimal options (53.73, 342.86): fraction ka andaaza lagao. 1200 × 2/7 ≈ 343 → 342.86.
Demo · 8 dabbe premium pen (daam 2 guna), kuch standard. Order ulta aaya, bill 25% badha. Original ratio? · ~15 sec Option 1 : 2 lo → premium 8, standard 16. Daam 2 aur 1.
Pehle: 8 × 2 + 16 × 1 = 32. Ulta: 16 × 2 + 8 × 1 = 40. 40/32 = 1.25 → 25% ✔ → 1 : 2.

Kaunsa method kab? (ek nazar mein)

Yaad karne wali sheet a : b = c : d → ad = bc · mean = √(ab) · third = b²/a · fourth = bc/a
Duplicate ratio (a² : b²), triplicate (a³ : b³), sub-duplicate (√a : √b). CGL+
Area ka ratio 9 : 16 → side ka 3 : 4 (square root). Side 2 : 3 → volume 8 : 27 (cube). CGL+
HCF diya ho: number = ratio × HCF. 2 : 5 aur HCF 11 → 22 aur 55.
Compound ratio of a : b aur c : d = ac : bd. CGL+
Componendo-dividendo: a/b = c/d → (a + b)/(a − b) = (c + d)/(c − d). CGL+
