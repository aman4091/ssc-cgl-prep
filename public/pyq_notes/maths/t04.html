Percentage: shuru se, aur 36-second wale tareeke

2025–26 SSC PYQs (CGL, CHSL, CPO, Selection Post XIV, MTS, Delhi Police, GD 2026) · sabse zyada: badhna-ghatna lagataar, "kitna % kam/zyada", income-kharcha-bachat, % se number

Pehle ek chhoti si kahani: 100 dabbe

Ek class mein 100 bachche hain. Unmein 25 ladkiyan hain. Toh har 100 mein 25 ladkiyan. Bas, isi ko 25 percent kehte hain. "Percent" ka matlab hi hai "har 100 mein".

Ab class mein 100 nahi, 4 bachche hain, aur 1 ladki hai. Har 4 mein 1. Agar 4 ko 25 se guna karo toh 100 ban jaata hai, aur 1 bhi 25 ban jaata hai. Toh yahan bhi har 100 mein 25 hi hai. Yaani 1/4 aur 25% ek hi baat hai.

Teen baatein, jinse saara chapter nikalta hai

  • 1. Percent = per 100 = fraction. 20% = 20/100 = 1/5. 12½% = 1/8. Toh "% of" ka matlab hai "fraction se guna".
  • 2. Har % kisi ka hota hai (base). "20% zyada" kis par? Jis se tulna ho rahi hai, wahi base hai, wahi 100 maano. Aadhi galtiyan yahin hoti hain.
  • 3. Badhna = guna karna. 20% badha = 100 se 120 = × 6/5. 20% ghata = 100 se 80 = × 4/5. Do baar badla? Bas do baar guna karo.
Ab naam rakh dete hain Jo shuru mein tha = original (base). Jitna badla = change. Change ÷ original × 100 = % change.
Badhne ke baad ka guna (6/5, 4/5) = multiplying factor. Isi ek cheez se successive %, population, depreciation, sab chalta hai.

Paper mein ek sawaal ke liye lagbhag 36 second milte hain. Neeche 9 tareeke hain. Sab haath se, rough mein, bina lambe decimal ke chalte hain.

Speed method 2 · 10% aur 1% se todo

Kab use karo: jab % saaf fraction na ho (35%, 18%, 11%, 8%).

  • 10% = number ka ek digit peeche khiskao. 460 ka 10% = 46.
  • 5% = 10% ka aadha. 1% = do digit khiskao. 460 ka 1% = 4.6.
  • Baaki sab inhi ka jod: 35% = 3 × 10% + 5%. 18% = 20% − 2%. 11% = 10% + 1%.
Demo · 35% of 460 · ~8 sec 10% = 46 → 30% = 138. 5% = 23. Jodo: 138 + 23 = 161.
Trick: x% of y = y% of x. 16% of 25 mushkil lage, toh 25% of 16 = 4. 2.5% of 200 = 200% of 2.5 = 5.

Speed method 3 · Ulta chalo: "x% = value" toh number?

Kab use karo: "60% of a number is 48", "16% mixture mein 64 kg", "75% beche, 720 bache".

Soch aise: x% ki keemat pata hai. Usse 1% nikaalo (value ÷ x). Phir jo % chahiye, utne se guna. Pura number = 1% × 100.

Demo · 35% = 140. Number? Aur 80%? · ~8 sec 1% = 140 ÷ 35 = 4. Number = 4 × 100 = 400. 80% = 4 × 80 = 320.
Fraction se bhi: 60% = 3/5 hai, toh "60% = 48" → number = 48 × 5/3 = 80.
Trick: "Do % ka farq diya" (45% − 30% = 0.9) toh farq wala % hi value ke barabar hai: 15% = 0.9. Number nikaalne ke liye number ko kabhi "x" mat likho.

Speed method 4 · % change aur "kis ke mukaable" (base)

Formula ek hi: % change = change ÷ shuru wala × 100. 400 se 500 hua: change 100, shuru 400 → 25%.

Ab asli khel: "A, B se 25% zyada hai. B, A se kitna kam?" Log 25% bol dete hain. Galat. Picture dekho.

Do formule (picture se hi nikle) A, B se r% zyada → B, A se r/(100 + r) × 100 % kam. 25% → 25/125 = 20%.
A, B se r% kam → B, A se r/(100 − r) × 100 % zyada. 40% → 40/60 = 66⅔%.

Kab use karo: 3–4 kadam ek ke baad ek (A, B se 20% zyada, B, C se 30% zyada …), ya population 3 saal, ya machine ki keemat girna.

Speed method 7 · Guna same rakhna (daam × khapat, lambai × chaudai)

Kab use karo: "daam 25% badha, kharcha same rakhna hai, khapat kitni ghatao?" ya "lambai 25% badhi, area same, chaudai kitni ghatao?"

"Daam ghata, utne paise mein x kg zyada" · ~10 sec Daam 20% gira, ₹200 mein 5 kg zyada aaya. Socho: 200 ka 20% = ₹40 bache. Inhi 40 se 5 kg aaya. Toh naya daam = 40 ÷ 5 = ₹8.
Purana daam = 8 × 5/4 = ₹10 (20% kam = 4/5, toh ulta 5/4). Koi equation nahi.

Speed method 9 · Baaki ki chain, aur "farq ka %"

  • Baaki ki chain: "20% gaye, phir baaki ka 25% gaye" → bache = 80% × 75% = 60%. Kabhi 20 + 25 = 45 mat jodo. Baaki % ko guna karo.
  • Farq ka %: exam aur election mein do baatein diye hon (30% par 12 se fail, 44% par 16 se paas), toh % ka farq = marks ka farq. 14% = 28 → 1% = 2.
  • Kam se kam ek (sets): A + B − dono. 60% English, 70% Maths, 45% dono → kam se kam ek = 85%.
Demo · 30% matric, baaki ka 60% graduate, baaki 84 PG. Matric? · ~12 sec Baaki 70%. Uska 40% PG (kyunki 60% graduate) → 70 × 40/100 = 28%.
28% = 84 → 1% = 3 → matric 30% = 90.
