Check yourself

Admission of a new State vs changing a State's boundary — which Articles?

Admission/establishment = Art 2. Forming a State or altering area, boundary, name = Art 3. Art 4 says such laws are not Art 368 amendments.

Which Article abolishes titles, and what is exempt?

Art 18; military and academic distinctions are exempt.

Name the five Fundamental Rights that foreigners do not get.

Arts 15, 16, 19, 29 and 30.

Which Article did Ambedkar call the "heart and soul" of the Constitution?

Art 32 — Right to Constitutional Remedies.

Which two Fundamental Rights cannot be suspended even during a National Emergency?

Arts 20 and 21 (since the 44th Amendment, 1978).

Directive Principles of State Policy — Part IV (Articles 36–51)

DPSPs are guidelines for the State. Art 37 says they are not enforceable by any court, yet they are fundamental in the governance of the country. They are the part that aims at social and economic reforms (a welfare State). PYQ Borrowed from the Irish Constitution (see Sources chapter).

Uniform Civil Code (Article 44) — the most asked DPSP

  • Basis of the UCC demand → Article 44 PYQ. It is a Directive Principle, not a Fundamental Right or Duty PYQ ×2.
  • Aim: one set of civil / personal laws for all citizens (marriage, divorce, inheritance, adoption), i.e. equal rights across religions — not criminal or tax law. PYQ ×3
  • The dilemma: UCC fits Art 44 but may be seen to infringe religious freedom under Art 25. PYQ
  • Courts: the Supreme Court (Shah Bano 1985, Sarla Mudgal 1995, Shayara Bano 2017) has urged a UCC but left the law to the legislature; Art 44 is non-justiciable. PYQ UCC is not part of the basic structure. PYQ
  • Status: not implemented nationwide. Uttarakhand is the first State to enact one (in force 27 Jan 2025) PYQ; it keeps Scheduled Tribes out of its scope PYQ. Goa already has a common civil code (from Portuguese times). CGL+

Amendment → Schedule: the pair SSC asks most

Trick: "1 → 9, 52 → 10, 73 → 11, 74 → 12". For the last two, the amendment number minus 62 gives the Schedule (73−62 = 11, 74−62 = 12).

Tricks that sort the confusing ones

Trick: Ireland = "DPR" — Directive Principles, President's election, Rajya Sabha nominations. Australia = "CTJ" — Concurrent List, Trade freedom, Joint sitting. Canada = "strong Centre family" — strong Centre, residuary powers to Centre, Governor appointed by Centre, Centre's (President's) advice from SC.
Trick: Rajya Sabha has two parents: members nominated → Ireland; members elected → South Africa. Emergency has two parents: the provisions → 1935 Act; suspending FRs → Germany (Weimar).
Trick: Law words: "procedure established by law" → Japan; "due process of law" → USA (not adopted in the text); "rule of law" → Britain; "judicial review" → USA.
⚠ Answer-key noteA Steno 2025 question (7 Aug, Shift 1) asked which feature is linked to the Australian Constitution, with both freedom of trade and commerce and joint sitting among the options. The key gave joint sitting. Both are Australian. If only one option is allowed, follow the key (joint sitting), but remember the trade-and-commerce link too.

How an amendment is made (Article 368)

  • Art 368 = power of Parliament to amend the Constitution and its procedure. PYQ The bill can start in either House, not in a State legislature. CGL+
  • Special majority: a majority of the total membership of each House and 2/3 of members present and voting. PYQ No joint sitting is possible for it.
  • For federal matters (e.g. representation of States in Parliament, distribution of legislative powers, High Courts) the bill also needs ratification by half the States. CGL+ Forming a new State (Arts 2–4) needs no Art 368 amendment. PYQ
  • Limit: basic structure doctrine — Parliament cannot change the core identity of the Constitution (Kesavananda Bharati, 1973, 13 judges). PYQ ×2
  • The procedure idea came from South Africa (see Sources).
