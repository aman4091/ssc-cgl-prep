Swadeshi and the 1906 Calcutta session

  • Partition of Bengal, 1905 — carried out by Viceroy Lord Curzon. PYQ It came into effect on 16 October 1905. CGL+
  • Swadeshi Movement, 1905 — formally launched at the Calcutta Town Hall on 7 August 1905. CGL+ Tagore used Raksha Bandhan as a symbol of Hindu–Muslim unity against the partition. PYQ
  • Barindra Kumar Ghosh, Abhinash Bhattacharya and Bhupendranath Dutta (1906) were part of the Swadeshi movement (the revolutionary Jugantar wing). PYQ
  • V.O. Chidambaram Pillai started the Swadeshi Steam Navigation Company (Tuticorin). PYQ
  • 1906 Calcutta session (President Dadabhai Naoroji) put four demands together: Swaraj, Swadeshi, Boycott, National Education. The word "Swaraj" was declared the goal here. CGL+
  • The Muslim League was founded in December 1906 at Dhaka. CGL+
Trick: 1906 ke 4 = "S-S-B-N" → Swaraj, Swadeshi, Boycott, National Education. "Sab Swadeshi Banao Naya."

Surat Split 1907 → Lucknow Reunion 1916

  • Surat, 1907 (President Rash Behari Ghosh): the Congress broke into Moderates (naram dal — Gokhale and others) and Extremists (garam dal — Tilak and others).
  • Do not mix Rash Behari Ghosh (Moderate, Surat president) with Rash Behari Bose (revolutionary, later in Japan with the INA). A PYQ asked what is NOT true about Bose: "he was independent India's first Commander-in-Chief" — false. PYQ
  • Morley–Minto Reforms, 1909 (Indian Councils Act 1909): nationalists attacked them because they brought separate electorates based on religion PYQ; they also enlarged the legislative councils. PYQ
  • Lucknow, 1916 (President Ambika Charan Mazumdar) had two big events: (1) Moderates and Extremists reunited; (2) the Congress–Muslim League Lucknow Pact. Under the pact the Congress accepted separate electorates. Tilak and Jinnah were its main architects. CGL+
  • The Home Rule Leagues were also born in 1916: Tilak's (April, Belgaum) and Annie Besant's (September, Madras). CGL+

Gaya 1922 → Swaraj Party 1923

  • Gaya, 1922 — President C.R. Das. The big fight: Council Entry. Should Congressmen enter the legislative councils (set up under the 1919 reforms) or keep boycotting them?
  • "Pro-changers" (C.R. Das, Motilal Nehru) wanted entry; "No-changers" (Vallabhbhai Patel, Rajendra Prasad, C. Rajagopalachari) wanted to stay out. CGL+
  • The pro-changers formed the Swaraj Party in 1923 (1 January 1923). Founders: C.R. Das + Motilal Nehru. Aim: enter the councils and block colonial policy from inside.
  • After C.R. Das died (1925), the Bengal Congress broke into two camps; the Anushilan group joined forces with J.M. Sengupta. PYQ
  • Delhi special session, 1923: Abul Kalam Azad (age 35) — youngest Congress president. CGL+

Belgaum 1924 and Kanpur 1925

  • Belgaum, 1924 — President Mahatma Gandhi. It was the first and only Congress session Gandhi ever presided over.
  • Kanpur, 1925 — President Sarojini Naidu, the first Indian woman president.

Simon Commission, Madras 1927 and Calcutta 1928

  • Madras, 1927 — President M.A. Ansari. Decision: boycott the Simon Commission. This session also passed a resolution for complete independence, moved by Jawaharlal Nehru. CGL+
  • Why the boycott? The Commission had no Indian member — all 7 were British. PYQ ×2 Slogan: "Simon Go Back". CGL+
  • Calcutta, 1928 — President Motilal Nehru. Main issue: the Nehru Report (1928), drafted by a committee under Motilal Nehru. It asked for dominion status, not complete independence. PYQ The session gave the British one year to grant dominion status. CGL+
PYQ statement check (Steno 2025)"Simon Commission was boycotted because of its all-Indian composition" — false (it was all-British). "Nehru Report demanded complete independence as the immediate aim" — false (it asked for dominion status). Answer: Neither 1 nor 2. PYQ

Lahore 1929 ⭐⭐⭐ — Purna Swaraj

  • President: Jawaharlal Nehru.
  • Most important decision: Purna Swaraj = Complete Independence declared as the Congress goal.
  • Nehru hoisted the tricolour on the bank of the Ravi on 31 December 1929. CGL+
  • 26 January 1930 was observed as Independence Day — the first time. That is why the Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950. PYQ

Karachi 1931 ⭐⭐⭐ — Fundamental Rights

  • President: Vallabhbhai Patel.
  • Two resolutions: Fundamental Rights and the National Economic Programme. The resolution was drafted by Nehru. CGL+
  • The session came right after the Gandhi–Irwin Pact (5 March 1931) and endorsed it. CGL+ Gandhi then went to the Second Round Table Conference as the Congress's only representative. CGL+
  • It met days after Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev were hanged (23 March 1931). CGL+
Patel — PYQ cluster"Bismarck of India" for uniting the princely states PYQ · National Unity Day on his birthday, 31 October PYQ · first Home Minister of free India PYQ · Chairman of the Constituent Assembly's Advisory Committee on Fundamental Rights, Minorities, Tribal and Excluded Areas PYQ. Neat link: Patel chaired Karachi (FR resolution, 1931) and the FR committee (1947).
Trick: Lahore = Liberty (Purna Swaraj, Nehru). Karachi = Kanoon ke adhikar (Fundamental Rights, Patel). "Nehru ne aazadi maangi, Patel ne adhikar likhe."

Check yourself

Who founded the Swaraj Party, and in which year?

C.R. Das and Motilal Nehru, 1923 — after the council-entry debate at Gaya (1922).

Belgaum, 1924.

Lahore 1929 or Karachi 1931 — where was the Fundamental Rights resolution passed?

Why did the Congress boycott the Simon Commission?

It had no Indian member. The boycott decision came at Madras, 1927 (President M.A. Ansari).

When was Independence Day first celebrated?

26 January 1930, after the Lahore resolution.

Trick: Individual Satyagraha = "V for Very first" → Vinoba Bhave; Nehru came second. And 1942 order: "C before Q" — Cripps (March) failed, so Quit India (August).

1945–1946: Simla, Cabinet Mission, Meerut

  • Simla Conference, 1945 — called by Viceroy Lord Wavell (the Wavell Plan). Main issue: reconstituting the Viceroy's Executive Council with Indians. It failed when Jinnah claimed the right to name all Muslim members. CGL+
  • Cabinet Mission, 1946 — three British cabinet ministers came to discuss the transfer of power and a constitutional framework. It proposed three groups of provinces (A, B, C) and a Constituent Assembly. CGL+
  • Gopinath Bordoloi kept Assam in India by opposing its grouping with Bengal under this plan. PYQ
  • The Constituent Assembly first met on 9 December 1946; Dr Rajendra Prasad became its permanent President on 11 December 1946. PYQ
  • Meerut, 1946 — President J.B. Kripalani, the last Congress president before Independence (in office in August 1947).

Doping: WADA, NADA, rule violations, TUE and ABP

Asked in Phase 13, CHSL 2025, CGL 2025, Steno 2026 and very often in Phase-XIV 2026 · most asked: NADA's role and HQ, TUE, whereabouts, famous Indian doping cases

What is doping?

Doping = the use or presence of prohibited substances or methods in sport, breaking anti-doping rules. In one line: use of banned performance-enhancing drugs. PYQ Example of a banned class: steroids PYQ; narcotics such as morphine and methadone are also on the list. PYQ

Anti-Doping Rule Violations (ADRVs)

A breach of the Code is called an Anti-Doping Rule Violation (ADRV). PYQ You can be punished even without a positive test.

⚠ Missing itemThe PDF lists 10 violations. The current World Anti-Doping Code (2021) has 11: the eleventh is acts to discourage, or retaliate against, people who report doping to the authorities.
