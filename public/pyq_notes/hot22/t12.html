Dynasties after the Mauryas: one table

After Ashoka's empire broke up (c. 185 BCE), two kinds of powers rose: Indian dynasties (Shunga, Kanva, Satavahana, Kharavela's line) and foreign dynasties from the north-west (Indo-Greek, Shaka, Parthian, Kushana).

Plate tectonics

The rigid lithosphere is broken into plates that move over the soft asthenosphere. The Pacific Plate is the largest.

  • Match: mid-ocean ridge – divergent; Mariana Trench – convergent; San Andreas – transform. PYQ
  • The Indian–Eurasian collision (convergent) formed the Himalayas; this is why the Himalayan belt is the most quake-prone. PYQ
  • Alfred Wegener: continental drift (1912). Supercontinent Pangaea, surrounded by the ocean Panthalassa, later split into Laurasia (north) and Gondwanaland (south).
  • Harry Hess: sea-floor spreading (1960s). Sea-floor spreading and plate tectonics explain the uneven spread of continents and oceans. PYQ
  • Ring of Fire: the belt of volcanoes and earthquakes around much of the Pacific.

Heredity

  • Gene = unit of heredity; DNA carries the genetic information; chromosomes = DNA + proteins.
  • Genotype = genetic make-up; phenotype = traits we can see.
  • Mendel worked on pea plants PYQ and gave the laws of dominance, segregation and independent assortment.
  • DNA double helix: Watson and Crick (1953); key X-ray diffraction evidence from Rosalind Franklin and Maurice Wilkins. DNA was first found as "nuclein" by Friedrich Miescher in 1869. PYQ
  • Human body cell: 46 chromosomes (23 pairs); gametes: 23. Usually XX = female, XY = male.
  • Mitochondria make most of the cell's ATP (but glycolysis happens in the cytoplasm PYQ); ribosomes = protein synthesis PYQ.

Ecology basics
