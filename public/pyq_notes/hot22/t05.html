2 · PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana) — crop insurance

PointFact
LaunchedKharif 2016 (replaced the older NAIS and MNAIS CGL+)
ObjectiveCrop insurance against crop loss from non-preventable natural risks (drought, flood, pests); stabilise farmers' income and encourage new practices
Farmer's premiumKharif 2% · Rabi 1.5% · Commercial / horticultural 5%; the government pays the rest
Revamp (Kharif 2020)Enrolment made voluntary for all farmers (even loanee farmers); claims sped up with a Crop Insurance Portal and smartphone / GPS-based crop-cutting experiments PYQ
  • Statement question: "covers losses from non-preventable natural risks" ✔, "aims to stabilise income" ✔, "enrolment mandatory for all" ✘, "premium uniform for all crops" ✘ → answer I and III only. PYQ
  • Odd one out among LEADS, NSWS, PMFBY, Gati Shakti: PMFBY (the only farm scheme; the others are trade / logistics / investment). PYQ
Trick: PMFBY premium "2 – 1.5 – 5" = Kharif, Rabi, Commercial. Kharif (monsoon) is riskier than Rabi, so it costs a bit more; cash crops cost the most.

11 · PM-JAY SEHAT

A health-cover scheme for Jammu & Kashmir under the Ayushman Bharat framework. It extends the ₹5 lakh cover to all residents of J&K (launched Dec 2020 CGL+).

12 · PMBJP (Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana)

  • Objective: affordable, good-quality generic medicines.
  • Sold through Jan Aushadhi Kendras. Runs under the Department of Pharmaceuticals; 7 March is Jan Aushadhi Diwas. CGL+
Trick: Janaushadhi → Generic Medicines ("jan ki aushadhi = sasti dawa").

13 · Mission Indradhanush

  • Objective: improve immunisation coverage.
  • Target: children and pregnant women, especially those partly vaccinated or not vaccinated at all.
  • Launched 25 Dec 2014; named after the 7 colours of the rainbow — it began with 7 vaccine-preventable diseases. CGL+
  • The Universal Immunisation Programme now covers 12 diseases (nationally and sub-nationally). PYQ Its digital registry to track every pregnant woman and child is U-WIN. PYQ
Trick: Indradhanush → Immunisation (I → I). 7 colours → started with 7 diseases.

20 · Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP)

  • Launched 22 Jan 2015 (Panipat, Haryana CGL+).
  • Ministry: Women & Child Development (a correctly matched pair) PYQ; run jointly with Health and Education. CGL+
  • Focus: protection, education and empowerment of the girl child; started to fix the falling child sex ratio. CGL+

21 · Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

  • Launched 2015, as part of Beti Bachao Beti Padhao.
  • A small savings scheme for the girl child.
  • Account opened before the girl turns 10; deposit ₹250 to ₹1.5 lakh a year; matures in 21 years. CGL+
Trick: BBBP → Girl Child; Sukanya → Girl Child Savings. Both born on the same day, 22 Jan 2015.

Mission Shakti (women's safety and empowerment) has two sub-schemes: Sambal for safety and security, Samarthya for empowerment. PYQ

24 · MGNREGA

PointFact
Act2005 (NREGA; in force 2 Feb 2006 from Anantapur, AP; "Mahatma Gandhi" added in 2009 CGL+)
MinistryRural Development
TargetRural households (adults willing to do unskilled manual work)
Guarantee100 days of wage employment per household per year
ExtraAt least one-third of workers should be women; work within 5 km; jobless allowance if no work within 15 days CGL+

  • Older roots: the National Rural Employment Programme began in the Sixth Five-Year Plan PYQ; the Antyodaya programme (1977) aimed to uplift the poorest sections. PYQ
⚠ Update the PDF does not haveSteno 2026 questions (as per their key) say the Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 (VB–G RAM G) replaced MGNREGA. Trick: MGNREGA → Rural → 100 Days. New law: 100 + 25 = 125.
