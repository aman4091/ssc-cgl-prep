World reports & rankings 2025–26: who publishes, where India stands, who is No. 1

Steno 2026 · Phase-XIV 2026 · CGL/CHSL 2025 · most asked: publisher of each index, India's rank (Happiness 116, HDI 130, Press Freedom 157), the No. 1 country, and the factors each index uses

How to read every index (the 4-question method)

For every report, learn exactly four things: (1) publisher, (2) India's rank, (3) the No. 1 country, (4) what it measures. Exams ask almost nothing else. Publisher questions are the most common and they never go out of date, so learn those first.

Rank logicIn most indices, rank 1 = best. Two are "bad-news" lists where rank 1 = worst-hit: the Global Terrorism Index (most affected by terrorism) and the Global Climate Risk Index (most hit by extreme weather). The Global Hunger Index tells you the hungriest by the lowest rank number at the bottom of the list (Somalia 123rd = worst).

World Happiness Report — the most-asked index

  • Published every year PYQ; first report in 2012. PYQ It is not a UNDP report (A true, R false in the A–R question). PYQ
  • Life evaluation is measured on the Cantril Ladder (Cantril self-anchoring scale, 0 to 10). PYQ ×2 Data comes from self-reported (Gallup) surveys PYQ, so it measures subjective well-being. PYQ Main difficulty: keeping survey methods uniform across countries. PYQ
  • "Social support" = having friends or relatives to count on in trouble. PYQ Trust in relationships is a social factor. PYQ Not a factor: political stability PYQ, internet speed, sports performance. PYQ ×2
  • Common to WHR and HDI: life expectancy. PYQ
  • 2026: Costa Rica was the non-European country in the top five PYQ; Norway 6th PYQ; Afghanistan last. PYQ
  • 2025: India was 118th PYQ, below its 2015 rank even though GDP roughly doubled. PYQ India ranks below most South Asian neighbours, but its GDP per capita is not the lowest in South Asia (A true, R false). PYQ
  • Bhutan first put happiness above growth (Gross National Happiness). PYQ

HDI — method questions

  • First published in 1990; devised by Mahbub ul Haq with Amartya Sen.
  • Health dimension = life expectancy at birth. PYQ ×2 Education = mean and expected years of schooling. CGL+ Income = GNI per capita (PPP), with a logarithmic transformation applied. PYQ HDI is the geometric mean of the three. CGL+
  • HDI can rise even if income falls, because better health and education offset it (A and R true, R explains A). PYQ A high-HDI country can still have high multidimensional poverty, since HDI is an average (both true, R explains A). PYQ
  • India's HDI improved after 2010, and the inequality-adjusted IHDI was also introduced in 2010, but the second does not explain the first. PYQ "Human development ethics" critics say HDI assumes universal values and development goals. PYQ

Other governance facts asked

  • CPI 2024: India 96th (score 38). A low score signals high regulatory risk and so hurts foreign investment. PYQ
  • Press Freedom 2024: India's fall was blamed on violence against journalists and digital surveillance. PYQ (India: 159th in 2024, 151st in 2025, 157th in 2026.)
  • Gender Gap 2024: India 129th; it lags most in labour-force participation and wage parity (economic participation). PYQ
  • Sustainable Development Report 2024: India 109th; weakest on SDG 2 (Zero Hunger) and SDG 6 (Clean Water). PYQ
  • UN E-Government Development Index 2024: India 97th; strong online services but lags in human capital and rural digital literacy. PYQ
  • Brand Finance Global Soft Power Index 2026 (not in the PDF): China 2nd PYQ; Japan overtook the UK to reach 3rd. PYQ

⚠ Dragon Hole is no longer the deepestThe PDF calls Dragon Hole (Sansha Yongle Blue Hole, ~301 m) "the world's deepest ocean sinkhole". In an exam, pick Dragon Hole only if Taam Ja' is not among the options.
