Universe and stars

  • Most accepted idea of how the universe began: the Big Bang Theory. PYQ It happened about 13.7–13.8 billion years ago. CGL+
  • Within about 3,00,000 years of the Big Bang, the temperature fell to 4,500 K. Atoms formed and the universe became transparent. PYQ
  • CGL+ Edwin Hubble (1920s) showed the universe is expanding (galaxies moving apart).
  • A star is made of gases (mostly hydrogen and helium). It makes its own heat and light by nuclear fusion, so "stars have no heat and light of their own" is false. Correct option: Only I. PYQ ×2
  • CGL+ Nearest star after the Sun: Proxima Centauri (~4.2 light years). A light year is a unit of distance (~9.46 × 10¹² km), not time.
Sunlight takes about 8 minutes PYQ ×2 Sun–Earth distance ≈ 150 million km (1 Astronomical Unit). Light covers it in about 8.311 minutes, i.e. roughly eight minutes (8 min 20 s).

PYQ Faint Young Sun paradox: the young Sun gave only ~70% of today's light, so early solar output was too weak to stop global freezing. Yet rocks show liquid water existed. Likely answer: a thick greenhouse atmosphere (CO₂, methane) kept early Earth warm.

Earth's motions

  • Seen from above the North Pole, Earth spins anti-clockwise (west to east). PYQ That is why the Sun rises in the east.
  • Seasons change because of the tilt of Earth's axis (23.5°), not because of distance from the Sun. PYQ
  • CGL+ The axis makes 66.5° with the orbital plane. Earth is closest to the Sun (perihelion) about 3 January and farthest (aphelion) about 4 July.

Inside the Earth

Plates and their boundaries

River landforms

  • A gorge forms most often in hard rocks: the river cuts down faster than the steep walls can wear back. PYQ
  • Meanders form by lateral (sideways) erosion in flat terrain. PYQ Cut-off loops become oxbow lakes. CGL+
  • Chambal ravines (badlands): the river cuts deep gullies into soft soil before joining the Yamuna — both A and R true, R explains A. PYQ They lie in Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh. PYQ CGL+ The National Chambal Sanctuary (gharial) is shared by the same three states.

Glacial landforms

  • Esker: a long, winding ridge of sand and gravel laid by meltwater streams flowing under the glacier. PYQ ×2
  • Till: unsorted mix dropped directly by ice (boulders to clay together). PYQ
  • Outwash: sorted, rounded sediment from meltwater streams, laid beyond the ice front. PYQ
  • Hanging valleys open into the main glacial valley high up, and their spurs are cut into triangular facets (truncated spurs) — 1 and 2 both correct. PYQ
Trick: Ice drops = unsorted (till, moraine). Water drops = sorted & layered (esker, outwash).

Desert (wind) landforms

  • In the other version, only statement 3 was right: pedestal rocks are hard-rock remnants shaped by wind deflation and abrasion. PYQ Mushroom rocks are made by wind, not by water in glacial valleys; a table rock (mesa) has a broad base, not a narrow one.

Ocean currents

  • Big ocean currents are driven by wind and density. PYQ Wind drives surface currents; density (set by temperature and salinity) drives deep currents.
  • CGL+ Coriolis force bends currents to the right in the northern hemisphere. Warm: Gulf Stream, Kuroshio. Cold: Labrador, Canaries, Humboldt (Peru), Benguela, Oyashio. Warm meets cold → fog and rich fishing (Grand Banks off Newfoundland).
AMOC slowdown PYQ The Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation carries warm water north. If it weakens: the North Atlantic cools, tropical monsoons are disturbed, sea level rises on the US east coast. NOT a likely result: more warming in the Arctic (less heat would go north).

  • NOT kharif from rice, cotton, wheat, mustard, maize, barley: wheat, mustard, barley (3, 4, 6). PYQ

How a population changes

The equation New population = old population + (births − deaths) + (in-migrants − out-migrants).
So there are 3 processes: births, deaths, migration. PYQ Employment is not a component. PYQ The natural causes are births and deaths, not migration → only I (births). PYQ

  • India: "broad at base and top, narrow in middle" ✘ (it is broad at the base and narrows upward); "large young population from high births and falling deaths" ✔ → Statement-I incorrect, II correct. PYQ

Malthus

Thomas Malthus (1798): population grows geometrically (1, 2, 4, 8), food only arithmetically (1, 2, 3, 4). So the gap between food and population brings hardship unless population is checked. PYQ CGL+ Positive checks: famine, disease, war. Preventive checks: late marriage, self-control.
