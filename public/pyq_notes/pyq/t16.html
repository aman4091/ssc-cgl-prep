Stone Age and Harappan clues

  • A celt is a polished stone axe, so it is a tool of the Neolithic age. PYQ
  • CGL+ Neolithic = polished tools + farming + pottery + settled life. Mehrgarh (oldest farming in the subcontinent), Burzahom (pit houses, dogs buried with owners), Chirand (bone tools), Koldihwa (early rice).
  • Harappan raw materials came from far away, and they made clay models of carts and boats. So the right reading is: external materials and routes show an integrated exchange network. PYQ
  • CGL+ Copper from Khetri (Rajasthan), lapis lazuli from Badakhshan (Afghanistan), outpost at Shortughai, dockyard at Lothal, bead factory at Chanhudaro. Mesopotamian texts call the Indus land Meluhha. No coins: trade was barter.
  • "Hindu" began as a place name (people beyond the Sindhu) and slowly became a religious label. This shows social categories evolve and words take new meanings over time. PYQ CGL+ Persians said "H" for "S": Sindhu → Hindu ("Hindush" in Darius I's inscriptions). Greeks: Indos → India.

Sakas in western India

  • Saka power in western India was spread by the Kshaharata rulers Bhumaka and Nahapana. PYQ
  • CGL+ Nahapana was beaten by the Satavahana king Gautamiputra Satakarni (Nasik inscription of his mother Gautami Balashri).
  • CGL+ Rudradaman I (Kardamaka line): Junagadh inscription (c. 150 CE), the first long inscription in pure Sanskrit; he repaired the Sudarshana lake. Saka era starts in 78 CE — base of India's national calendar.

Modern India: Acts, Land Revenue & Freedom Fighters

SSC Selection Post Phase-XIV 2026 · 16–26 Sep · statement questions on 1773, 1784, 1892, 1909 Acts; Ryotwari vs Permanent Settlement; education despatches

Acts of Parliament on one line

PYQ The body that framed the structure of British administration in India: British Parliament (through a chain of Acts, 1773 onwards). CGL+ GoI Act 1858 moved power from the Company to the Crown; Lord Canning became the first Viceroy; Queen Victoria's Proclamation was read on 1 Nov 1858 at Allahabad.

Regulating Act, 1773

  • Passed by the British Parliament to control the Company — true. It raised (not reduced) the Governor of Bengal to Governor-General of Bengal. So answer: Only 1. PYQ
  • It set up a Supreme Court at Calcutta: 1 Chief Justice + 3 judges (started 1774). PYQ
  • First Chief Justice: Sir Elijah Impey. PYQ Other judges: Chambers, Lemaistre, Hyde. CGL+
  • CGL+ First GG of Bengal: Warren Hastings, with a council of 4. Bombay and Madras were placed under Bengal. Impey is linked with the hanging of Nandakumar (1775).
  • CGL+ Independent India's first CJI: H.J. Kania (1950). Federal Court (1937, GoI Act 1935): Maurice Gwyer.

Pitt's India Act, 1784

  • Created the Board of Control (political work) while the Court of Directors kept trade → political and commercial functions separated. With the 1773 Supreme Court statement, answer: Both 1 and 2 are correct. PYQ
  • The Governor-General and council could not declare war or sign treaties without the Directors or the Secret Committee — true. Madras and Bombay were put under Bengal in war, diplomacy and revenue, not "only revenue" — false. Answer: Only 2. PYQ
  • CGL+ This made a "dual system of control". Company lands were called "British possessions in India" for the first time. PM: William Pitt the Younger.

Three land-revenue systems

  • Ryotwari "started by Alexander Read in 1729 in Bombay" ✘ (wrong year, wrong place); "Madras came before Bombay" ✔. Answer: Only 2 is correct. PYQ CGL+ Bombay Ryotwari is linked with Mountstuart Elphinstone.
  • Permanent Settlement: before 1793 zamindars only collected revenue ✔; after 1793 zamindars (not peasants) became owners ✘. Answer: Only 1 is correct. PYQ CGL+ Zamindar kept 1/11, Company took 10/11; John Shore helped plan it.

Education policy

Railways

  • CGL+ First passenger train: Bori Bunder (Bombay) → Thane, 16 April 1853, under Dalhousie. Acworth Committee → separate Railway Budget (1924). Rail budget merged with the Union Budget in 2017.

Badminton

  • Server's score even (0, 2, 4…) → serve from the Right service court. PYQ
  • All court lines are 40 mm wide, and a shuttle landing on a line is "in". PYQ
  • A fault under BWF laws: the same player hitting the shuttle twice in a row. PYQ A net-cord that drops into the other court is fine; hitting below the waist in a rally is legal.
  • CGL+ Game to 21 points, best of three; at 20-all lead by 2; at 29-all the 30th point wins. Shuttle has 16 feathers. Olympic sport since 1992. BWF HQ: Kuala Lumpur.
ShotWhat it does

Basketball

  • A standard match has four periods (quarters). PYQ
  • Backboard size: 1.80 m × 1.05 m. PYQ

Kabaddi

  • Maximum players on the field per team: 7. PYQ

  • Antis = the defenders in the court. PYQ
  • CGL+ Pro Kabaddi League began 2014. India won every Asian Games men's kabaddi gold except 2018 (Iran). Kabaddi World Cups 2004, 2007, 2016 — all won by India.
