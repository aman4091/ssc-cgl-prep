World Happiness Report (asked 4 times)

  • Factor common to Happiness Index and HDI: life expectancy. PYQ HDI uses health (life expectancy), education and income per head. CGL+
  • Not a happiness factor: political stability. PYQ
  • India's rank in 2025: 118 of 147 (up from 126th in 2024). PYQ Finland 1st for the 8th year running.
  • India's 2025 rank was worse than in 2015 (117th) even with GDP roughly doubling — growth alone does not make people happier. PYQ

Industry, business and connectivity

  • Incentive on incremental sales of goods made in India → PLI scheme (2020). PYQ 4–6% incentive; 14 sectors (mobiles, pharma, auto, solar PV, ACC batteries, textiles…).
  • Women's entrepreneurship finance → Stand-Up India (5 April 2016). PYQ Bank loans ₹10 lakh–₹1 crore to at least one SC/ST and one woman borrower per branch, for greenfield units. Budget 2025–26: term loans up to ₹2 crore for 5 lakh first-time women, SC and ST entrepreneurs.
  • UDAN (Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik, 2016): modified UDAN to connect 120 new destinations and carry 4 crore more passengers in 10 years (Budget 2025–26). PYQ
  • Free LPG connections to women of poor households → Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana, launched 1 May 2016 at Ballia (UP). PYQ 10 crore+ connections; Ujjwala 2.0 from 2021.

Tribal development

  • PM-JUGA (Pradhan Mantri Janjatiya Unnat Gram Abhiyan, approved Sept 2024): saturation-based, convergent, participatory approach with last-mile delivery through many ministries. PYQ About 63,000 tribal-majority villages, 17 ministries, 25 interventions. Later named Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan.
  • PVTGs are identified by pre-agricultural technology, stagnant or falling population, very low literacy and subsistence economy; no constitutional mechanism for periodic review. PYQ
  • CGL+ "Primitive tribes" category came from the Dhebar Commission (1960–61), first listed 1973; renamed PVTG in 2006. Now 75 PVTGs. Examples: Jarawa, Onge, Sentinelese (A&N), Baiga, Sahariya, Chenchu. PM-JANMAN (Nov 2023, ~₹24,000 crore) is the scheme meant only for PVTGs.
⚠ Answer-key noteThe PM-JUGA option says it "targets PVTGs". The chosen option is the best of the four, but strictly, PM-JUGA covers tribal-majority villages in general; the PVTG-only scheme is PM-JANMAN (2023).

Education and literacy

  • NEP 2020: mother tongue / regional language as medium at least till Class 5, preferably till Class 8, in line with Article 350A; hurdles are language diversity and resources. PYQ
  • CGL+ Art 350A = primary instruction in mother tongue for linguistic minority children (not only STs). Art 29 = minorities may conserve language, script, culture. NEP 2020 brought the 5+3+3+4 structure and keeps the three-language formula.
  • India's literacy rate in 2025 (estimated): about 79% (as per key). PYQ Census 2011: 74.04% (male 82.1%, female 65.5%); Kerala highest, Bihar lowest. CGL+
