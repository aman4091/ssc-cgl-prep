Gravity, Sky-Watching, Light & Motion

Selection Post Phase-XIV 2026 · 16–26 Sep · most asked: gravity keeps planets in orbit (×3), rotation vs revolution, mirror/lens numericals

Gravity runs the solar system

  • Planets stay in stable orbits because of gravitational attraction of the Sun. PYQ ×2 Without it, inertia would send Earth off in a straight line.
  • Earth keeps a nearly constant distance because inertia (go straight) and the Sun's pull (come inward) cancel out: gravitational balance. PYQ Gravity supplies the centripetal force.
  • Newton's law: F = G·m₁·m₂ / r², G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg². CGL+
  • If gravity became twice as strong, Earth would grip its air harder, so atmospheric pressure would rise sharply. PYQ Weight doubles too, so rockets would find escape harder.

Rotation or revolution? The classic pair

  • Stars shifting position during a night → Earth's rotation on its axis. PYQ
  • Different constellations in different seasons → Earth's revolution around the Sun. PYQ
  • CGL+ 88 official constellations (IAU). Saptarishi (Ursa Major) points to the Pole Star, which looks fixed because it sits almost above Earth's axis. Orion is a winter constellation. 12 zodiac constellations lie along the ecliptic.

Our galaxy, brightest star, red planet

  • Our solar system is in the Milky Way Galaxy PYQ — a barred spiral, Hindi name Akashganga. The Sun sits in the Orion Arm, ~26,000 light-years from the centre, where the black hole Sagittarius A* lies. CGL+
  • Nearest big galaxy: Andromeda. Orion Nebula is a star-forming cloud, not a galaxy. CGL+
  • Brightest star of the night sky: Sirius (Dog Star, in Canis Major). PYQ Nearest star after the Sun: Proxima Centauri (~4.24 ly). Polaris is useful, not brightest. Betelgeuse = red supergiant in Orion. CGL+
  • Mars is the Red Planet — iron oxide (rust) in its soil. PYQ

Eclipses and atmospheric refraction

  • Cause of eclipses: alignment of Sun, Earth and Moon. PYQ
  • CGL+ Solar eclipse types: total, partial, annular ("ring of fire"). Umbra = full shadow, penumbra = part shadow. A totally eclipsed Moon looks red ("Blood Moon") because Earth's air bends red light onto it.
  • Light bending in layers of air of different density = refraction. PYQ Results: stars twinkle (planets don't), Sun visible ~2 min before real sunrise and ~2 min after sunset. CGL+

Mirrors and lenses: two solved numericals

Concave mirror, h = 1 cm, u = −24, f = −16 PYQ 1/v = 1/f − 1/u = −1/16 + 1/24 = −1/48 → v = −48 cm. m = −v/u = −2. Image: inverted, 2.0 cm (real, enlarged). Object between F and C → image beyond C.
Convex lens, h = 4 cm, u = −24, f = +10 PYQ 1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/10 − 1/24 = 7/120 → v = +17.14 cm. m = v/u = −0.714 → height 2.86 cm. Answer: 17.14 cm, 2.86 cm, real and inverted. Object beyond 2F → image between F and 2F, smaller.
  • Object between P and F of a concave mirror: image is behind the mirror and virtual-erect → both 1 and 2 correct. PYQ That is why it is used as a shaving / dentist mirror.
  • CGL+ Mirror m = −v/u; lens m = v/u. Negative m = inverted. Lens power P = 1/f (metre), unit dioptre; f = 10 cm → +10 D. Convex lens = converging (+), concave = diverging (−).
  • CGL+ Convex mirror → rear-view mirror (always virtual, erect, small; wide view). Plane mirror → same size, laterally inverted.

Why things shine or conduct

  • Diamond sparkles because its high refractive index allows dispersion and total internal reflection. PYQ RI ≈ 2.42, critical angle only ≈ 24°.
  • CGL+ TIR needs light going from denser to rarer medium beyond the critical angle; used in optical fibres. RI: water 1.33, glass ~1.5.
  • Metals conduct because they have free electrons that move easily. PYQ Best conductor: silver, then copper, gold, aluminium. Graphite is a non-metal that conducts. CGL+

Motion and energy

  • Remove all friction → the object keeps moving forever at constant velocity (Newton's 1st law, law of inertia). PYQ
  • CGL+ 2nd law F = ma; 3rd law action = reaction. Friction order: static > sliding > rolling. Ball bearings and lubricants cut friction.
Spring numerical PYQ All KE becomes spring energy: ½mv² = ½kx² → ½ × 2 × 36 = ½ × k × 0.04 → 36 = 0.02k → k = 1800 N/m. Hooke's law: F = −kx. CGL+

Heredity and Mendel

  • Passing traits from parents to children = heredity. PYQ Differences among members of a species = variation.
  • Rules of inheritance from pea plants: Gregor Mendel, the "Father of Genetics". PYQ
  • CGL+ Pea = Pisum sativum, 7 pairs of traits. Laws: Dominance, Segregation, Independent Assortment. Ratios: monohybrid 3:1 (genotype 1:2:1), dihybrid 9:3:3:1. Rediscovered in 1900 by de Vries, Correns, Tschermak. "Genetics" word — Bateson; "gene" — Johannsen.

How DNA was found

  • DNA first isolated as "nuclein" in 1869 by Friedrich Miescher (spelt "Meischer" in the options). PYQ
  • CGL+ Watson, Crick and Wilkins got the Nobel in 1962; Rosalind Franklin's X-ray "Photo 51" was the key evidence.

Hershey–Chase experiment (asked twice)

  • Why bacteria became radioactive only with labelled phosphorus: phosphorus is in DNA but not in proteins. PYQ
  • Purpose of radioactive phosphorus: to label viral DNA. PYQ Sulphur labels protein (cysteine, methionine have S; DNA has none).
⚠ Answer-key noteThe question prints the isotope as "35P". The correct isotope is ³²P (phosphorus-32) for DNA; ³⁵S (sulphur-35) was used for protein. The answer itself (P is in DNA, not protein) is right.
Trick: P = Pure DNA, S = Shell (protein coat).

DNA structure: pairs, charges, start signal

  • Adenine–Thymine are held by two hydrogen bonds; G–C by three (so G–C rich DNA melts at a higher temperature). PYQ
  • Chargaff: A = T, G = C. If A = 20%, then T = 20%, and G = (100 − 40) ÷ 2 = 30%. PYQ
  • Nucleosome: negatively charged DNA wrapped around a positively charged histone octamer. PYQ DNA is negative because of phosphate; histones are positive (rich in lysine, arginine). About 200 bp per nucleosome; "beads on a string". CGL+
  • Phosphorus is essential because it forms the backbone of nucleic acids. PYQ It is also in ATP, phospholipids and bones (calcium phosphate). Phosphorus cycle has no gas phase. CGL+
  • Promoter: binding site for RNA polymerase, where transcription starts. PYQ Transcription unit = promoter + structural gene + terminator. Central dogma DNA → RNA → protein (Crick). RNA uses uracil instead of thymine. CGL+

Biotechnology and useful microbes

  • Herbal medicine is NOT a biotech product. PYQ Insulin from E. coli, Golden rice and recombinant Hepatitis-B vaccine are.
  • Golden rice makes beta-carotene (Vitamin A precursor). PYQ Fights Vitamin A deficiency (night blindness). Made by Potrykus and Beyer. CGL+
  • Bt gene comes from the soil bacterium Bacillus thuringiensis. PYQ Its cry protein kills bollworms; toxin activates only in the insect's alkaline gut. Bt cotton approved in India in 2002 — the only GM crop grown commercially here. CGL+
  • Yogurt bacteria: Streptococcus thermophilus (with Lactobacillus bulgaricus). PYQ
  • Regenerative medicine aims to repair or replace damaged tissues using stem cells. PYQ iPS cells — Shinya Yamanaka, Nobel 2012. CGL+

Energy in the cell

  • Glucose → pyruvate (glycolysis, EMP pathway) happens in the cytoplasm. PYQ
  • Correct match: anaerobic respiration – yeast. PYQ Trap: lactic acid goes with muscles, alcoholic with yeast — the options swap them.
  • Enzymes act as catalysts that lower activation energy; they are not used up. PYQ Lock-and-key — Emil Fischer; induced fit — Koshland. Pepsin works in acid, trypsin in alkali. CGL+
  • During apoptosis (programmed cell death), cytochrome c is released by the mitochondria. PYQ Lysosome = "suicide bag"; necrosis = accidental death. Mitochondria have own DNA and 70S ribosomes. CGL+
  • Studied in microbiology but NOT a prokaryote: fungi (true nucleus; wall of chitin). PYQ Bacteria and cyanobacteria are prokaryotes; viruses are not cells.

Classification ladder

  • CGL+ Linnaeus — father of taxonomy, binomial names. Five kingdoms — R.H. Whittaker (1969). Arthropoda = largest animal phylum. Species = basic unit.
