Food chain, energy pyramid and poison that climbs up

  • Toxic chemicals piling up more and more at higher levels of a food chain = Biomagnification. PYQ Inside one organism over its life = bioaccumulation. Classic toxins: DDT, mercury. CGL+
  • CGL+ Energy flow is one-way; nutrients cycle. Pyramid of energy is always upright. Pyramid of numbers for a single tree, and pyramid of biomass in the sea, can be inverted.
  • CGL+ Decomposers (bacteria, fungi) return nutrients to soil. Green plants = producers = autotrophs.

Plants and gases

  • Gas given out in photosynthesis: Oxygen. PYQ 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (needs sunlight and chlorophyll).
  • Loss of water from plants as vapour = Transpiration, mainly through stomata. PYQ It cools the leaf and pulls water up from roots.

Layers of the atmosphere

  • Ozone layer, which absorbs harmful UV rays, is in the Stratosphere. PYQ ×2
  • Troposphere = the lowest layer of the atmosphere. PYQ Thin at poles (~8 km), thick at the equator (~18 km). CGL+
  • Layer that protects us from meteoroids (they burn up there): Mesosphere. PYQ
  • CGL+ Air by volume: nitrogen ~78%, oxygen ~21%, argon ~0.93%, CO₂ ~0.04%. Ozone thickness is measured in Dobson units.

Renewable or not?

  • Renewable: Solar energy PYQ ×3 · Wind energy PYQ. Non-renewable: Coal PYQ.
  • Main part of natural gas: Methane PYQ. Main part of biogas: Methane PYQ (about 50–75%, rest mostly CO₂). Biogas comes from dung and wet waste broken down without oxygen.
  • CGL+ CNG is mostly methane. LPG is mainly propane + butane.

Waste habits and food keeping

  • Turning waste into new materials = Recycling. PYQ The 3 Rs in order: Reduce → Reuse → Recycle.
  • A sustainable practice: Rainwater harvesting (not plastic bags, overfishing or deforestation). PYQ
  • Food preserved by low moisture so microbes can't grow: Dehydration (drying). PYQ
MethodHow it stops microbes
Dehydration / dryingRemoves water
RefrigerationLow temperature slows growth

PasteurisationMild heat (milk ~70 °C for 15–30 s, then chilled); named after Louis Pasteur CGL+

Check yourself

Meteors burn up in which layer?

Mesosphere, the coldest layer.

Biomagnification vs bioaccumulation?

Biomagnification: toxin rises across the food chain. Bioaccumulation: toxin builds up inside one organism.

Main gas in both natural gas and biogas?

Methane.

Water and sunlight: biotic or abiotic?

Abiotic (non-living).

Pollution & Climate Change

Most asked: CO₂ as the main greenhouse gas, CFCs and the Montreal Protocol, acid rain, eutrophication, biodegradable waste

The greenhouse effect

  • Gas most responsible for the greenhouse effect: Carbon dioxide. PYQ ×3
  • Another major greenhouse gas: Methane. PYQ Released by cows and other ruminants (gut fermentation): Methane. PYQ
  • NOT a greenhouse gas: Nitrogen. PYQ
  • Primary cause of global warming: the greenhouse effect. PYQ Biggest human contributor: burning of fossil fuels. PYQ Also a major cause: deforestation (fewer trees to absorb CO₂). PYQ

What warming does

EffectWhy
Rising sea levels PYQWarm water expands + glaciers and ice sheets melt
Himalayan glaciers shrinking — cause: global warming PYQHimalaya is called the "Third Pole" CGL+
Coral bleaching — cause: rising ocean temperatures PYQStressed corals throw out their partner algae (zooxanthellae) and turn white
Ocean acidification — cause: oceans absorbing CO₂ PYQCO₂ + water → carbonic acid; pH falls; shells and corals weaken
  • Kyoto Protocol's main purpose: to reduce greenhouse gas emissions. PYQ Adopted 1997, in force 2005; binding targets only for developed (Annex I) countries. CGL+

Ozone hole and acid rain: two different problems

  • Ozone layer depletion is caused mainly by Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs). PYQ ×3
  • Treaty to protect the ozone layer: Montreal Protocol (1987). PYQ ×2 CGL+ Its Kigali Amendment (2016) phases down HFCs. World Ozone Day: 16 September. The ozone hole is most marked over Antarctica.
  • Acid rain is caused by sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides. PYQ ×2 CGL+ Yellowing of the Taj Mahal is called "marble cancer".
Trick: "Montreal = Mask for ozone; Kyoto = Karbon cuts."

Air and noise pollution

  • Gas from incomplete burning of fuel: Carbon monoxide. PYQ It binds haemoglobin far more strongly than oxygen, so it suffocates. CGL+
  • A secondary pollutant: Ozone (ground-level). PYQ Primary pollutants come straight out of a source (CO, SO₂, NOₓ). Secondary ones form in the air by reaction (O₃, PAN, photochemical smog). CGL+
  • Major source of air pollution in cities: Vehicular emissions. PYQ
  • Delhi's air pollution: vehicles + industry + crop (stubble) burning = All of the above. PYQ Winter temperature inversion traps the smog.
  • Major source of noise pollution: Traffic. PYQ Noise is measured in decibels (dB). CGL+

Water pollution and eutrophication

  • Water over-rich in nutrients, leading to excess algae: Eutrophication. PYQ ×2
  • Main source of water pollution in urban areas: Sewage discharge. PYQ
  • A major cause of water pollution (other options were eco-friendly): Industrial waste. PYQ
  • CGL+ Minamata disease = mercury; Itai-itai = cadmium; blue-baby syndrome = nitrate in drinking water; fluorosis = excess fluoride.

Soil, land and waste

  • Major cause of soil erosion, and the main result of deforestation: Deforestation → soil erosion. PYQ ×2 Tree roots hold soil; without them rain washes topsoil away.
  • Primary cause of soil pollution: chemical fertilisers and pesticides. PYQ
  • Fertile land turning into desert: Desertification. PYQ CGL+ UN treaty: UNCCD (1994); Desertification Day: 17 June.
Biodegradable (microbes break it down)Non-biodegradable (stays for centuries)
Paper PYQ ×2, vegetable peels PYQ, food waste, wood, cottonPlastic PYQ, glass, metal cans, DDT

Earthquakes made by humans

In the past decade there has been a substantial increase in earthquakes triggered by injection and production of underground fluids. PYQ Pumping wastewater deep down raises pressure on faults and makes them slip. This is called induced seismicity.

Check yourself

Which treaty is for the ozone layer, and which for greenhouse gases?

Montreal (1987) for ozone; Kyoto (1997) and Paris (2015) for greenhouse gases.

Ground-level ozone: primary or secondary pollutant?

Secondary. It forms from NOₓ and hydrocarbons in sunlight.

Most abundant natural greenhouse gas vs most responsible one?

Water vapour is the most abundant; CO₂ is the most responsible for warming.

Two gases behind acid rain?

Sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides.

Biodiversity & Conservation

Biodiversity hotspots

  • A biodiversity hotspot in India: Western Ghats (not Thar, Gangetic plains or Deccan). PYQ
  • Key feature of a hotspot: high levels of endemism. PYQ
  • Endemic to the Western Ghats: Lion-tailed macaque. PYQ

IUCN Red List ladder

  • Red List of Threatened Species is published by IUCN. PYQ CGL+ IUCN founded 1948, HQ Gland, Switzerland.
  • Red Data Book's purpose: to list endangered species. PYQ
  • Great Indian Bustard = Critically Endangered PYQ; it is a threatened bird species PYQ. State bird of Rajasthan; found in Desert National Park; killed by power lines.
  • Critically endangered species in India (vs peacock, elephant, tiger): Gharial — fish-eating crocodile of the Chambal. PYQ
  • Critically endangered mammal in the options: Pygmy Hog — world's smallest wild pig, survives in Manas (Assam). PYQ
  • Threatened species (vs dog, cow, cat): Red Panda — Endangered; state animal of Sikkim. PYQ
⚠ Answer-key noteBook says the Pygmy Hog is "Critically Endangered". It was CR earlier, but IUCN moved it to Endangered in 2008. Among the given options it is still the right pick (the rarest one), so mark Pygmy Hog.

Laws and treaties for biodiversity

  • Wildlife Protection Act, 1972: to protect wild animals and plants. PYQ Protected species are listed in Schedules (the 2022 amendment cut them to 4). CGL+
  • Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD): to promote sustainable development — its three aims are conservation, sustainable use, and fair sharing of benefits from genetic resources. PYQ Signed at Rio, 1992. Trade in endangered species is CITES (1973), not CBD. CGL+
  • Biodiversity Heritage Site: Nallur Tamarind Grove (Bengaluru), India's first, 2007. PYQ Declared by state governments under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002; the National Biodiversity Authority sits in Chennai. CGL+

Forest, plants and animals asked

  • Highest forest cover by area: Madhya Pradesh. PYQ Highest by percentage: Mizoram. Next by area: Arunachal Pradesh, Chhattisgarh. CGL+
  • State animal of Madhya Pradesh: Barasingha (hard-ground swamp deer of Kanha). PYQ
  • A deer found in India: Chital (spotted deer). PYQ Moose, impala and gazelle are foreign. CGL+ Other Indian deer: sambar (largest), hangul (Kashmir stag, Dachigam), sangai (Manipur).
  • Ayurvedic medicinal plants Neem, Tulsi, Ashwagandha: All of the above. PYQ
  • Himalayan medicinal plant: Yew (Taxus) — gives taxol, an anti-cancer drug. PYQ CGL+ Sarpagandha (Rauwolfia) gives reserpine for high blood pressure; cinchona gives quinine for malaria.

Wetlands and the Ramsar Convention

Ramsar Convention — conservation of wetlands PYQ Main purpose: conservation of wetlands. Signed 1971 at Ramsar, Iran. India joined in 1982. CGL+ World Wetlands Day: 2 February.

  • Wetland of international importance (vs Pushkar, Naini, Dal): Chilika Lake, Odisha. PYQ India's first Ramsar sites (1981): Chilika and Keoladeo. Chilika = Asia's largest brackish-water lagoon. CGL+
