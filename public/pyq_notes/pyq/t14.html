The Thar Desert

Why is the Thar dry? PYQ It lies northwest of the Aravallis, in their rain shadow. CGL+ Luni is the only major river; it ends in the Rann of Kutch. Small salt lakes are called playas; dunes are called barchans.

The Northern Plains

  • Made by the Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra. They are flat, very fertile, densely populated and flood-prone. "Uneven terrain with many hills" is NOT true of them. PYQ

Earth's Interior, Plate Tectonics and Earthquakes

Steno 2026 · 9–15 Sep · most asked: Gutenberg discontinuity, why the inner core is solid, mantle convection, seismic zones

Layers and boundaries (depth to scale)

  • Boundary between mantle and outer core, with a sudden drop in seismic wave speed: Gutenberg discontinuity. PYQ
  • The inner core is solid although very hot because huge pressure at the centre raises the melting point above the actual temperature. PYQ The outer core has less pressure, so it stays liquid; its motion makes Earth's magnetic field.
  • Most abundant mineral in the continental crust: feldspar (about half of the crust), then quartz. PYQ
  • CGL+ Elements in the crust: O (46.6%) > Si (27.7%) > Al (8.1%) > Fe (5%). Core is mostly nickel + iron (NIFE); crust layers: SIAL (continents), SIMA (ocean floor).
  • CGL+ P-waves (primary) travel through solids and liquids; S-waves (secondary) only through solids; surface (L) waves do the most damage. Instrument: seismograph. Point inside = focus (hypocentre); point above it on the surface = epicentre.

Plate tectonics

  • Best summary of the theory: plates of the lithosphere move over the asthenosphere, driven by slab pull, ridge push and mantle convection, which creates trenches, rift valleys and faults. PYQ
  • Most direct cause of plate movement: convection currents in the mantle. PYQ
  • Traps in the options: the Himalayas did not form by ocean–continent subduction; they formed by continent–continent collision (Indian + Eurasian plates). Continents are not "geologically stable".
  • CGL+ Continental Drift: Alfred Wegener (1912), supercontinent Pangaea, ocean Panthalassa. Convection currents: Arthur Holmes (1930s). Sea-floor spreading: Harry Hess (1960s).

Earthquakes in India

  • Most earthquake-prone region: the Himalayan belt — the Indian plate still pushes north into Eurasia by about 4–5 cm a year. PYQ The Deccan plateau is an old, stable block.
  • Correct statement on preparedness: western and north-eastern regions lie in high zones, and the real weakness is poor enforcement of building codes, outdated land-use planning and little retrofitting. PYQ Wrong options claimed microzonation is complete everywhere or early warning exists in all states.
  • CGL+ BIS seismic map (IS 1893) has zones II to V (Zone I was merged). Zone V = North-East, J&K/Ladakh, Himachal, Uttarakhand, Rann of Kutch, Andaman & Nicobar. Big quakes: Bhuj 2001, Kashmir 2005, Nepal 2015.
  • CGL+ Richter scale measures magnitude (energy); Mercalli scale measures intensity (damage seen).

Solifluction

Soil that flows PYQ Solifluction = slow, gravity-driven flow of water-soaked soil over a frozen (permafrost) layer in periglacial, tundra and high-mountain areas. In summer the top layer thaws; the ice below does not let water drain away, so the wet soil creeps downhill and forms lobes and terraces. CGL+ Other mass movements: creep (very slow), landslide (fast), mudflow, rockfall.

Climate: Pressure Belts, Monsoon, Jet Streams, El Niño

Steno 2026 · 9–15 Sep · all four asked "explain the mechanism" type questions: ITCZ, monsoon reversal, jet streams, El Niño

Pressure belts and planetary winds

  • Doldrums / ITCZ = a low-pressure belt near the equator where the trade winds of both hemispheres meet, giving thunderstorms, calm or variable winds; its northward shift affects monsoon onset in South Asia. PYQ
  • CGL+ Winds bend because of the Coriolis force — to the right in the Northern Hemisphere, left in the Southern (Ferrel's law). It is zero at the equator.

Why the monsoon reverses

PYQ Main cause of the reversal of monsoon winds: differential heating of land and sea.

⚠ Answer-key noteThe official key marks "Young fold mountains". The standard (NCERT) answer is differential heating of land and sea: the land heats and cools faster than the ocean, so pressure over India flips between seasons and so does the wind. The Himalayas shape and hold the monsoon but do not cause the reversal. Learn the textbook answer; if the same question repeats, check whether "differential heating" is among the options.

CGL+ Monsoon onset over Kerala: around 1 June. "Mango showers" = pre-monsoon rain in Kerala/Karnataka; "Kalbaisakhi" = Bengal's evening storms; "Loo" = hot dry wind of north India. Mawsynram (Meghalaya) gets the most rain.

Universe, Solar System, Moon and Seasons

Steno 2026 · 9–15 Sep · from basic (Mercury, Moon) to tough (ΛCDM, quasars, CMB, retrograde motion)

Origin and make-up of the universe

  • The Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) supports the Big Bang theory. PYQ It is leftover heat from the early universe, now about 2.7 K. Found by Penzias and Wilson (1965; Nobel 1978). CGL+
  • The CMB is seen with a radio telescope (microwaves are part of the radio band). PYQ Satellites that mapped it: COBE, WMAP, Planck. CGL+
  • ΛCDM (Lambda Cold Dark Matter) is the standard model of the universe. The incorrect statement: "dark matter is made of hot, relativistic particles" — in ΛCDM dark matter is cold (slow). PYQ
  • Quasars = active galactic nuclei powered by supermassive black holes; they can outshine their whole galaxy. PYQ

CGL+ Edwin Hubble (1929) showed galaxies move away — the universe is expanding. Age ≈ 13.8 billion years. Rival idea: Steady-State theory (Hoyle). Stars: a dead star below 1.4 solar masses (Chandrasekhar limit) becomes a white dwarf; heavier ones become neutron stars (pulsars) or black holes.

Heat and thermodynamics

Evolution

  • Homologous pair: flippers of a whale and forelimbs of a human. PYQ
  • Marsupials (pouched mammals): they diversified separately in Australasia and South America, often filling the same niches as placental mammals through convergent (parallel) evolution. PYQ Examples: kangaroo, koala, opossum; marsupial mole, flying phalanger vs placental mole, flying squirrel.
  • CGL+ Mammal groups: monotremes lay eggs (platypus, echidna); marsupials raise young in a pouch; placentals nourish young through a placenta. Vestigial organs in humans: appendix, wisdom teeth, tail bone (coccyx).

Vinegar

  • Incorrect statement about vinegar: "mixing it with baking soda makes a strong alkaline solution that cleans better". PYQ
  • True statements: acetic acid preserves food by lowering pH; vinegar is made when Acetobacter oxidises ethanol in air; it dissolves limescale (calcium carbonate).
  • CGL+ Vinegar ≈ 5–8% acetic acid (ethanoic acid, CH₃COOH). Baking soda = sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO₃); washing soda = Na₂CO₃·10H₂O; baking powder = baking soda + tartaric acid.

Water

PYQ Chemical formula of water: H₂O — two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen. CGL+ "Universal solvent"; polar; boils at 100 °C at sea level; densest at 4 °C (so ice floats). Heavy water D₂O is a moderator in nuclear reactors. Hard water has calcium and magnesium salts.
