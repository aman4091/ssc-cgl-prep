Five building styles to tell apart

  • Ribbed vaults, pointed arches, flying buttresses, stained glass = Gothic. PYQ
  • Correct pair: Qutub Minar – Indo-Islamic. PYQ (Khajuraho is Nagara, India Gate is British, Lotus Temple is modern.)
  • Provincial styles: Bengal and Jaunpur had their own styles ✔; regional styles mixed Islamic forms with local art ✔; "completely uniform" ✘ → 1 and 2 only. PYQ
  • Gujarat: used local temple ideas ✔; mosques and tombs carry toranas and bell motifs ✔; "avoided local influence" ✘ → 1 and 2 only. PYQ
  • Kailasa Temple (Ellora, 8th c., Rashtrakuta Krishna I) is in Maharashtra. PYQ
  • "Taj Mahal of the South" (from the options) = Meenakshi Temple, Madurai. PYQ
Don't confuse the "Tajs" CGL+ Bibi ka Maqbara (Aurangabad) = "Taj of the Deccan" / "poor man's Taj". The Meenakshi answer above is what SSC's key accepted among its four options.
