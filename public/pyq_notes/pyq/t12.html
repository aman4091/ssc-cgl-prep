Dances: Classical & Folk

Steno 2026 · 9–15 Sep · most asked: Manipuri (×3), Bharatanatyam (×3), folk dances of Karnataka, Haryana and Rajasthan

The eight classical dances

Bharatanatyam (asked 3 times)

  • What sets it apart: temple origins, expressive gestures and rhythmic footwork. PYQ It grew out of the devadasi dance called Sadir.
  • Heavily based on the Natya Shastra (Bharata) and Abhinaya Darpana (Nandikeshvara), and uses sanchari bhavas (many passing moods inside one theme) → Bharatanatyam. PYQ
  • Rukmini Devi Arundale revived Bharatanatyam and brought it into formal schooling: Kalakshetra, Chennai, 1936. PYQ First woman nominated to the Rajya Sabha (1952). CGL+
  • Margam (order of a recital): alarippu → jatiswaram → shabdam → varnam → padam → tillana. CGL+ Basic half-sitting pose: aramandi; steps: adavus.

Manipuri and Mohiniyattam

  • Manipuri Ras Leela differs from Kathak/Bharatanatyam by bhakti rasa, soft rounded movements, little facial expression, circular patterns, Radha–Krishna love. PYQ No ankle bells; stiff barrel skirt called potloi. Started by King Rajarshi Bhagyachandra (18th c.).
  • Statements on Manipuri: "Kartal Cholom uses cymbals" ✔, "Sankirtana uses no instruments" ✘ → 1 only. "Sankirtana is a main style" ✔, "Ras Leela is not part of Manipuri" ✘ → 1 only. PYQ ×2
  • Manipuri Sankirtana = ritual singing, drumming (pung) and dancing; UNESCO Intangible Heritage 2013. Pung Cholom = drum dance, Kartal Cholom = cymbal dance. CGL+
  • Mohiniyattam (Kerala, white-and-gold kasavu saree) = feminine grace (lasya). PYQ Revived by poet Vallathol at Kerala Kalamandalam (1930). CGL+
Trick: "Manipuri = mann ki bhakti" — soft, round, no bells. Bharatanatyam = "temple + footwork + mudra".

Two streams of classical music

  • The two principal traditions: Carnatic and Hindustani. PYQ
  • Correct pair: Hindustani music – developed under Turko-Persian influence (Delhi Sultanate, Mughal courts, Amir Khusrau, Tansen). PYQ

Hindustani vocal forms

  • One raga developed in depth, slow elaborate alaap, then complex talas → Dhrupad. Pair "Dhrupad – one of the oldest and most austere forms" is the correct pair. PYQ ×2 Patron: Raja Man Singh Tomar of Gwalior; singers Tansen, Baiju Bawra. CGL+
  • 18th century, flexible, most improvisation, most popular today → Khayal ("imagination"). PYQ Rose at Muhammad Shah Rangila's court.
  • From Punjab, sung by camel riders, fast intricate taans → Tappa. PYQ
  • Wrong definitions in the "correct pair" question: alaap is slow and unmetred (not fast); bandish is a fixed composition (not improvisation); tarana is vocal with syllables (not instrumental).

Instruments by type

  • Pakhawaj (barrel-shaped, two-headed drum) is a membranophone. PYQ
  • Its classic partner: Dhrupad vocal style (also Kathak and Odissi). In khayal, the tabla took its place. PYQ

Carnatic music

  • "Pitamaha of Karnataka Sangeeta" → Purandara Dasa. PYQ He fixed Mayamalavagowla as the beginner's raga and graded lessons (sarali varisai, alankaras, geetams).
  • Venkatamakhin's system (Chaturdandi Prakashika) → Melakarta system. PYQ
  • "72 melakartas are parent scales" ✔; "melakartas come from janya ragas" ✘ (it is the reverse) → Only 1. PYQ
  • Kshetrayya's padams → Sringara rasa with nayika–nayaka bhava (love, addressed to Muvva Gopala). PYQ Used as abhinaya pieces in Bharatanatyam and Kuchipudi.
  • Andhra-based compositions stand out by predominant use of Telugu, flowing and gamaka-rich. PYQ Even Tyagaraja (Tamil Nadu) composed in Telugu.
  • CGL+ All three of the Trinity were born in Tiruvarur. The Tyagaraja Aradhana festival is held at Thiruvaiyaru. Annamacharya sang for Lord Venkateswara.

Sikh festivals

  • Hola Mohalla is held at Anandpur Sahib (Punjab), on the day after Holi. Started by Guru Gobind Singh, 1701. PYQ ×2

How the Buddha appeared in art

  • Human (anthropomorphic) Buddha first appeared in the Gandhara and Mathura schools. PYQ
  • Early stupas had no human Buddha because of the aniconic tradition of early Buddhism (out of reverence, not a ban or lack of artisans). PYQ

Painting

  • Famous Ajanta painting: Padmapani Bodhisattva (Cave 1). PYQ "Descent of the Ganges" (Arjuna's Penance) is a Pallava rock relief at Mahabalipuram, not Ajanta.
  • Ajanta was mainly carved under the Gupta and Vakataka period (main phase under Vakataka king Harishena, 5th c.; early caves Satavahana era). PYQ About 30 Buddhist caves near Aurangabad; UNESCO 1983 with Ellora. CGL+
  • Prehistoric rock paintings used black, white and red ochre. PYQ Best site: Bhimbetka (MP), found by V.S. Wakankar (1957), UNESCO 2003. CGL+
  • Amrita Sher-Gil → Painting ("Three Girls", "Bride's Toilet"; born Budapest 1913). PYQ
  • CGL+ Other painters: Raja Ravi Varma (oil paintings of epics), Abanindranath Tagore ("Bharat Mata", Bengal School), Nandalal Bose (illustrated the Constitution), M.F. Husain.

Rajput, Mughal & North-East buildings

  • Chhatri in Rajput architecture = commemorative structure symbolising honour (cenotaph at cremation site; e.g. Bada Bagh, Jaisalmer; Gaitore, Jaipur). PYQ
  • Rajput forts beat long sieges with stepwells for a steady water supply (baoris, tanks inside the walls). PYQ Hill Forts of Rajasthan (Chittorgarh, Kumbhalgarh, Ranthambore, Gagron, Amber, Jaisalmer) = UNESCO 2013. CGL+
  • Taj Mahal symmetry and pietra dura (parchin kari) inlay → Persian and Indian traditions. PYQ Built by Shah Jahan; architect Ustad Ahmad Lahauri; Makrana marble. CGL+
  • Meitei architecture (Sanamahism, Manipur): mainly wooden, raised platforms, open-air shrines to nature deities. PYQ Sacred groves = Umanglai.

Language families of India

  • Statements: 1 (Indo-Aryan largest) ✔, 2 (Odisha only Indo-Aryan) ✘ — Odisha also has Munda (Santali, Ho) and Dravidian (Kui, Gondi) speakers, 3 (Tamil Nadu, Kerala mainly Dravidian) ✔ → Only 1 and 3. PYQ
  • CGL+ 22 languages in the Eighth Schedule. Classical languages: 11 — Tamil was first (2004); Marathi, Pali, Prakrit, Assamese and Bengali were added in 2024.

International Solar Alliance (asked 3 times)

  • Launched jointly by India and France at COP21, Paris, 30 November 2015 (PM Modi and President François Hollande). PYQ
  • Brings together nations that get consistent sunshine — the "sunshine countries" lying fully or partly between the Tropics of Cancer and Capricorn. Since 2020 any UN member can join. PYQ
  • India's support: solar project funding and technical aid — hosts the secretariat (Gurugram), Lines of Credit, training; pushes "One Sun One World One Grid". PYQ
  • CGL+ ISA is the first treaty-based intergovernmental organisation with its headquarters in India.

Corridors around Pakistan

  • Main block to India's land link with Afghanistan and Central Asia: unresolved border disputes with a key transit country (Pakistan). PYQ
  • Facility India is developing in Iran → Chabahar Port (Sistan-Baluchestan, Gulf of Oman); IPGL runs the Shahid Beheshti terminal on a 10-year deal signed in 2024. PYQ
  • IMEC connects India → Middle East → Europe. PYQ Partners: India, USA, UAE, Saudi Arabia, EU, France, Germany, Italy.
  • IMEC's edge over China's BRI: access to Europe through a US-backed, secure, rules-based network. PYQ

India in the Indian Ocean

  • SAGAR (Security and Growth for All in the Region; Mauritius, 2015) = maritime ties with Indian Ocean states. PYQ Expanded in 2025 (again in Mauritius) as MAHASAGAR, aimed at the Global South.
  • Ties with Seychelles, Mauritius and Maldives help monitor Sea Lines of Communication and give strategic depth against China's "String of Pearls" (Gwadar, Hambantota, Kyaukphyu, Djibouti). PYQ Examples: Agaléga (Mauritius), Assumption Island (Seychelles), Colombo Security Conclave.
  • True statement: Indian Ocean states come from Africa, Asia and Oceania; France and the UK hold territories (Réunion, Mayotte; Diego Garcia/Chagos). Russia does not touch it. PYQ
  • "Littoral state" = one with a sea coast → Mauritius. Kazakhstan, Mongolia and Switzerland are landlocked. PYQ
  • CGL+ In 2025 the UK agreed to hand the Chagos Islands to Mauritius, keeping a lease on the Diego Garcia base.
