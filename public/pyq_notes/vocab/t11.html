1 · "Cannot be ___": in-/im-/ir- + -able/-ible

The biggest group. Question pattern: "That which cannot be…". The prefix in-/im-/ir-/il- = not; the suffix -able/-ible = can be. So in-…-ible = "cannot be".

Invincible vs Invulnerable.

A decision that cannot be called back; a statement that cannot be disproved?

Irrevocable; Irrefutable.

Too great to be expressed in words?

Ineffable (not inedible, which is "cannot be eaten").

Burial vs confinement in war?

Interment (terra = earth) vs Internment.

The period between two reigns?

Interregnum.

One who dies without a will?

Intestate.

Enters a house uninvited vs meddles in affairs for own gain?

Intruder vs Interloper.

Does "inflammable" mean "will not burn"?

No. Inflammable = flammable = burns easily.

Narrow land joining two land masses?

Isthmus. (Narrow water joining two seas = strait.)

Shamelessly rude; too careless to plan for the future?

Impudent; Improvident.
