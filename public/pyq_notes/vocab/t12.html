Vocabulary · One Word Substitution · Black Book 1400–1823 · part 1

OWS (Black Book P–Z): People

Black Book OWS 1400–1823 (letters P to Z) · this part: "a person who…" words — criminals and betrayers, wanderers, ages, habits, jobs and sleep words
How to read these chapters The Black Book list runs alphabetically (Provender … Zoology). Here the words are regrouped by theme, so families stay together. Highlighted word with Q = the answer that was asked (Q ×2 = asked twice). Plain bold words = wrong options (distractors). In this book most distractors are other answers from the same page, so they are learnt in their own rows. CGL+ = a standard OWS of the same family, not in this list but common in SSC.
