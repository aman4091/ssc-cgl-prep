Vocabulary · 120 questions

One Word Substitution

First 120 questions of the bank (SSC CGL Sep–Oct 2025, Selection Post Phase XIII 2025) · most asked: "-ist" people (Hedonist, Fatalist, Cynic), -phobia, speech words, -logy
How to read this chapter Words are grouped by theme, not by question order. Each table gives the word, a short meaning, a Hinglish hint and the root. Highlighted word with Q = the answer that was asked (Q ×2 = asked twice). Plain bold words = the wrong options of those questions (distractors) — SSC reuses them as answers later. CGL+ = a standard OWS of the same family, not in these 120 but commonly asked.

Check yourself

Hedonist vs Stoic vs Cynic — which one believes everyone is selfish?

Cynic. Hedonist lives for pleasure; Stoic bears pain silently.

Killing one's brother, and killing a king?

Fratricide (frater = brother) and Regicide (rex = king).

Fear of darkness, fear of heights, fear of being touched?

Nyctophobia, Acrophobia, Haphephobia.

Government run by the military? By the rich? By corrupt thieves?

Stratocracy, Plutocracy, Kleptocracy.

Study of fish, and study of soil?

Ichthyology and Pedology.

Anagram or Palindrome: MADAM?

Palindrome (same both ways). LISTEN → SILENT is an anagram.

Speech without preparation vs speech alone on stage?

Extempore vs Soliloquy.

A story with animals that teaches a moral vs a short true story about a real person?

Fable vs Anecdote.

Arsonist or Pyromaniac — who sets fire on purpose as a crime?

Arsonist. A pyromaniac has a compulsive urge (a disorder).

Vegetarian vs Vegan — who avoids milk too?

Vegan. A vegetarian avoids only meat.
