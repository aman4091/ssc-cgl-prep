Synonyms bank items 1–382 (SSC CGL Sep–Oct 2025, Selection Post Phase XIII 2025, Steno 2025 and older CGL/CHSL) · this part: mood words — Rapture, Serene ×3, Pugnacious ×2, Loathe ×2, Amiable ×2, Cacophony ×2
How to read these chapters Words are grouped by meaning family, not by question order. Each row: Asked word → keyed synonym Q, then a short meaning, a Hinglish hint, and more synonyms / the opposite (opp.). Plain bold words in "Other options" rows are the wrong options of those questions — learn them too, SSC reuses them. CGL+ = a standard word of the same family, not asked in this range.

Check yourself

Synonym of Rapture?

Ecstasy (intense joy). Opposite: misery.

Serene, Quiescent, Insouciant — which one means "dormant"?

Quiescent. Serene = calm; Insouciant = carefree.

Mourn: Dread or Grieve?

Grieve. The book keyed "Dread", which is wrong.

Pugnacious — two keyed synonyms from this bank?

Belligerent and Waspish (quarrelsome, irritable).

Cacophony vs Mellifluous?

Cacophony = harsh noise (discord); Mellifluous = sweet, musical.

Allay and Abate — synonyms?

Allay → Pacify; Abate → Moderate (lessen).

Pusillanimous means…?

Cowardly. Opposite: courageous, gutsy.

Noxious vs Salutary?

Noxious = poisonous, harmful; Salutary = beneficial.

Alacrity?

Eagerness (brisk readiness). Opposite: sluggishness.

Entice — beguile or entreat?

Beguile (tempt, charm). Entreat = request earnestly.
