How to read these spelling chaptersEvery question was "Select the CORRECTLY spelt word". Words are grouped by spelling pattern, not by question order. Beginning but Benefited — why?

Begin is stressed on the last syllable (double n); benefit is stressed on the first (no double t).

Personnel.

One: -ful. But successfully has -fully (ll).
