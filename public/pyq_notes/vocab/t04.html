Antonyms 1: Kind vs cruel, praise vs blame

Antonyms bank, items 1–370 (SSC CGL 2025, Selection Post Phase XIII 2025, older PYQs) · most asked: Benevolent ×4, Pernicious ×4, Generous ×3, Ignominy ×2, Obdurate ×2
How to read these chapters Words are grouped by meaning family, not by question order. Each table gives the asked word, its meaning, a Hinglish hint and, in the last column, its opposite. Bold word + Q = the word that was asked (Q ×2 = asked twice). Highlighted = the keyed antonym. "syn:" lists the words that mean the same — in antonym questions these are exactly the wrong options, so learn them too. CGL+ = a standard word of the same family, not in these questions but commonly asked.
