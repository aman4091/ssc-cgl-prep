How to read this chapter Idioms are grouped by family, not by question order. Each table gives the idiom, a short meaning, a Hinglish hint and a note (origin, opposite or look-alike). Bold idiom with Q = it was asked (Q ×2 = asked twice). Highlighted = the keyed answer (the meaning that was marked right, or the idiom that filled the blank). Plain bold idioms without a tag = wrong options of those questions. CGL+ = a standard idiom of the same family, not in this range.
