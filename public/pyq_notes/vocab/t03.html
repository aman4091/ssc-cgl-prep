Check yourself

Amiable or Amicable: which one describes an agreement?

Amicable (an amicable settlement). Amiable describes a person.

Give the antonym of Benevolent.

Malevolent (wishing harm).

Arrange from weakest to strongest: furious, annoyed, indignant.

Annoyed → indignant → furious.

Synonym of Chide?

Scold (also rebuke, reprimand).

Which word means 'rude, reckless boldness': temerity or timidity?

Temerity. Timidity is shyness and fear.

Craven means…?

Cowardly. Its opposite: gallant, valiant.

Synonym of Propitious?

Auspicious (favourable).

Serendipity = ?

A lucky chance discovery — a godsend.

Flagrant vs Fragrant?

Flagrant = shockingly obvious (a flagrant violation). Fragrant = sweet-smelling.

Synonym of Culpable, and its root?

Guilty; L culpa = fault (same root as culprit).
