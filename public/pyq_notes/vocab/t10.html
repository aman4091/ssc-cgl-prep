Check yourself

Conformist vs Iconoclast — who attacks cherished beliefs?

Iconoclast. A conformist follows accepted practice.

A person who believes governments are unnecessary?

Anarchist (an = without, arkhos = ruler).

Gourmand or Gourmet — who simply loves to eat a lot?

Gourmand. A gourmet is a refined judge of food.

Virtuoso vs Virtuous?

Virtuoso = master of an art (music); virtuous = morally good.

A person who lends money at very high interest?

Usurer (usury).

One who compiles a dictionary, and one who makes maps?

Lexicographer and Cartographer.

Kidney specialist and eye specialist?

Nephrologist and Ophthalmologist.

A person in his seventies?

Septuagenarian (80s = octogenarian, 100+ = centenarian).

A leader who sways people by emotional speeches?

Demagogue.

A person unfairly blamed for others' faults?

Scapegoat.

Preface is written by the author; a foreword by someone else. Both come at the start.

Elegy vs Epitaph.

Posthumous publication.

Hyperbole.

Biennial. Biannual = twice a year.

Just beginning to develop?

Nascent.

Absolutely necessary, cannot do without?

Indispensable.

Malevolent; opposite Benevolent.

To put off work out of habit?
