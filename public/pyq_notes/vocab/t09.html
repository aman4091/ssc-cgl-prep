"Nothing can be known about God" vs "God does not exist"?

Agnostic vs Atheist. A Theist believes God exists.

One who gives up his religion or party?

Apostate. A heretic stays inside but disagrees.

Lover of music? Lover of women?

Melophile; Philogynist (opposite: Misogynist).

Talks too much vs speaks very little?

Garrulous (also loquacious) vs Reticent (also taciturn).

Shyness that comes from lack of self-confidence?

Diffidence.

A person avoided and rejected by society?

Pariah (also outcast).

Mercenary.

Official who investigates public complaints?

Ombudsman.

A woman with more than one husband at the same time?

Polyandry. A man with many wives = polygyny.

Immigrant or Emigrant: a Japanese man who comes to live in India is, for India, an …?

Immigrant (comes in). For Japan he is an emigrant.
